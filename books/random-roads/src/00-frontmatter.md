# Random Roads

A magazine about independent traveling — hitchhiking, sailing, hiking, cycling — compiled from [randomroads.org](https://randomroads.org/).

Developed by Robino, hosted by Guaka. License: **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0**. Additional terms may apply per author or piece.

Stories appear in the order fetched from the zine. Reviews of other people's copyrighted books are omitted.
