# Bringing Blogs Together

There is quite some writing about hitchhiking and other forms of independent traveling, especially on blogs. However, these are not easy to aggregate: some people have special travel-blogs, while others again are only writing about hitchhiking now and then.
There have been some attempts at bringing them together, most notably the [planet-function](http://hitchwiki.org/planet) at Hitchwiki. But as you can see there, we have a lot of unrelated content.
On Random Roads we wanted to have a good aggregation for a long time already. Not only to have a nice working portal for hitchhiking blogs, but also to be able to scout for good stories.
Therefore I am happy to announce a new page where we are bringing the blogs together, see http://randomroads.org/hitchhiking-blogs
So far the blogs are mostly about hitchhiking, but soon we also want to add more blogs of people who are into also other forms of independent traveling and nomadic lifestyle. And if you know one or two, feel free to [add them](/node/add/hitchhiking-blog).
