# Hitchhiking Magazine

Stick up your thumb, go from one place to another. Feel the road, hear the cars, find your drivers. In your hands a sign, in your bag your towel, food, clothes and... this magazine!
Showing your driver something tangible like a traveling magazine is a great way of showing where you come from, what you do and the culture you are part of. How will your driver respond when he finds out there is a hitchhiking community with even a magazine? And why not offer it to your driver for a donation or give it away for free? Support your hitchhiker, buy this magazine!
