# Be a Writer!

Write a story, make a photo-report, share an anecdote, upload a photo, send us a postcard or share your hitchhiking art-work with us. Click on 'share'. There you will find all the formats, including also hitchiking video-links you can share with everyone, you can suggest literature and share links to stories that you think might also be worthwile for Random Roads.
