# New Feature: Postcards

Since recently we have a new feature on Random Roads: Postcards. The idea is simple: pick a good photo that you took and tell us the story that goes with it. To add a postcard, [login](/user) and go to [Share Postcard](/node/add).
All postcards come together in a special page: http://randomroads.org/postcard
I also have been working the past week on making the website better readable. You can see there is more space for content and less clutter in the sidebar. One of the additions is also the the bar with photos on the top, as well as the bar with videos at the bottom.
I look forward to more of your comments and further suggestions to improve the website.
