# Join Random Roads

We want your content, your stories! We want hitchhiking stories, about the culture of free-fall traveling, nomadism. We want photo-stories, anecdotes, post-cards, your art-work, comics. Any format and subject can do as long as it is related to what you think should be Random Roads. Also think of country-reports, interviews with other fellow-travelers, people you met and who gave you a lot of kindness, hospitality exchange, lightfoot, and so on.
