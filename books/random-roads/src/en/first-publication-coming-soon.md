# First Publication Coming Soon

We're coming closer to our first print publication. Ever since we started almost 1,5 years ago, progress has been steady but we are now at the stage where we actually can really move further.
So far we have more than 20 articles that have been selected for the first issue. As soon as we have the selection finished, they will be send to our designer and then fingers crossed we will have our first edition in print ready soon after.
There is still work to be done though. We hope to have ready a couple of more articles before that time, such as an article on the [hitchgathering](http://hitchgathering.org) 789 and 6810, as well as an editorial, and more photo-reports.
Currently we are also enabling better photo-reports and a new section on postcards. If you haven't submitted your article yet, there is still some time left, but it won't take forever anymore.
