# Privacy

Privacy and Cookies
Geldloos.nl uses cookies for the analysis of statistics. This data is nowhere else used or shared with third parties, except anonymised summaries of visitor.
Furthermore Money Loos let allow ads through the Google Adsense network (Google Adwords). This also place cookies. You do not want ads, use a Adblocker. Do not you want third-party cookies, block them through your browser or use Ghostery.
more information
External suppliers (including Google) use cookies to serve based on previous visits ads.
With the DoubleClick cookie, Google and its partners also display ads based on your visits to other sites on the Internet.
You can unsubscribe advertise the use of the DoubleClick cookie for interest-based by visiting. Managing ad preference Look on http://www.google.com/ads/preferences
You can also opt for advertising cookies from external suppliers for interest-based by visiting. Http://www.aboutads.info/choices/
