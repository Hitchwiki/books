# Design

Visual identity for this book is a generic handbook of living with less — warm ochre paper, a crate of oranges, serif type. It is **not** sindinero.net branding.

sindinero.net is a **source** of chapters (see [README](README.md) and front matter), not the cover or chrome. The shared moneyless Drupal cyan stays unused here, as on the English [Moneyless](../moneyless/DESIGN.md) edition.

## Sites (content only)

| Site | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [sindinero.net](https://sindinero.net/) | Spanish chapters |

Sister editions: [Moneyless](../moneyless/) (English, moneyless.org) and [Geldloos](../geldloos/) (Dutch, geldloos.nl).

## Logo

None on the cover or catalog card. No cyan wordmark, no dollar-sign O, no black field cloned from the Drupal header.

## Colors

Inspired by citrus peel and dry earth — not sampled from the shared moneyless Drupal CSS (`#0ca6bb` cyan on `#181818`):

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `#f8f0e4` | Page |
| Ink | `#2c1a10` | Text |
| Muted | `#8a6240` | Secondary |
| Orange peel | `#c45c1a` | Links, cover bar |
| Earth | `#6b3a18` | Headings |
| Cover field | `#4a220c` | Photo scrim |

## Type

**Source Serif 4** throughout so it reads as a book, not the site’s grotesque.

## Applied

Crate of oranges, dark umber fade, cream title, peel-orange bar. Catalog kicker is “Vivir con menos, o sin nada.”
