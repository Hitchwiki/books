# Design

Visual identity for this book, taken from Nomadwiki (plus Casa Robino as a content source, not as the color system).

## Sites

| Site | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [nomadwiki.org](https://nomadwiki.org/) | Source wiki |
| [casarobino.org](https://casarobino.org/) | Extra chapters (license only; no Casa brand on the cover) |

## Logo

Hand-drawn **yellow globe** (Americas in white, black outline).

- Live: `https://nomadwiki.org/branding/nomadwiki/logo.png`
- Local: `assets/logos/shoestring-nomad.png`

## Colors

From `https://nomadwiki.org/branding/nomadwiki/monobook-refresh.css`:

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yellow | `#ffdc18` | Logo field, accent |
| Yellow soft | `#fff7ca` | Notices |
| Canvas | `#f6f3e9` | Page chrome / cover |
| Paper | `#fffdf8` | Reading background |
| Ink | `#25232b` | Text |
| “Blue” (named in CSS; actually ochre) | `#654d00` | Links |
| “Purple” (named in CSS; dark brown) | `#4a3a08` | Headings, visited |
| Line | `#d9d0bc` | Rules |
| Gold line | `#e7c62f` | Notice border |

## Type

Wiki is system-ui. This book uses **Source Serif 4** on titles and **Source Sans 3** in the body.

## Applied

Cream wash over a campsite, yellow globe and yellow bands, ochre links. No steel-blue “nomad grid” invented for this repo.
