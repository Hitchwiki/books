# hitchhiking in Colombia

Just sitting here at [Camiel](http://robino.dnsalias.org/casarobino/?q=user/8)'s place, eating some delicious aubergines (again!), and he is just telling me of his hitchhiking adventures in [Colombia](http://www.hitchwiki.org/en/Colombia). Hitching on the back of a truck, holding on the ladders, hoping he wouldn't fall off, and friendly people all over. As he didn't know [hitchwiki](http://www.hitchwiki.org) yet, he now edits the entry on Colombia which still says, more or less, do not try to hitchhike here :)
