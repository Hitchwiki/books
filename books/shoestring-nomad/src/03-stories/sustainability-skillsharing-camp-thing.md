Dear everybody,
in the name of the whole organisation team, I want to invite you all to the great upcoming event of Skill Sharing Camp in the Traumschule from 31st of March till 10th of April. You will find the official flyer in the attachment (please do send it to everybody who could be interested, as we wish to have an international camp).
What actually is the Traumschule?
The question should better be: What is a Dreamschool supposed to be? On an approximately very very big area in the Altmark, a Province in Saxony Anhalt, Germany, we want to establish an open and free learning & living project. You will find an antique military building, factory work rooms, some site caravans and some other adjoining buildings. Here, we want to found an autonomous place of free education, providing space to anybody willing to live, learn and teach freely.
Right now a small core group of more or less 6 people is working hard at creating a basic infrastructure, to make the place ready for our camp (connecting ovens, water and electricity, straighten, separate stuff we might need from rubbish...). For the latest information on how it's going here you can find us at www.traumschule.org We're planning to build up more and more infrastructure on the 2 weeks of Skill Sharing Camp to make this place what it's supposed to be: A Dreamschool! For this we're busy organising workshops for the following tasks:
Gardening: build greenhouses and hump beds, permaculture & self supply, excavate a pond, how to attract beneficial organisms, design a healing plant garden, build compost piles, plant hedges & trees...
Energy: build windmills, connect electricity, build solar showers, construction of a bale of straw house...
Handcraft: pottery, construction of compost toilets, build bikes from discarded metal, build a sweat lodge, sewing on sewing machines, fit in doors...
Health care: first aid (also first aid for demonstrations and actions), healing plant excursion, edible wild plants, do it yourself: natural cosmetics & medicine (for example making soap and elixirs), autogenous training, yoga...
Body arts: capoeira, juggling, climbing, skateboarding, wand dancing, contact dance, poi, yoga...
If you're interested in the topics above, come and join us on the Skill Sharing Camp. If you're not interested, come and join us anyways and organise workshops on the topics you choose. We have a load of stuff, materials and tools, so just in case you need something really special for your workshop contact us before, so we can see, if we have or are able to organise it somehow.
The supply of (bio – vegan) food, water and green electricity is based on donation, that's why we need small and big amounts of money. So we can be sure to enable financially limited people to take part as well. On top of that we can dumpster dive here, but neither vegan, nor bio food.
If you think you're able to help us with fundraising, as you have a connection to a good sponsor, know a foundation, which still has money to spend or have a rich granny: Your help is always welcome!
We're looking happily forward to welcome you all at the Skill Sharing Camp in March. What you should bring definitely: Sleeping bag & sleeping pad, as well as warm clothing and sturdy shoes (at the moment we can only heat up two caravans by wood ovens!).
Nazis and other strange people are naturally not welcome, the whole project is supposed to be ecological, vegan, anarchist, nonhierarchical and consuming on minimal level / self supplying.
Wish you all the best and see you soon,
Christina & the orga team
