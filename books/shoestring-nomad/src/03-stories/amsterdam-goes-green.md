# Amsterdam goes green

![](http://casarobino.org/sites/casarobino.org/files/principle_10_0.gif)Some of us went to see a presentation on permaculture yesterday, which was organised by the [SWOMP](http://swomp.wordpress.com/)-people. We had a good time hearing about the 12 [principles](http://www.permacultureprinciples.com/principles.php) of [permaculture](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permaculture) - and after a video sharing ideas and inspiration with some of the people around. I also got some new ideas for the balcony ([vertical gardening](http://swomp.wordpress.com/klimaatneutraal-leven/verticale-tuinen/)) and also learned more about some of the ideas that are currently buzzing, related to transition town networks and knowlegde/ skill-sharing.
Our ideas stand very close to these and there are many ways we can help enabling this revo-thing. At the same time, it is funny to [note](http://translate.google.com/translate?u=http%3A%2F%2Famsterdam.nl%2Fgemeente%2Fcollege%2Fmarijke_vos%2Fredactionele%2Fgroene_daken_in&hl=en&ie=UTF-8&sl=nl&tl=en) that even the Amsterdam city council wants to investigate how to make their rooftops green.
