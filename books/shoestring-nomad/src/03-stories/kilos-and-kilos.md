# Kilos and kilos

Anca and I went to the [Ten Kate market](http://trashwiki.org/en/Amsterdam) today. First we were taking stuff from the blue cans, but then we heard "Gratis! Gratis!" from another stand, with people flocking there and loading up on veggies and fruit. We joined them. It was a bit heavy, but we managed to take the kilos of food home. Meanwhile Erga called to say that she found a lot of stuff at the Dappermarkt! Now we're making a nice soup and Robin will prepare kouseband.
Later today we might head to [STEIM](http://www.steim.org/steim/) for some electronic weirdness.
