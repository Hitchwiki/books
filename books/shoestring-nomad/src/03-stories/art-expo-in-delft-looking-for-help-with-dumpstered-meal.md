# Art expo in Delft looking for help with dumpstered meal

Hiya,
We are putting on an Art expo June 20-21 at the Poppehuis squat in Delft and I want to put together a dumpster dived dinner saturday evening. I thought the folks that enjoy the casa's dumpstered dinner might be interested in helping. If so, let me know at moorepants@gmail.com. Here is the info about the event:
Spread the word!!
Invitation:
Als Het Maar Beweegt is an art exhibition Saturday June 20th and Sunday June 21st at the Poppehuis, Delft!
Everyone is invited!
The expo at a glance:
The exhibit takes place the weekend of June 20th and 21st. Both days, there is the possibility of a cup of coffee, tea or a tasty beer and snacks. Saturday we will serve dinner at 6pm.
On Saturdays at 7pm performances include:
Flopidisk (www.flopidisk.com)
Vasiliki and Cyrille
Kaspar Konig (www.kaspar.bareminimum.org)
The rest of the exhibition consists of moving installations including:
Thomas GOBBELS and Arjen Zuidgeest (www.landbouwbelang.org) (www.azxxl.nu)
Volkert van de Wijk (www.kineticart.nl)
Kim Laugs (www.feedbacksociety.com)
Tim van Akkerveken (www.flopidisk.com)
By Mark Doorduin, Dewi de Vree and Rachida Ziani
The residents of the Poppehuis will help you get to heaven! Using the latest green trend, not by plane but by bike.
All this is courtesy of ARM, Arts Pace Rondeel Maastricht.
Check out www.myspace.com/Poppehuis for the latest news!
Poppehuis, Delft
Westvest 32 by the Central Station Tram Stop
www.myspace.com/Poppehuis
Call for help:
We also need some help preparing for the expo and volunteers to help us run the show! Please email Bonne (bonne.knibbe@gmail.com) if you would like to give a helping hand. Just two or three hours of help will go a long way. Volunteers will get free dinner and some drinks.
