# d.i.y. clean

i was researching different ways of making soaps, since this is one of the things on the list of cool things i could make for house and give a workshop on. i've made soap several times before, usually coconut-based. this time, i was thinking more savon de marseille or [savon de aleppo](http://www.alepposoap.com/presentation.html).
instead, i found a [link for making you own laundry detergent](http://www.thesimpledollar.com/2007/03/15/how-to-make-your-own-laundry-detergent-and-save-big-money/), and recalled the days in france and maroc when i used the afore-mentioned soaps to wash my clothing; this recipe would have helped stretch them out a lot longer.
there's also [directions for simple natural soaps](http://www.motherearthnews.com/Natural-Health/2007-08-01/Recipes-to-Make-Your-Own-Soap-Lotion-and-More.aspx)
still looking for more recipes and directions...
