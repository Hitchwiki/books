# hitchhikers all along

While Kasper [made it](http://guaka.org/2008/10/28/three-days-ago-in-damascus/) to Jerusalem in no more than twelve days of hitchhiking, Kadry (a friend of Marc who is arriving tomorrow but sitting next to Kasper yesterday) hitched from Estonia to Amsterdam in 4 days, with only 5 rides. Around the same night of her arrival, Lena and Kardan were stuck in some random toilet in a German petrol station.
They got here today right before dinner-time, after 888 km of hitchhiking in 29 hours (check their [route](http://maps.google.com/maps?f=d&saddr=Braunschweig&daddr=A2+to:A2+to:A29+to:52.130116,4.534607+to:Amsterdam+(Noord-Holland)&hl=nl&geocode=%3BFdjAHQMdEqGmAA%3BFYbiFgMdpq59AA%3BFVSIKQMdPoh9AA%3B%3B&mra=dpe&mrcr=0&mrsp=4&sz=9&via=1,2,3,4&sll=52.264796,5.784302&sspn=1.022023,3.515625&ie=UTF8&ll=52.422523,9.272461&spn=4.074088,14.0625&z=7)) And when Lena and Kardan arrived, we received a txt message from Julien, saying: "Stranded. Desperately. We'll sleep in a truck and start again tomorrow at 6. GodDamnit!" Also at the same time, Amylin decided not to come yet to Amsterdam but instead hitch [Marocco](http://askamylin.blogspot.com/2008/10/what-are-you-doing-in-morocco-now.html).
