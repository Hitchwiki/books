# The daily exchange

There are so many new people to meet, i feel like i have the same conversaions almost everyday...
Random person (after a few minutes of mindless chatter): " so what course are you studying?
Me (smile inwardly but reply with a straight face): "Law."
Random person (looks very confused, notes my general appearence before finally clocking the lack of shoes): "Really? huh.....You don't look much like a law student...."
(awkward pause)
Random person: "can i just ask...where are your shoes?"
Hehehee.....
Loving it so far, The law is hard but i feel like i'm really learning, and have managed to find lots of charities to work with using my soon-to-be legal knowledge, Amnesty being the one i am most interested in. Changing the world, one legal document at a time:)
