# New Give Away Shop Goodies

Surprises, I love 'em! Just in front of the casa we now have a [give away shop](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Give-away_shop)! I was contacted by Olga, who lived with Heather whilst in London, telling me she is just opening a give-away-shop in the building in front of the casa.
The shop is really nice from the inside, there is lots of goodies and it is all official and legal and that kind of stuff. So from now on, we don't really have to go far to get rid of our stuff or to find new things. Having a baby en no money (see the article: [zwanger en geen geld](geldloos.nl/geen-geld-en-zwanger) in Dutch) is no problem: as for heaven is great and its toys magical :-)
