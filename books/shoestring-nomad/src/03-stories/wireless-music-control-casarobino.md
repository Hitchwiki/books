# wireless music control @ casarobino

today i installed [MPD](http://mpd.wikia.com/wiki/Music_Player_Daemon_Wiki) on laptopserver (which also got a fixed IP address now). on top of it, the web client [phpMp](http://mpd.wikia.com/wiki/Client:PhpMp) was installed, so anybody can control what music is being played at the casa thru your web browser. connect to the network CasaOpen and point your rig to [phpMp @ laptopserver](http://192.168.10.105/phpmp) to engage in a playlist war with your fellow hosts.
currently the music files are to be found in /media/music and you should click the [update link](http://192.168.10.105/phpmp/update.php?dir=&sort=Artist,Album,Track,Title) after you have added more files.
Marc knew about another solution which he installed afterwards. look at [Jinzora](http://192.168.10.105/jinzora) for a spiffier interface that even runs on Marc's mobile phone. not only can you play music via MPD, the files can also be streamed to your machine, so you would have your individual music channel.
have fun,
