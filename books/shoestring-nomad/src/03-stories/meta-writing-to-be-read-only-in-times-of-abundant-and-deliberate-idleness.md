# meta-writing. to be read only in times of abundant and deliberate idleness.

I would like to contribute, rather "create a story" for casarobino.org
Except...
whenever it's time to document insight, my thoughts scurry back into hiding, sort of like a family of foxes running back into their holes when threatened with a shining light.
Yea, foxes...deer?
not so much
(for deer sort of stand still in the light).
Yes, hard for me to contribute something worthwhile...
The blank, white screen is quite disruptive to my thoughts, in the way that
when the lights go on in a bar, the conversation stops.
Is this talker's version of writer's block?
Talk block?
Cock block?
Or what is happening to me may best be explained in fiscal terms.
This documentation, this exposure, this THREAT...
this tariff?
a thought tariff?
Yes!
The documenting is a tax that increases cost to the point that it costs the producer (writer) more than it is worth to the consumer (reader).
So, then producers are reluctant to sell.
and consumers are reluctant to buy.
So, what is the alternative?
The producer finds another market.
Back to the sink it is.
Thank god for dirty dishes.
