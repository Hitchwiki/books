# let's get away from here.

[![P8020622`1](http://farm3.static.flickr.com/2434/3787270144_474d92a61e.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/amylin/3787270144/ "P8020622`1 di amylin, su Flickr")
i want to record all of the crazy rants that have been streaming through my mind in the past few days, but i find myself unable to, somewhat incapacitated, and buzzing with one-liners devoid of actual descriptions or explanations;
everything just at the tip of the tongue and unable to be uttered.
where were we? where did we go?
this is to be filed under: "i don't know".
this is to be filed under: "remember me later."
this is to be filed under: "that was a long walk to absolutely nowhere, with everything in mind."
this is to be filed under: "we went on a trip together, one night, that we never saw coming and didn't know how to return from, but when we did come back, some things had changed, or our perceptions were never quite right to begin with, perhaps."
