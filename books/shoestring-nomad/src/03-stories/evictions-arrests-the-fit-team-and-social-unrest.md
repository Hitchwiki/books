# Evictions, Arrests, The FIT Team, and Social unrest

G20...
The looks on their faces as they slammed their batons, shields, and truncheons in to people was unlike any I have seen in a while. There was such a hatred for the people in front of them. How can a COP a protector of the people, have such contempt for those he/she serves? It puzzles me... After playing a few games of red-rover with police cordons and my black clothed and masked up accomplices, we got tired and decided to stay in the kettle within 10 minutes I found myself fighting with The Terminator, he whacked me a good one on the head, so cheers to him. We finally pushed through only to be chased more until we got away to climate camp, which by the way was shortly after faced with similar violence from the London Police.
The next day...
At the convergence space,after 2 days of no sleep and clashes with police, i finally got to sleep, only to wake up to a 100+ riot police battering the door down, we went peacefully, they went in the house on grounds that there were dangerous and wanted anarchist and violent protesters inside. Well I ended up being one of those they seemed interested in, after refusing to give my name or details, I was threatened with deportation, and they wanted to try and charge me because I had all of Mathieu's stuff in my bag, which appeared to be stolen. I finally gave half my name as they walked me over to the police van and then they proceeded to have me photographed by the British Forward Intelligence Team,full body with a profile from each angle, as I said aloud my name, date of birth, country of origin... and so on... Those zip-cuffs hurt!
As for Bristol
We have court tomorrow,with a .5 percent chance of success but, one can dream!
"The Church" was short-lived but, we managed to get a movie night going, paint the entire place, have a theatre from the area rehearse in our space, and feed some of the homeless in the area.
Remember everyone, direct action can be simple as saying no, or saying nothing at all! Good luck and take care!
