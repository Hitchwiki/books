# We have settled on earth and forgotten heaven [1]

Is this the first story actually written in a car?
Well, I must admit I didn't connect my mobile to the notebook to post it directly...;)
Anyway, as already mentioned last time, I attended a meeting of [Speak!](http://www.speak-netzwerk.de/), a loose christian network with political focus. As some of you may know, I do believe in a personal God but since I'm quite religion-sceptic I didn't have much people to share spirituality in the past time. Having religious but undogmatic people to share and discuss gave me a lot of new input.
Besides the deconstruction of religious tales designed to legitimate those in power, the people around are also busy with a lot of political action. Members of the network lived in the squat in Erfurt that was recently busted with 500 pigs with tanks and helicopters, another girl is using the network to visit a friend that was imprisoned for half a year during the G20 protests...
Sunday we worshiped in a village that is in the process of being destroyed by a corporation that has coal mining "rights" to the area. I hid an axe alongside my djembe in the bag but we decided not to break into the church through the window since that would have made it difficult for people to join us or leave. Good choice - we were spotted by "security" five minutes after our arrival. We stayed in the sun and towards the end, the woman formerly responsible for the church showed up and opened the door with a key she still had. Looks like the network could need more lockpickers:]
[1] Henry David Thoreau (Walden)
