# The Patron Saint of Bicycles

This week at the casa we could have used some help from [Madonna del Ghisallo - the Patron Saint of cycling](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madonna_del_Ghisallo). This time last week there were around 8 working bikes, this time yesterday we had two working bikes, two other half-usable ones and all the rest were unrideable in one way or another or lost in town.
If we had our own chapel with an eternal flame for cyclists no longer with us - our bicycle karma might improve.... Or perhaps we should curry favour with [St Christopher](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Christopher) as I'm sure the casa still has some karma credit with him :)
