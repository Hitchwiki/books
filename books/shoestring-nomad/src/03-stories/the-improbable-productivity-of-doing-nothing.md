But attention I'm not talking about doing nothing lurking on internet, playing video game or read a book that actually doing something ( and take the rest of my time left) no what I prefer is to be lying on a couch or sit and look at the people, and of course standing on the onramp of a highway in the middle of nowhere It make you think !
That the point, everybody should be able to do nothing and have the right to do it... nothing ! The only problem with that philosophy of life it's to start doing something after. And sometime express all you through or why you just don't want to go there and here but just stay can be difficult.

Maybe it's just the easiest way to meditate.
