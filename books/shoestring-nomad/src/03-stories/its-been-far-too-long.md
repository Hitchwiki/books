# its been far too long!

I've just realised that it has been a dangerously long time since i wrote anything on here so i thought i would just write a quick note to express my general feelings of joy at life:D
I'm in my second year of my law degree now and the work load is pretty intense but I have managed to hold on to my enthusiasm for the subject and still find it massively interesting (even contract law!). i began volunteering in the local refugee centre as a caseworker for the advice and advocacy team which is great, if a little distressing at times, some of the asylum seekers coming to this country have been through some horrific things and sometimes it can be hard to listen to their stories. However it gives me a great sense of satisfaction to know that i'm using my degree for good:)
I have set myself the task of exploring England a little more as I feel I really should know more about my home country, turns out its really quite beautiful and the people are much more open than i had anticipated. I guess hitch-hiking anywhere in the world will always bring out the best in people:)
I miss the casa so very much sometimes, especially kitten and all the people who made such an impact on my life, i hope to be able to come and visit soon! To deal with the loss of kitten i have bought two hamsters, king tubby and Bear hamster, they aren't any kind of replacement (obviously nothing could replace kitten) but they have been hugely entertaining and its strange how much i love those little critters!
Anyhoo, i know this is all a bit random but i just wanted to give you guys at least a little glimpse into my life back in blightey and to express my unending love for you all! I hope all is well with everyone in all corners of the world, i send you all huge hugs and lashings of good energy! Peace x
