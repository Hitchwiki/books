# Homenade to our cooks

[![](http://farm1.static.flickr.com/49/142649790_e3a40987a4_m.jpg)](http://flickr.com/photos/altemark/142649790/)

Cooking is language, cooking is identity, cooking is a primary need of all humankind. It is culinary skill, manual dexterity, and the ability to capture the right mix of flavor and spiciness. (...) a sign of humankind's serene relationship with nature and life. ([ref](http://www.transitionsabroad.com/publications/magazine/0501/index.shtml)). Those words I just read in the [book](http://casarobino.org/content/new-books-house) Manifestos on the Future of Food & Seed, while waiting for everyone to sit around the table to eat the simple but great dinner Amylin had just prepared.

Dinner is a basic thing here in the house, it helps building community, it creates a nice atmosphere, and most important, space for everyone to interact with each other. Almost daily we therefore eat jointly with whoever is there. We always eat well here - the last couple of months also mostly [freegan](http://trashwiki.org) - with at times brilliant cooks.
