# we want skillsurfers!

The traveling school of life is a network of travelers sharing their skills. It is as such a [real social](http://realsocial.org/content/realsocialorg) network. We just created an [entry](http://wiki.tsolife.org/Casa_Robino) on their [wiki](http://wiki.tsolife.org) for this house, where we list some of the ideas and projects we are working on, and what type of skill-surfers are needed here. But there is more.
At this very [moment](http://casarobino.org/content/creation#comment-38), a group of these [skillsurfers](http://www.skillsurfers.eu) are also touring through Europe to create networks of collectives, places where people could go to learn, to get input, share ideas and ultimately share *their* skills.
Things that immediately popped up in our heads was a very simple but effective one. Here we have been [thinking](http://casarobino.org/content/rooftop-gardening#comment-42) for a while now to create an urban wind-mill, for on the roof. If a skill-surfers come by to teach or learn how to build such a wind-mill, this could create a cascading effect of people going to all over the place to install new wind-turbines. The same thing applies to the rooftop- and balcony [gardening](http://casarobino.org/content/rooftop-gardening) that we are planning to do here.
