# Monday one year after

[This day](http://anujossain.blogspot.com/2008/09/day-1.html) one year ago, I stepped on a plane up north, to be welcomed by what was then simply known as "Robin's place". This day one year ago [trashwiki](http://trashwiki.org/en/Main_Page) was imagined out of late night talks in what was simply called the "living room". And one year minus ten days ago Casa got a name, and a [site](http://casarobino.org/2008/09/creation).
One year after, what have I learnt? Lots. One year after, where am I going? Not sure, yet.
Thank you everyone for the love, stories, company, dinners, dumpsters, magic and whateverness. See you around :)
