# Dumpster Diving goodies from the Dappermarkt

Today's dumpster haul from the Dappermarkt by Angie, Caroline, and Ethan garnered us about 30 pounds (15 kilos) of fruit and veggies, including a dozen clementines, many potatoes, 5 mangoes, 2 GIANT and many smaller carrots, grapes and strawberries, tomatoes, avocadoes, a basil plant, a bulb of fennel, a box of beautiful mushrooms, and peppers upon peppers upon peppers.
The best part was, when we were dumpstering in front of a corner veggie market, they handed us a big box of veggies they were about to throw out. Christmas spirit, the benefits of having 2 pretty girls along, or just general support of using instead of wasting, you be the judge.
Tomorrow we'll hit the Albert Cuijp as part of our goal to dumpster 3 markets in 3 days leading up to the celebration dinner on Wednesday. Updates to follow, hopefully with pictures tomorrow.
