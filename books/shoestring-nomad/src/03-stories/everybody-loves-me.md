# Everybody Loves Me

Miaaauw. What a silence here on the website. Well in the casa it's all loud. There is this baby here that just keeps on screaming from being so excited about life. And guess what?! All the attention goes to him. Prrrr. Well I guess it is natural. It is a little human you know and not a bird and one day I can also get cuddles from him... Prrrrrrr.
I am happy though that it is getting winter. Robino put seeds on the balcony so the birds come to eat and that is exciting me waaaaay more than anything else. I see so many of them, and I just can't stop talking to the birds - I am sure one day they will think I am one of them and then .... miaaaaaaaaaauw!
And I am kind of happy also that my friend-cat left the casa - remember we were two? She was getting more attention than me and she was eating all my food. Robino said it was "getting too much" for the casa - two cats and a baby. Prrr. I am happy I am still here and that I have my own litter box again. Don't get me wrong or anything but I soooo didn't like to share my box.
Miaaauw, I am off running through the house again. Up and down the furniture, from the zula through the junction and into the slagroom. Once a crazy kitten, always a crazy kitten. Prrrrrr, prrrrr, prrrr.
