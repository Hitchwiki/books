# Neighboorhoud dumpstering

One of the favorite spots for the hosts in our house is the market in Ten Katestraat. It is a daily market relatively close by to the house, and there are nice big blue containers with lots of left-overs. These are great for digging. Since we come there often, we also have a lot of knowledge on this market. A [special page](http://trashwiki.org/en/Ten_Katestraat) on [trashwiki](http://trashwiki.org/en/Main_Page) has recently been added to share this knowledge.
