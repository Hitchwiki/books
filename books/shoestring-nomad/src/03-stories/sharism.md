# Sharism

It was a whimsical long shot, so i proposed that Dennis and Sierra (dear friends on my eclectic pal Ahnika) who are coming to host at Casa that they write about their willingness to build windmills and roof top gardens. If fact they are not only willing, but experienced in this things which have topped the Casa wish list for a month.
And of course it set Robins mind running. "We have thought of this before", Robin repeats, but his voice belies something new, something exciting. We talk about cooperatives and guaranteed income. He is envisioning cashwiki cover alternative currencies . We agree on the need for a site to help travelers with short term paid work as one of the missing compliments to the digital nomads idea i posted on http://trashwiki.org/en/DigitalNomads
