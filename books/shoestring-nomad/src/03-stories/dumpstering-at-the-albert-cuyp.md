# Dumpstering at the Albert Cuyp

Saturdays are always a great day for Dumpstering in Amsterdam. And what is a cozier place for dumpstering than the most famous Amsterdam market, [Albert Cuyp](http://trashwiki.org/en/Albert_Cuyp)? Not only you will find lots of left-overs between 5 and 6 but you also get to meet quite a few of your fellow divers.
This Saturday I recognized 2 women with whom I had gone through some bins before. Now though there were quite some more people I hadn't met before, making us a total of 8.
I didn't get that much food this time but it was just enough for the weekend. Rosymay also joined - it was her first time - and when she spotted freshly thrown away flowers in a corner, she didn't stop for more :) It is always great to see people's responses when they find out they can get all that food and these flowers for free.
