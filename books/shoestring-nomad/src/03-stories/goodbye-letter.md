# goodbye letter

Dear Casa Robino,
I left in rush, as I came and as I spend the time there...
not the pace that belongs to this place, unfortunately I didn't manage to keep up with the relaxed rhythm of the house...
I also didn't manage to write a goodbye letter before I left, therefore am doing it now.
Things are still going fast over here, but am finding a moment out of it to say thank you!
When I first arrived I was overwhelmed with the input form everyone in the house and was worried i wouldn't manage to return all that I could receive from the hospitality of casa. I was doing my best, hope that I managed at least 50% of success...
I appreciate what is being carried out with SHE and it felt like an adventure as much as a familiar feeling throughout...
But most of all, the place was the unique people I came across.
What else can I say... Keep moving on, keep being these passionate Nomads you are and I am sure to come across you again- in flesh or in spirit.
Thank you
Ania Micińska
