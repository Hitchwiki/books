# Largescale Hitchhiking on San Francisco Bay. sort of.

I just heard about a largescale hitchhiking-ish movement in the Bay area, called "[Casual Carpool](http://maps.google.com/maps/ms?msa=0&cd=3&sll=37.970185,-122.124023&sspn=2.693273,7.064209&hl=en&ie=UTF8&z=9&msid=115522987064764290562.00046286a65db503a79eb)." Named something of which Americans won't be terrified (because hitchhiking is scary), Casual Carpool is a great way to miss the $7.50 (minimum) round-trip charge to get across the bay into San Francisco. Meet-up spots are all over the "East Bay" to help people get across the bay bridge to and from the meet-up spot in the "West Bay," (San Francisco), where I guess we use public transit (or in my case, my fold-up bike).
[Casual Carpool Map Link!](http://maps.google.com/maps/ms?msa=0&cd=3&sll=37.970185,-122.124023&sspn=2.693273,7.064209&hl=en&ie=UTF8&z=9&msid=115522987064764290562.00046286a65db503a79eb)
Also, some of my friends just voted to increase the tax on marijuana in Oakland, California. They can do that?
The Bay area is really pretty nice - you should come visit.
~George J.
