# Down the Danube with a paddle boat

Sailing over the Danube with a paddle-boat. Sounds crazy huh? Flore and Manuel, both based in Berlin, are currently sailing down the Danube river on a paddle boat. A couple of days ago they started in Vienna and they are writing about their adventures at http://pepora.wordpress.com/
The [Danube](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danube) is Europe's second largest river with 2850 km, going from Black Forest in Germany to the Black Sea in Romania. See also this [post](http://casarobino.org/node/16738) by Hugo earlier this year about the idea to sail down the Danube with a self-made raft.
