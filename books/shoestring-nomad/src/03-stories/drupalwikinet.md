# drupalwiki.net

Just started ''another'' wiki, [http://drupalwiki.net/en/Main\_Page Drupalwiki]. I often find myself wading through tons of pages to find an answer to a Drupal question, and when I found it I don't know where I should record the info or how I can even update the thing.
MediaWiki is still vastly superior to Drupal when it comes to wikis - especially when open for anonymous editing.
