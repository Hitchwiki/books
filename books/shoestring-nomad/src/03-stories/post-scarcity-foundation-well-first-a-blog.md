# Post Scarcity Foundation - well first a blog

About three weeks ago I couldn't sleep well and I had this idea. It's time to set up [a foundation to get us beyond scarcity](http://sharewiki.org/en/Post_Scarcity_Foundation). Naturally I started that wiki page on Sharewiki.
"Post scarcity or post-scarcity describes a hypothetical form of economy or society, often explored in science fiction, in which things such as goods, services and information are free, or practically free.” [[Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post_scarcity)].
I think quite a few of you who read this are close to this. We just have to find ways to [spread this mindset](http://blog.sharewiki.org/2010/07/post-scarcity-for-everyone/ ).
So I invite you to [share your own thoughts on how to make this happen on the blog](http://blog.sharewiki.org/wp-login.php?action=register) and [on the wikipage](http://sharewiki.org/w/index.php?title=Post_Scarcity_Foundation&action=edit).
