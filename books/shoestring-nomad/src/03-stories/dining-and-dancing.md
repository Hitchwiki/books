# Dining and Dancing

As every week, also this Thursday we have an open dinner. We normally are going [Dumpster Diving](http://trashwiki.org/en/Amsterdam) before that time. If you want to get along, please be here around 4, or let us know if you are going to bring food from a market around Amsterdam.
On Saturday, we'll go to [Club 8](http://maps.google.com/maps?q=poolcentrum+club+8+amsterdam) to dance (theme is love this time), where we'll sneak in before 23.00. We also have a pre-party over at Anu's new place. Get in touch with any of us to know where we'll be.
And if you want even more, on Sunday there is a nice [event](http://www.couchsurfing.com/meetings.html?mid=26656) going on in the Buurtboerderij.
