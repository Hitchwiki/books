# English is not enough

Holiday Tuesday midday, smells like Sunday and it´s snowy outside. The phone rings... Unknown number.
I pick up, and the dutch woman is already speaking a lot.
DW (Speaking a lot of dutch)
me Yes, hello
DW (more dutch... "Adan Music"... More dutch)
me Sprekt u Engels?
DW No, I do not speak English. You don´t speak dutch? We are in The Netherlands
me Ok, I am learning
DW Is there anyone who speaks dutch?
me No, not at the moment
DW Ok, then I know enough, goodbye
