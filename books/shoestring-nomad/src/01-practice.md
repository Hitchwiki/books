# Part I — Practice

Cheap travel is a pile of ordinary skills: sleep, food, moving, staying well, not spending money you do not have. The pages that follow are those skills without a destination attached.

Hitchhiking, dumpster diving, and hospitality exchange have their own books in this catalog. Where Nomadwiki repeats them, this edition keeps a short pointer. What stays here is the rest of the kit — walking, camping, volunteering, water, winter.

Place-specific notes come after this. If you only read one section before you leave, read this one.
