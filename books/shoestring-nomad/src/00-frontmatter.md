# Shoestring Nomad

Compiled from [Nomadwiki](https://nomadwiki.org/), the collaborative guide for nomads — free camping, tramp lifestyle, cheap travel, not tourism. Extra nomadbase stories from [Casa Robino](https://casarobino.org/) (CC-BY-SA 3.0).

License of this book: **CC-BY-SA-4.0**.

This edition starts with practice (how to travel light anywhere), then a geographic half (each country, with city pages nested where they exist), then voices from Casa Robino, and ends by sending you out and back to the wiki. Hitchhiking, dumpster diving, and hospitality-exchange how-tos belong in the other books in this catalog. Where Nomadwiki repeats those topics, this edition keeps a short pointer instead of a second copy.

Nomadwiki city pages that exist (Amsterdam, Berlin, London, Paris, New York, Istanbul, Tbilisi, Beijing, and others) sit under their country. Hostels and camps change; use the live wiki, not this book, for pins.
