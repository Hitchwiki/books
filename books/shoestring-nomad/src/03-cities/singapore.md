# Singapore

> Hostels, camps, and neighbourhood tips change. Use the live [Nomadwiki](https://nomadwiki.org/) city page for what is current. This chapter is a snapshot of general orientation, not a pin list.

**Singapore** is a tiny country in [Southeast Asia](Southeast_Asia "Southeast Asia"){.wikilink}. It is super modern compared surrounding [Malaysia](Malaysia "Malaysia"){.wikilink} and [Indonesia](Indonesia "Indonesia"){.wikilink} and can thus feel very exhausting for a backpacker traveling in the region, but on the other hand offers good opportunities for making quickly some [money](money "money"){.wikilink}.
