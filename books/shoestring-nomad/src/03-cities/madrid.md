## Laboratorios ciudadanos {#laboratorios_ciudadanos}

Citizen laboratories are created in vacant city spaces. Twenty have opened in the last few years, including [La Tabacalera](http://latabacalera.net/c-s-a-la-tabacalera-de-lavapies/), [Esta es une plaza](http://estaesunaplaza.blogspot.fr/) and [Campo de la Cebada](https://es-la.facebook.com/campodecebada/). Each specialises in a particular field, such as agriculture and urban economy, social and cultural integration, collaborative art or digital economy. An introduction to the scene can be found [here](https://theconversation.com/how-madrids-residents-are-using-open-source-urban-planning-to-create-shared-spaces-and-build-democracy-79717).
