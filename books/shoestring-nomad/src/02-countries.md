# Part II — Places

Visas, seasons, and what "cheap" means change at borders. These country chapters are orientation, not a promise that last year's camp still exists.

City chapters sit under the country they belong to, capital and main cities first, where Nomadwiki actually has them. Hostels, squats, and neighbourhood tips go stale. Use the live [Nomadwiki](https://nomadwiki.org/) page when you are actually going.

The order is geographic, not A–Z: Europe first, then North America, the rest of the Americas, Asia, Africa, Oceania.
