# Part III — Your turn

You do not need a round-the-world ticket, a laptop visa, or a year off. You need a way to sleep, a way to eat, and a direction. The practice pages are that kit. The country and city pages are so the first border is not a surprise. The Casa Robino stories are proof that houses like that existed, and that people walked in.

Leave for a week. A tent or a couch, a stove or a dumpster, walking or a thumb. See whether the days get larger. If they do, go further. If they do not, you still went.

This is not the coworking-passport version of nomadism. It is the older one: low spend, high trust, the wiki in your pocket, the next town instead of the next booking. You already know enough to start. The rest you learn by being somewhere.
