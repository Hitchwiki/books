# Scotland

**Scotland** is a highly nomad-friendly country, with the ease of hitchhiking and legality of wild camping. The weather isn\'t really as bad as its reputation. As with everywhere in the [United Kingdom](United_Kingdom "United Kingdom"){.wikilink}, supermarkets like Tesco, Morrison\'s and Sainsbury\'s provide extremely cheap food.
