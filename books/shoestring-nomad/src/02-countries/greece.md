# Greece

**Greece** is a country in [Southern Europe](Southern_Europe "Southern Europe"){.wikilink}, bordering [Albania](Albania "Albania"){.wikilink}, [FYRoM](Republic_of_Macedonia "FYRoM"){.wikilink}, [Bulgaria](Bulgaria "Bulgaria"){.wikilink}, and [Turkey](Turkey "Turkey"){.wikilink}. It is a member state of the [European Union](European_Union "European Union"){.wikilink} as well as the [Schengen Agreement](Schengen_Agreement "Schengen Agreement"){.wikilink}.

There are some [Free hot springs](Free_hot_springs "Free hot springs"){.wikilink} in Greece.
