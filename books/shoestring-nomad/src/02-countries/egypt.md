# Egypt

Egypt is a country situated in the north-east corner of [Africa](Africa "Africa"){.wikilink}. It is bordered by the [Gaza Strip](Gaza_Strip "Gaza Strip"){.wikilink}, [Israel](Israel "Israel"){.wikilink}, [Lybia](Lybia "Lybia"){.wikilink}, and [Sudan](Sudan "Sudan"){.wikilink}. The capital of Egypt is [Cairo](Cairo "Cairo"){.wikilink}, one of the biggest city in Africa.

This country is part of the Arab world, and the population is mostly Muslim.

The currency is the Egyptian pound (EGP). As for August 2015 1 EGP = 0.11 EUR = 0.12 USD
