# Uruguay

**The Oriental Republic of Uruguay** (*República Oriental del Uruguay*) is a country in [South America](South_America "South America"){.wikilink} and it is one of the most *awesome countries ever*.

More than half of Uruguay\'s population is concentrated in the capital of [Montevideo](Montevideo "Montevideo"){.wikilink}, meaning a very low population density in the country\'s interior. In the Uruguayan countryside you will doubtlessly encounter some of the most laid back, friendly folks you could imagine. Humble, generous and curious about anyone passing through, it is the people rather than the geography that make travel memorable in this overlooked corner of the continent. The tranquility of the countryside and the openness of the people will leave an impression on any traveler. Uruguay is a relatively small country, so traveling from the top Northern city to [Montevideo](Montevideo "Montevideo"){.wikilink} would not take much time. From [Rivera](Rivera "Rivera"){.wikilink} (a border town with [Brazil](Brazil "Brazil"){.wikilink}) to Montevideo it is about 520 km.

## Why is Uruguay awesome {#why_is_uruguay_awesome}

- President of Uruguay, [José Mujica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mujica), has been described as \"the world\'s \'humblest\' president\", due to his austere lifestyle and his donation of around 90 percent of his \$12,000 (£7,500) monthly salary to charities that benefit poor people and small entrepreneurs. He might also [give a lift for a hitchhiker](http://www.abs-cbnnews.com/lifestyle/classified-odd/01/16/15/uruguay-president-gives-hitchhiker-ride)
