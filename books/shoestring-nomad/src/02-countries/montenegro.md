# Montenegro

Montenegro, officially the Republic of Montenegro, is a small and beautiful country in the [Balkans](Balkans "Balkans"){.wikilink}, located on the mediteraneen coast between [Croatia](Croatia "Croatia"){.wikilink} and [Albania](Albania "Albania"){.wikilink}. Montenegro was part of Yugoslavia and declared independence from Serbia on 3 June of 2006. Montenegro use euro, but is not part of [European Union](European_Union "European Union"){.wikilink}.
