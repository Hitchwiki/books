# Burkina Faso

**Burkina Faso** formerly **Upper Volta**, is a landlocked country in [West Africa](West_Africa "West Africa"){.wikilink}. It is surrounded by six countries: [Mali](Mali "Mali"){.wikilink} to the north, [Niger](Niger "Niger"){.wikilink} to the east, [Benin](Benin "Benin"){.wikilink} to the south east, [Togo](Togo "Togo"){.wikilink} and [Ghana](Ghana "Ghana"){.wikilink} to the south, and [Côte d\'Ivoire](Côte_d'Ivoire "Côte d'Ivoire"){.wikilink} to the south west. Many experienced travellers consider Burkina Faso to be one of the most friendly and inviting countries in Africa. However Burkina Faso is also a very poor country.
