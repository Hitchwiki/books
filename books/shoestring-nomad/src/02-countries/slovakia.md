# Slovakia

**Slovakia** is a very beautiful country located south of [Poland](Poland "Poland"){.wikilink}, neighboring [Ukraine](Ukraine "Ukraine"){.wikilink}, [Hungary](Hungary "Hungary"){.wikilink}, [Czech](Czech "Czech"){.wikilink} and [Austria](Austria "Austria"){.wikilink}. Its people are generally friendly, though as always can differ from each personal experience.
