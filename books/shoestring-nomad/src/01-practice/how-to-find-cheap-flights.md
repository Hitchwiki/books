# How to find cheap flights

![](Ryan.b737.750pix.jpg "Ryan.b737.750pix.jpg") **Finding cheap flights** is a nice skill. [Hitchhiking](Hitchhiking "Hitchhiking"){.wikilink} is a good way to get around but how can you get to other continents if you don\'t have oceans of time?

## Search engines {#search_engines}

- [Skyscanner](http://skyscanner.net/) is a great way for finding cheap flights. Choose \"whole month\" or even \"whole year\" for this to work properly.
- [AZair.com](http://www.azair.com/) is a specialized low-cost search engine. It can combine diff. carriers (up to 3 changes), search by region (from: Scandinavia, to: Mediterranean), search in long time windows (June-October), has data form 45+ LCCs (mostly European and Middle-eastern), works fast
- [Azuon](http://azuon.com/) is Windows program that has many low-cost airlines in its database such as [Ryanair](Ryanair "Ryanair"){.wikilink}, [Norwegian](Norwegian "Norwegian"){.wikilink}, [Wizzair](Wizzair "Wizzair"){.wikilink}, [AirAsia](AirAsia "AirAsia"){.wikilink} and many more.
- [ITA\'s Matrix](http://matrix.itasoftware.com/) lets you search in advanced ways.
- [Drungli](http://www.drungli.com) is very simple search tool for flights in EU, but it covers some cheaper airlines (eg. [Ryanair](Ryanair "Ryanair"){.wikilink}).
- [Kayak](http://www.kayak.co.uk/flights/)
- [Hopper](http://www.hopper.com/tools) Flight research tools.
- [airixo](http://airixo.com/en/) nice presentation, allows for search to \'anywhere\'. Limited to Europe.
- [skypicker](https://en.skypicker.com/) maps options, allows for search to \'anywhere\'.
- [JetMap](http://www.jetmap.co/) shows routes for low cost airlines but doesn\'t have a search. Especially good for European connections; outside Europe it\'s not always up to date. [See airliners list](https://www.facebook.com/nizkocenovci.si/posts/10152793488418740?comment_id=10152819538348740&offset=0&total_comments=2)
- [SkipLagged](https://skiplagged.com/) finds \"Hidden City\" connections, sometimes it\'s cheaper to book a flight to a third city, and you simply don\'t board the last plane.
- ~~[Jetcost](http://jetcost.com/)~~ Compare different search engine - they also list companies that scam their customers though

## Avoid scams {#avoid_scams}

- **[Edreams](http://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g1-i10702-k5806571-EDreams_fraud-Air_Travel.html)**, **GO Voyages**, **Opodo** and **Travellink** belong to one company, Odigeo, that [keeps on scamming people](http://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g1-i10702-k6603455-EDreams_GO_Voyages_Opodo_and_Travellink-Air_Travel.html)
