# Car

For nomads traveling by their own motor vehicle, unique issues may arise. If these are not properly taken care of, a journey by car might become very expensive. Remember, your vehicle may also be your [Accomodation](http://nomadwiki.org/en/A_Vehicle). On this page we share practical tips.

## Fuel

Gas prices vary between European countries. Here is an [overview from December 2014](http://www.drive-alive.co.uk/fuel_prices_europe.html)

## Parking

Parking in inner cities can be very expensive. The good news is that most cities have excellent P+R infrastructure. General information about car parking in Europe is on [car-parking.eu](http://www.car-parking.eu/) If you are looking for free parking in Germany, there is [Gratisparken.de](http://gratisparken.de)

## Insurance

Insurance fees vary hugely between different European countries. Consider registering a car in another country (but be aware that in most cases, you need to re-register your vehicle when it\'s longer than three months outside of the country of registration)
