# Sailing

![Anker spill](Ankerspill.jpg "Anker spill") **Sailing** can be a good way to get around. If you\'re lucky you don\'t have to [pay](money "pay"){.wikilink} to go long distances.

## Finding boats {#finding_boats}

[findacrew.net](http://www.findacrew.net/) is a good website for people looking for jobs/voluntary work on sailboats.

On [7knots.com](http://www.7knots.com/) some people offer free places on their boats in exchange for work, some will ask you to share common living costs.

## Hitchhiking boats {#hitchhiking_boats}

More information about hitchhiking boats:
