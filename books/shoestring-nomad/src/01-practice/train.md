# Train

![](Train_by_the_Durango_and_Silverton_Narrow_Gauge_Railroad.jpg "Train_by_the_Durango_and_Silverton_Narrow_Gauge_Railroad.jpg"){width="300"} **Trains** are a form of transport in (almost) all countries in the [world](world "world"){.wikilink}. As one can expect, rates, speeds and quality standards will differ greatly from country to country! There is also the concept of *Freighthopping*, *freight train hopping*, or simply as *train hopping*.

## [Europe](Europe "Europe"){.wikilink}

A cheap way to get around by train in Europe during a certain period of time might be via [Interrail](http://www.interrail.eu).
