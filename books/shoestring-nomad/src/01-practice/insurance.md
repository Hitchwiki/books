# Insurance

**Insurance** is a tricky topic for nomads.

## Health insurance {#health_insurance}

Within the [EU](EU "EU"){.wikilink} things are fairly well organized, but not too much either. If you are a resident somewhere you\'re supposed to pay for health insurance in that country and it will also cover you in other EU countries.
