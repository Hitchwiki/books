# Walking

**Walking** is the oldest and probably the most zen way of getting around. You need time, but it\'s quite rewarding. With some training you can walk about 25 to 30 km a day.

![Paths to Santiago de Compostela](Stjacquescompostelle1.png "Paths to Santiago de Compostela") There are many relatively convenient [trails](trails "trails"){.wikilink} out there, on which you will not be bothered much by [cars](car "car"){.wikilink}. For example on the [Camino de Santiago](Camino_de_Santiago "Camino de Santiago"){.wikilink}. Often you can also find relatively cheap [accommodation](accommodation "accommodation"){.wikilink} in strategic locations so you don\'t have to carry a [tent](tent "tent"){.wikilink} and other [camping gear](camping_gear "camping gear"){.wikilink} with you.

It can be nice to combine walking with [hitchhiking](hitchhiking "hitchhiking"){.wikilink}.
