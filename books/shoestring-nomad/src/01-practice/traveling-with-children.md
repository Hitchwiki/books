# Traveling with children

## Useful Links {#useful_links}

While being a nomad is sometimes traveling alone, it often may mean traveling with others, for short or long periods. Sometimes, it may include your child. Having a child (or children) should not disqualify you.

- [The Vagabond Family](http://www.vagabondfamily.org/blog/road-schooling/roadschooling-highschool/) that answers some basic questions.
- [Education and socialization](http://edventureproject.com/education-socialization-why-didnt-we-think-of-that/).
- [Long term Travel As Education](http://www.bootsnall.com/articles/12-06/long-term-travel-as-education.html). It was published on Boots-N-All.
- [Road Schooling 101](http://wandrlymagazine.com/article/winter-travel/roadschooling-101/). Published in Wandr'ly Magazine with lots of resources and age leveled recommendations.
- [How to Take Your Kids Out Of School](http://www.bootsnall.com/articles/12-08/educational-travel-how-to-take-your-kids-out-of-school.html) and travel with the teacher's blessing.
- [Five Great Resources for World Schooling](http://www.wanderingeducators.com/best/traveling/5-great-resources-world-schooled-kids.html) on Wandering Educators.
- [Traveling with Children in Central America](http://www.irishtimes.com/life-and-style/travel/long-haul/central-america-with-all-the-family-1.1772631) on the Irish Times Newspaper.
- [Petit Bibi on the road](http://en.bibiontheroad.net/) - Sitarane\'s ordeal hitchhiking around the world with Weirdo and Cranky
