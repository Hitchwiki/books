# miracle ticket

george clinton and parliament funkidelic came to town, i need to see these guys, legends, but it was 33euro to get in which my poor ass couldnt handel. so of corse busking with the guitar and my sign saying 'I NEED A MIRACLE TICKET' was the way forward.
the first 2 hrs of playing infront of the venue paradiso brought nothing, just a few strange looks from people who didnt get the sign and didnt want to ask. i went to have some food and when i returned there were more people and the show was to start in an hour. the band walked by but i guess they didnt see my sign since they didnt give me a ticket,im sure they would have. got 50cents from some nice lady who apologized for not having a ticket for me, and another young guy came over to smoke a joint with me. we talked a while about squats and animal rights until his friends he was waiting for arrived. his friend jack asked me what a miracle ticket was (someone giving you an extra ticket for a show you MUST see but cant afford) then scratched his head wondering if he should be my saviour. his friends were all saying 'ya come on do it do it' and he said 'ya sure why not'.
the kid didnt even have an extra ticket, no, instead he went to an atm and handed me the cash! they said jokingly that i met the nicest people in holland. jack didnt have much money either, he was just a young student with a good heart, always smiling.
inside we pushed our way to the front where george and p funk funked our asses. i danced the funky chicken with the guitar as a dance partner 4 hous strainght, my shirt soaking wet and feet stompin, my new friends passing me a number of joints, the music came through the ground and up through my bones into my head and shot up through to space. a beautiful black blues singer came on stage and her soul poored through her voice. i was in awe with tears streaming down my face it was so powerful. that may have happended to me at a concert before but never with so much feeling, i cant describe it.
anyway...it was funkadelic
