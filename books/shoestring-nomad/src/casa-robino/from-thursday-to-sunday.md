# From Thursday to Sunday

It's almost spring, so time for some events! This Sunday and for the upcoming months: every 2nd and 4rd Sunday of the months casa will be open all afternoon for [tea](http://casarobino.org/2011/01/free-tea-big-surprise-party), bread-making & soup. Come early enough in the afternoon to [learn how to make bread](http://casarobino.org/2009/05/pastamadre-casa-preparing-bread) (based on sourdough), or at any other time to enjoy tea, a sunny balcony and great conversations.
Bring along your sense of humor, organic wheat flour or soja-flour, (ingredients for) bread-spreads and or veggies to cook soup with. Tea, bread-making skills, good company and space provided by the house. Bring along friends and people you think we should meet.
You want to come, but don't have the address? [Contact us](/contact)!
