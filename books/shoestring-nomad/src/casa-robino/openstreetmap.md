# OpenStreetMap

[img\_assist|nid=3972|title=|desc=|link=node|align=left|width=200]Manu and me visited Amsterdam to go to the '[State of the Map](http://www.stateofthemap.org)'-conference, about the [OpenStreetMap](http://www.openstreetmap.org). If you've never heard about OpenStreetMap, I will tell you a little about it: Basically the OpenStreetMap is to maps, what [Wikipedia](http://www.wikipedia.org) is to encyclopedias; a community-based project to collect geographic information like streets, parks, pubs - [everything](http://bestofosm.org) which might be interesting. The level of detail can be very high, e.g. the zoos in [Berlin](http://www.openstreetmap.org/?lat=52.50759&lon=13.33868&zoom=17) and [Amsterdam](http://www.openstreetmap.org/?lat=52.36603&lon=4.91652&zoom=17) even show the areas of the various animals. Even more interesting is what people make of the data - specialized maps for [cycling](http://www.opencyclemap.org), [skiing](http://www.openpistemap.org), [public transportation](http://öpnvkarte.de/); [routing](http://maps.cloudmade.com) for cars, cycling and walking; applications for mobile devices like the [iPhone](http://www.offmaps.com/); a [browser for the data](http://www.openstreetbrowser.org) (my project); or you can make a cake out of it.
The conference lasted three days - Friday, the "Business day" and Saturday/Sunday the "community days". Lot's of talks, space for networking - and many male nerds :/
The best thing of the weekend (beside staying in CasaRobino) was the [squat sauna Fenomeen](http://www.saunafenomeen.nl/) - it was just great to relax there.
