# Academy of Free Travel

The Academy of Free Travel (AFT) sounds vaguely Casa-esque, but more temporary and exclusively for hitchhikers. Apparently they have set up hitchhiker houses (in the past) in 3 Russian cities, Kyrgistan, Tajikistan, and Egypt. I hear the next one is to be in Tbilisi, Georgia. An English AFT page is [here](http://www.avp.travel.ru/AFT-2002.htm), with links to other pages in Russian, and a CouchSurfing thread is [here](http://www.couchsurfing.org/group_read.html?gid=12&post=8812191), including a couple people who have stayed at AFT houses.
