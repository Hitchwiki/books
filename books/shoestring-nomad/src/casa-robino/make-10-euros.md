my friend's co-worker Tessa is looking for participants in a language learning experiment. It takes about an hour and you get 10 euros for your trouble. See below for more details & email her if you are interested.
Dear Lab-meeters,
Following up on the online pilot study that I did a while ago, I am now starting a new experiment for which I would like to invite participants. With this study we investigate the evolution and learnability of structure in speech sounds by conducting artificial language learning experiments in which sounds are produced by drawing shapes on a computer screen.
Participants of all ages with normal hearing (and who did not yet participate in the pilot) are welcome. Language doesn’t matter.
It will take place in the UvA Bungehuis, Spuistraat 210, 3rd floor (in the studio). The experiment will not take more than an hour and participants will be compensated for their time with a payment of 10 euros.
It would be a great help if you could participate or if you could let me know if you know someone who would be interested to participate! You can contact me at t.verhoef@uva.nl and please feel free to forward this message to as many people as you like.
Thank you very much in advance!
Best wishes,
Tessa Verhoef
