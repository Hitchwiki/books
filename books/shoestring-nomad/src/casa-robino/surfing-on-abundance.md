# Surfing on Abundance

Two months ago already a journalist approached me in response to the dumpster dive tour we organised in Den Haag [in March](http://casarobino.org/2011/04/dumpster-diving-tour-den-haag). He was working on an article on freeganism and was looking for some people who could help him out.
It is all in Dutch but features both me as well as Wrenaqua. It is published in the June edition ([pdf](http://www.mugweb.nl/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/2011-06.pdf)) of [MUG Magazine](http://www.mugweb.nl/), a monthly Amsterdam based magazine for people who live with little or no money.
For more articles published about dumpster diving in the Netherlands, check my profile page at [Trashwiki](http://trashwiki.org/en/User:Robino).
