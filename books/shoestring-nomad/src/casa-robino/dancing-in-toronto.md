# dancing in toronto

hello casa!!!!! how i miss thee! back to school in kansas city! however winter break is coming my way and i'm looking forward to it. i haven't forgotten my magnificent stay at the casa and will be back to visit as soon as i get the chance to boogie over there.
through youtube, someone in canada discovered my dance videos and invited me to dance at a concert he was putting on through a grant with the toronto arts council! i jumped on the opportunity and went, it was a lovely time, met great friendly people with inspiring ambitions! just thought i would share the video from the performance and add to the excitement and potentials of web based networking!
