# You have to log in again

In order to avoid downtime of this website (and others) I just ALTERed some MySQL TABLEs to use InnoDB. I also cleaned up some other TABLEs, such as the session table. So you will have to create a new session - or in normalpeoplespeak: log in again to this website.
[added by Julien]: you don't have to create an account again. Just log in with your usual username and password.
