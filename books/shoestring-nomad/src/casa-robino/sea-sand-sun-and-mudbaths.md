# Sea, Sand, Sun and mudbaths

Reports are coming in about the [hitchgathering](http://hitchgathering.org/) that took place last weekend, and apparantly it was a great gathering, with almost 150 people attending this year's event.
As Julien writes, all in all, it went very smoothly, the logistical nightmare of keeping that bunch fed with the nearest shop out of walking reach was defeated thanks to the wonderful initiative of the few volunteers that had arrived in advance and prepared the camp ; and by the joint initiative of the participants that spontaneously organised supplying forays in littles groups, funded by public donations.
Read more [here](http://hitchgathering.org/sand-everywhere)
