# Can you host casa-people in A'dam?

You like the Casa and its people? Did you visit us and do you live in the area? Then maybe you have offered us to host casa-people when we get somewhat overloaded? We heard you but we never created a list of you friendly people!
So now we ask you that if you have the ability to host someone, to let us know.
You can write [an e-mail to us here](/contact) and please give us some relevant information (for example your hosp-ex profile). When we would have some overflow, we will contact you. And when someone does contact you, this of course doesn't mean you have to say yes. It just means that we have a friendly person who is looking for a temporary place to crash for some days or maybe even longer.
Any comments, or suggestions? Let us know!
