# Not Plan A

So there was a plan A. Inspired by they hostel workers in Greece who get on the train several stops before Athens and hand out propaganda for their hostels to tourists coming in on the train. What if we hijacked this distribution idea for the power of good.
Say we got into the trains coming into Am\*dam at perhaps Aachen or Groningen (sp?) then we hand out Underground Guide fingerbooks to the tourists on the train who were interested. These guides would talk about the squat cafe's and best dumpster locations and and free stores and most righteous coffeeshops and graffiti galleries and couch surfing portals other under identified progressive/radical places to be in the city.
Plus we would identify our own email/cell contact so people who wanted to do more with underground visiting could. We would meet dozens of new cool people and play with some of them. The research into the city would be wiki format, so people could add and comment with time.
And so grows the Digital Nomad movement [[http://butthedevil.blogspot.com/2008/11/digital-nomads.html]]
