# Just a little text I wrote today

In an oasis of sounds one can sometimes let the voices speak.
These are the times when you’re in control,
they don’t come out uninvited,
not to frighten you,
not to mock you.
You evoke them.
You are prepared.
The hard part is that I can’t let go of reality.
I see it,
even when the other filters take over and make everything blurry,
but other things clear.
When the faces pop out and laugh or scream, smile or fleer,
the presence of the existence of reality is there,
there and draws me back despite my longings for free falls.
I see myself from the outside,
I see the simple context in which the others have built their nests.
But that’s not where I am,
not where I breathe,
not where I live.
But I know, and I see.
The hard part is to let go.
And then I laugh again.
