# For the World of Tomorrow

We can get 5000 euro for a project if it wins the contest "[For the World of Tomorrow](http://voordewereldvanmorgen.nl)". All we have to do is submit an idea and to have people voting for it. Ideas that can be forwarded here are any idea that helps bringing about a more sustainable world.
The [ideas so far](http://voordewereldvanmorgen.nl/site/page/acties_ideen) include a movie-project, C02-reduction ideas, and so on. None of these ideas is really groundbreaking though and so I think we have a fairly good chance of winning some cash here for one of the projects we are working on - or maybe even several if we submit more than one (there are three prices). I am not sure though which project, would it be urban-farming, or trashwiki, or something else? Deadline is end of this month.
The idea of urban farming is [simple](http://casarobino.org/content/five-minutes-bambi). We will set up a DIY (Do It yourself) guide in Dutch for how to turn your garden into a little farm (open source). And secondly, set up a network of urban farmers who go and create a garden-farm for a fixed amount, and a monthly fee for maintenance if the people don't want to maintain it by themselves. To submit the idea, we could also just submit the idea for the DIY-guide.
Another question, would this project be best fitted in [trashwiki](http://trashwiki.org/en/Main_Page) alternatives section or in the section of Free Social Solutions in [sharewiki](http://sharewiki.org/en/Main_Page)?
Your thoughts please.
