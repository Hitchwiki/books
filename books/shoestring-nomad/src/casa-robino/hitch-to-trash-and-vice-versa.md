# Hitch to Trash and vice versa

Sometimes ideas are just to obvious to not happen. [Trashwiki](http://trashwiki.org/en/Main_Page) was created in the week that [Hitchwiki](http://hitchwiki.org/en/Main_Page) reached 1000 articles (in its English language version). This week Trashwiki reached 100 articles and today we set up convenient links from Trashwiki to Hitchwiki and vice versa.
Check e.g. [the article about first time dumpster diving](http://trashwiki.org/en/First_time), which contains a nice link in the left sidebar that points to [first time hitchhiking](http://hitchwiki.org/en/First_time). And the other way around of course.
