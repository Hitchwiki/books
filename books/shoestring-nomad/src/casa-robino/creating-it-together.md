# creating it together

"Whoever is here is creating it together", writes [Kasia](http://casarobino.org/users/karmakas) on her [blog](http://karma-kas.blogspot.com/) Mama Kas on the Road. She stayed together with [Sky](http://casarobino.org/users/skybluestar) for some days in the house, which was a great experience I think for all of us.
She also writes how she got 40 euro while [busking](http://karma-kas.blogspot.com/2008/10/busking-and-pitfalls-of-dutch-rap.html), how she tried fixing the bikes with Sky, the dumpster diving results (3 giant bags with avocados, tomatoes, radishes, eggplants, carrots, star fruit, melon, pineapple, plums, and papaya) and how she [observed](http://karma-kas.blogspot.com/2008/10/home.html) the communal trash-dinner we recently had: "It felt totally comfortable and easy to take over the kitchen, rummage for spices, cook up a bunch of food, make tea... making me realize that my longing to root is not a desire to own my own house necessarily - the need is totally satisfied in being able to cook for people and host in this fluid way."
