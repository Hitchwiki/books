# Design

Visual identity for this book, taken from Trashwiki. Not hazard-stripe yellow.

## Sites

| Site | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [trashwiki.org](https://trashwiki.org/) | Source wiki |

## Logo

Black stencil **Trasher**: figure leaning into a wheeled dumpster, on olive-beige.

- Live: `https://trashwiki.org/branding/trashwiki/logo.png`
- Local: `assets/logos/dumpster-diving.png`

## Colors

From `https://trashwiki.org/branding/trashwiki/monobook-refresh.css` (`:root`, sampled from `mediawiki/branding/trashwiki/logo.jpg`):

| --- | --- | --- |

## Type

Trashwiki is system-ui on MonoBook. This book uses **Oswald** on the cover (short, loud titles) and **Source Sans 3** in the body.

## Applied

Olive canvas over a dumpster-diving haul, black Trasher mark, forest-green links. Kicker is the contributor estimate (“By 230+ dumpster divers”), not “From Trashwiki.” No diagonal hazard stripes.
