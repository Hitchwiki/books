# Los Angeles

> Named dumpsters and map pins go stale. For current spots use [dumpstermap.org](https://dumpstermap.org/) and the live Trashwiki page. This chapter is culture, legality, and general practice — not a dumpster list.

In **Los Angeles**, [California](California "California"){.wikilink}, [USA](USA "USA"){.wikilink}, there are plenty of [dumpsters](dumpster "dumpster"){.wikilink} great for [diving](dumpster_diving "diving"){.wikilink}. There are often useful items put out at the curb.

### Divers\' meetings {#divers_meetings}

[The Los Angeles Dumpster Diving Meetup](http://dumpsterdiving.meetup.com/205/) hosts events where people in Los Angeles interested in dumpster diving and [freeganism](freeganism "freeganism"){.wikilink} can meet.

## Food Not Bombs {#food_not_bombs}

Various chapters of [Food Not Bombs](Food_Not_Bombs "Food Not Bombs"){.wikilink} give out food in Downtown Los Angeles, Santa Monica/Venice and Long Beach.
