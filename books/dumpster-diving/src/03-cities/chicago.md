# Chicago

> Named dumpsters and map pins go stale. For current spots use [dumpstermap.org](https://dumpstermap.org/) and the live Trashwiki page. This chapter is culture, legality, and general practice — not a dumpster list.

**Chicago** is in [Illinois](Illinois "Illinois"){.wikilink}, [US](US "US"){.wikilink}. The city offers many possibilities for [dumpster divers](dumpster_diver "dumpster diver"){.wikilink} and [freegans](freegan "freegan"){.wikilink}.

### Locations

See [this editable map of good places](http://maps.google.com/maps/ms?msa=0&msid=207086338829578593196.0004865be240c8af49148&ct=docsearch&cd=17&cad=docsearch,cid:16930773761989617594) for dumpster diving.

- *Whole Foods* Lincoln Park (1550 North Kingsbury Street) - this is the third largest *Whole Foods* in the country and has a ton of [dumpsters](dumpster "dumpster"){.wikilink} out back. There\'s bakery stuff around every day, and it\'s usually still in its individual wrappers inside trash bags, so pretty clean. Also, old food, at least the bread, seems to go in a separate place from plain old trash, which is always nice. They close at 10, so the best time to get there is after 9 pm. `<small>`{=html}*(Last checked: May 2010)*`</small>`{=html}
- *Produce* in Hyde Park ( 1226 East 53rd Street ) one of two shops where Hyde Park goes buying fruits and vegetables. Its a very expensive store and so they sort out very good and a lot of stuff. Though the shop is pretty small, they throw away 4 bags full with food every day. Sometimes next to vegetables and fruits, u can find cheese, creams, hummus etc not even expired yet. Easy access, quite light and clean. They close at 8pm, its good to go there in the night though, so thats the stores around are closed aswell. Its a complete sidestreet with dumpsters, if u can still carry something its worth it to check the other dumpster as well. `<small>`{=html}*(Last checked: February 2011)*`</small>`{=html}
- *Fruitful Yield* in Elmwood Park on North and Harlem tosses stuff on Saturdays. Best to go there about 1am or later so all the other businesses in the strip are closed (workers from the pizza place like to hang out in the back smoking). It\'s a health food store so you will find many organic, gluten free, vegan foods, some produce, snack foods, vitamins, flours and such. `<small>`{=html} (Last checked: October 2015)`</small>`{=html}
