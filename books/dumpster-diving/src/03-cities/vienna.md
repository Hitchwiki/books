## Free food {#free_food}

### VoKüs (Volksküche - people\'s kitchen) {#voküs_volksküche___peoples_kitchen}

[Freegan](Freegan "Freegan"){.wikilink} food is prepared and served for free. Similar to [Food not bombs](Food_not_bombs "Food not bombs"){.wikilink}.

### Tüwi

Student house of the BOKU (University of horticulture) is home to a Vokü on the 1st Sunday of the month starting at around 3 PM. They also have a [freebox](freebox "freebox"){.wikilink} with all kind of things, mostly clothes. <http://tuewi.action.at/>

### uoqbon

A culture/info/art/community space offers a Vokü together with a local [food coop](food_coop "food coop"){.wikilink} on the third Sunday of the month with food ready by around 8 PM. Another coop offers a Vokü on every other Sunday from 8 PM in the same place. <http://uoqbon.obda.net/>

## Freeshops

Via <http://www.umsonstladen.at> you can find [freeshops](freeshop "freeshop"){.wikilink} in Vienna with their addresses and opening hours. For English, see [Google translated version of the page](http://translate.google.com/translate?js=n&prev=_t&hl=en&ie=UTF-8&layout=2&eotf=1&sl=auto&tl=en&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.umsonstladen.at%2F).

Tüwi - don\'t forget about the student house of the BOKU which has a [freebox](freebox "freebox"){.wikilink} with all kinds of things, mostly [clothes](clothes "clothes"){.wikilink}. (It is also a home to a Vokü on the 1st Sunday of the month from around 3 pm.)

## Free price {#free_price}

This is not free food!

### Deewan

You can find a Pakistani restaurant (very good, self-service\...) with a pay-what-you-want-model, i.e. you decide how much money you give! It is called *Der Wiener Deewan* and is situated on Liechtensteinstraße 10 ([map)](http://maps.google.at/maps?f=q&hl=de&geocode=&q=+1090+Wien,+Liechtensteinstra%C3%9Fe+10&sll=&sspn=&ie=UTF8&z=16&iwloc=addr). Their website is [here](http://ref.guaka.org/?r=http://deewan.at).

### Gagarin

There is also the *Cafe Gagarin* that has local specialities with a free price policy model, additionally it is a center for anarchist/bike people. It is at [Garnisongasse 24, 1090 Wien](https://goo.gl/maps/SgbavgvYc2E2) and their website (in german) is [cafegagarin.at](http://cafegagarin.at/).
