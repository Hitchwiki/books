# Dumpster Diving

Compiled from [Trashwiki](https://trashwiki.org/), the collaborative world-wide guide to creating value from trash.

More than **230** named dumpster divers have edited the pages collected here, plus anonymous edits. That is an estimate from Trashwiki’s contributor lists for this edition’s chapters, not a headcount of every account on the wiki.

The chapter “WikiHow” originated with wikiHow contributors under CC-BY-NC-SA-3.0 and came to this edition through Trashwiki. Full source links are collected in the attribution section at the end.

This edition starts with practice (how to dive anywhere), then a geographic half (each country, with city chapters nested where Trashwiki actually has them), and ends with resources and an invitation to go look. It does **not** include dumpster-map coordinates or named bins. Those go stale; use [dumpstermap.org](https://dumpstermap.org/) and the live wiki.

Dumpster diving sits in a legal grey area in many countries. Trashwiki does not encourage breaking the law; it is a space where people share experience. This book reprints that already-public guide.
