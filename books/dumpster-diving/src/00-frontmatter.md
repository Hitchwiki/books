# Dumpster Diving

Compiled from [Trashwiki](https://trashwiki.org/), the collaborative world-wide guide to creating value from trash.

License: **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0**. Non-commercial ShareAlike.

This edition is practice, country overviews, and city chapters where Trashwiki actually has them (Amsterdam, Berlin, Paris, Copenhagen, Rotterdam, Mexico City, Tokyo, and others). It does **not** include dumpster-map coordinates or named bins. Those go stale; use [dumpstermap.org](https://dumpstermap.org/) and the live wiki.

Dumpster diving sits in a legal grey area in many countries. Trashwiki does not encourage breaking the law; it is a space where people share experience. This book reprints that already-public guide.
