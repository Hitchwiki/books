# Mold

[Dumpstered](Dumpster_diving "Dumpstered"){.wikilink} [food](food "food"){.wikilink} can contain **mold** - be careful and picky with food that shows traces of it.

With hard foods such as hard [cheeses](cheese "cheese"){.wikilink}, [salami](meat "salami"){.wikilink} and ham it\'s okay to cut away the mold. [Fruits](Fruits "Fruits"){.wikilink} and [vegetables](vegetables "vegetables"){.wikilink} can still be okay after cutting away about an inch.

Apart from these: chuck it!
