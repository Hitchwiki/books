# Precycling

**Precycling** is a form of avoiding [recycling](recycling "recycling"){.wikilink} or reducing the amount or size of [waste](waste "waste"){.wikilink}. Precycling involves a conscious effort to dispose of waste responsibly throughout its usable lifespan.

Some ideas:

- Crush cans before bagging
- Repurpose or repair broken and damaged goods
- Sort resources
