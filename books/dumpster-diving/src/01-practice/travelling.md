# Travelling

It\'s a popular myth that **travelling** *has* to be expensive. Fortunately [hitchhiking](http://hitchwiki.org) and [hospitality exchange](http://bewelcome.org) have made it possible to make travelling cheaper than renting a place in many places. [Dumpster diving](Dumpster_diving "Dumpster diving"){.wikilink} adds another way for frugal travelling. See for example [Doing The World For Free](Doing_The_World_For_Free "Doing The World For Free"){.wikilink} - a mission with 3 young people who do what many of us dream to do, without spending a single cent!
