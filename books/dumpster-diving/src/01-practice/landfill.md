# Landfill

A **landfill**, also known as a dump (and historically as a midden), is a site for the disposal of waste materials by burial and is the oldest form of waste treatment. Historically, landfills have been the most common methods of organized [waste](waste "waste"){.wikilink} disposal and remain so in many places around the world.
