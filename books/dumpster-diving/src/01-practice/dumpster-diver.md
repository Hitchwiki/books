# Dumpster diver

![It is nearly always good to have a flashlight.](Dumpster_diving_in_canada.jpg‎ "It is nearly always good to have a flashlight."){width="300"} A **dumpster diver** is someone who takes items that have been discarded ([trash](trash "trash"){.wikilink}), in order to make better use of them, principally by turning them into something usable or edible via [dumpster diving](dumpster_diving "dumpster diving"){.wikilink}.

## Why it is cool to be a dumpster diver {#why_it_is_cool_to_be_a_dumpster_diver}

Because dumpster diving is:

- free (as in freedom).
- free (as in no money).
- free (as in beer).
- ecological, a form of waste reduction.
- fun and can be a nice social activity.

## So, I am a diver\... What do I need to have? {#so_i_am_a_diver..._what_do_i_need_to_have}

Sometimes, often or even always you will need:

- a small [flashlight](flashlight "flashlight"){.wikilink} (headlamp is the best)
- something to pull the stuff to you, some kind of pole with a hook at the end (a hoe maybe?)
- a stepping stool (can help you reach over the top)
- bags (backpacks, trash bags, plastic bags, etc.)
- wet wipes and/or water bottle to clean up with
- a basic First Aid Kit in case you hurt yourself.

### How to dress {#how_to_dress}

- mind the weather (wind rain snow), union-style is good, as the activity might make you sweat
- you don\'t want to catch too much attention
- you don\'t want to look like a burglar
- make sure to wear shoes with thick sturdy soles: dumpsters can be surrounded by broken glass.

## Is there something you have to watch out for? {#is_there_something_you_have_to_watch_out_for}

Yes, there are some potential hazards like:

- never climb into a [dumpster](dumpster "dumpster"){.wikilink} with Medical and Hazardous Waste, anyone can throw out a needle that could jab you; wear protective clothing
- lids can suddenly slam shut when windy
- sharp objects
- icky stuff (dead animals\...)
- [legal implications](law "legal implications"){.wikilink}

## Most common places to look for useful trash\... Where are those? {#most_common_places_to_look_for_useful_trash..._where_are_those}

- dumpsters behind stores that sell merchandise you are interested in, like bakeries, florists, toy stores, bookstores, etc.
- apartment buildings where people move often and leave stuff behind

## Diver\'s Etiquette {#divers_etiquette}

- Don\'t leave a mess. Leave the dumpster in no worse condition (or perhaps even better?) than you found it.
- Avoid making noise - it can wake neighbors up (or attract store employees) and make them call the police.
- Avoid ripping trash bags - try to untie them if possible, and then tie them back.
- Take only what you can use, and leave the rest for someone else.
- Even though some dumpster divers chose to [break into locked dumpsters by force](Unlocking_dumpsters "break into locked dumpsters by force"){.wikilink}, it is recommended to look for those dumpsters which are not locked.
- If there are other dumpster divers around (often happens at some [food markets](food_markets "food markets"){.wikilink})
  - Don\'t take more than you are possibly going to use
  - If you already have too much of something, offer it to other people
  - Leave a trace if you found a whole pile of something hidden away so that others can find the stuff you\'re not going to take

Basically, try to appreciate the fact that you get [stuff](stuff "stuff"){.wikilink} or [food](food "food"){.wikilink} for free, and don\'t make life harder for other dumpster divers, especially when you know you won\'t come to this spot again. You are not alone diving in this world!
