# Festivals

**Festivals** such as *Reading* allow great [dumpster diving](dumpster_diving "dumpster diving"){.wikilink} opportunities. You can also see many great bands for free if you volunteer some hours. See <http://casarobino.org/2009/09/music-festivals-free-or-festival-dumpsterdiving> for inspiration!

During festivals there are often dumpsters in front of the entrance to the stages were everyone is supposed to throw away their alcohol. Just wait next to it and some poeple will offer you their half emptied bottles.

In some countries (e.g. [Germany](Germany "Germany"){.wikilink}) you [get money for empty bottles of beer, water and lemonade](Making_money "get money for empty bottles of beer, water and lemonade"){.wikilink}. It\'s easy to collect enough to finance your festival ticket.

Some people leave everything behind when they drive home. Look out for huge piles of abandoned things and you will find tents, sleeping bags, all kind of food, alcohol and lots of weird things.

[This video](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8DXNSL_1tmY) shows the aftermath of the 2009 Glastonbury Festival. Hundreds of tents, folding chairs, inflatable mattrasses and many other goodies abandoned by punters at festival\'s end.
