# Vegan

![](V_for_vegan_relief_frosted_glass_reedited.png "V_for_vegan_relief_frosted_glass_reedited.png"){width="144"} **Vegans** eat plant-based food (+ mushrooms).

Among [freegans](freegan "freegan"){.wikilink} and [dumpster divers](dumpster_diver "dumpster diver"){.wikilink} many are vegans. The word *freegan* actually comes from combining *free* and *vegan*.

**[Defintion of Veganism](https://www.vegansociety.com/go-vegan/definition-veganism):**

: Veganism is a way of living which seeks to exclude, as far as is possible and practicable, all forms of exploitation of, and cruelty to, animals for food, clothing or any other purpose.

Whilst the act of salvaging animal products is ethically vegan, it makes sense to distinguish the act of eating not-dietary vegan food as doing something freegan.
