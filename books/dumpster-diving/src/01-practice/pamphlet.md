# Pamphlet

One way of improving the public relations between the [dumpster diving community](Dumpster_diver "dumpster diving community"){.wikilink} and the general populace is by setting a good example for your fellow hobbyists. One way of doing this is by authoring a pamphlet for dumpster divers to give to store owners or staff who [lock](lock "lock"){.wikilink} their [containers](container "container"){.wikilink} or don\'t allow their [trash](trash "trash"){.wikilink} to be taken by divers. Such a pamphlet would give store owners/employees basic information on reasons why it is good to leave trash bins unlocked, and would also help to maintain a clean image for the community.

#### What is dumpster diving? {#what_is_dumpster_diving}

\'Dumpster diving\' is the act of recovering previously unwanted goods from a dumpster in order to give them another use.

#### Why should shops allow this? {#why_should_shops_allow_this}

The essence of why shops should allow dumpster divers to work without difficulty is summed up in the well-known adage: \"One man\'s trash is another man\'s treasure.\" In a world where the demands of humanity are ever-increasing, and the waste generated by our growing population so too escalates, extra measures must be taken to alleviate the effect this issue is having on the world, both at the governmental and local level. It would be folly to discard resources that could be reused, and as the contenders of the planet we have a responsibility to make do with what we have. Is it that immoral to reuse what other people don\'t want? This is why allowing dumpster diving to continue should be allowed.

We understand that the idea of picking from the trash is seen as unattractive, unsanitary and just plain disgusting, and for very good reasons. For a long time, places where waste was disposed of were often hotbeds for pathogens such as deadly bacteria to thrive. However oftentimes dumpster divers work under the cover of night with proper protective equipment, out of courtesy for the shop owners.
