# Dumpster

![](Dumpster_in_Groningen.JPG "Dumpster_in_Groningen.JPG"){width="250"}

**Dumpster** is something that contains [trash](trash "trash"){.wikilink}, whatever the discarded [stuff](stuff "stuff"){.wikilink} (incl. [food](food "food"){.wikilink}) is. In the [United Kingdom](United_Kingdom "United Kingdom"){.wikilink} and [Iceland](Iceland "Iceland"){.wikilink} a dumpster is called a **skip**, in the [Netherlands](Netherlands "Netherlands"){.wikilink}, [Germany](Germany "Germany"){.wikilink} and [Sweden](Sweden "Sweden"){.wikilink} it is simply a **container**.

The action of getting things out of the dumpster is called [dumpster diving](dumpster_diving "dumpster diving"){.wikilink}.
