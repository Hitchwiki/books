# Part II — Places

What is thrown away, and what happens if you take it, changes at borders. These country chapters are culture and legality in outline, not a promise about tonight's supermarket.

City chapters sit under the country they belong to, capital and main cities first, where Trashwiki actually has them. Named dumpsters and map pins stay on [dumpstermap.org](https://dumpstermap.org/). Use the live map when you are actually going.

The order is geographic, not A–Z: Europe first, then Asia, Africa, the Americas, Oceania.
