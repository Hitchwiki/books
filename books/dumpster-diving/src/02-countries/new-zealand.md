# New Zealand

**New Zealand** doesn\'t seem particularly great for finding [trash](waste "trash"){.wikilink}, but there are some good things set up. For example, in [Nelson](Nelson "Nelson"){.wikilink} you can find a trash store, next to the [recycling](recycling "recycling"){.wikilink} point, where you can buy a lot for much cheaper than the usual [second hand store](second_hand_store "second hand store"){.wikilink}. [Christchurch](Christchurch "Christchurch"){.wikilink} has *The Free Shop* (everything\'s for free) and also *SuperShed* which sells recycled items at incredibly cheap prices.

## Dumpster diving {#dumpster_diving}

In New Zealand dumpsters are usually referred to as [skips](dumpster "skips"){.wikilink}.

Officially NZ police treat dumpster diving as theft, however, enforcement and attitude of individual police officers may vary. Nevertheless, be discreet and remember about [diver\'s etiquette](dumpster_diver#Diver.27s_Etiquette "diver's etiquette"){.wikilink}.

As of May 2011, things seem to be getting better, as somebody arrested for dumpster diving had the charges dropped `<small>`{=html}[(see link)](http://www.stuff.co.nz/oddstuff/5079640/Police-drop-minced-up-charges-over-thrown-out-pies)`</small>`{=html}. In December 2013 six men caught dumpster diving in Blenheim were released by the police without being charged.[1](http://www.stuff.co.nz/the-press/news/9542093/Dumpster-divers-never-skip-a-feed)
