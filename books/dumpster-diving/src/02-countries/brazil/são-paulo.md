# São Paulo

> Named dumpsters and map pins go stale. For current spots use [dumpstermap.org](https://dumpstermap.org/) and the live Trashwiki page. This chapter is culture, legality, and general practice — not a dumpster list.

In São Paulo they have street markets that have name *feiras*. During the working hours or in the end you can go and take as much fruits and vegetables as you want. Usually they are laying on the roads but also you can ask the vendors to take some that they don\'t need. The best time to go there is around 14:00-15:00 hours. And, of course, here is a map where you can find them: <http://www1.folha.uol.com.br/infograficos/2014/07/85141-colheita-urbana.shtml>
