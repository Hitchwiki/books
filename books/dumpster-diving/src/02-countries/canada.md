# Canada

- - *For more detailed info for different places in Canada, see template \"Provinces and territories of Canada\" below.*

**Canada** is a pretty decent country for [dumpster diving](dumpster_diving "dumpster diving"){.wikilink} and other [freegan](freegan "freegan"){.wikilink} opportunities.

Most major cities, definitely [Montreal](Montreal "Montreal"){.wikilink}, [Toronto](Toronto "Toronto"){.wikilink}, [Vancouver](Vancouver "Vancouver"){.wikilink} and [Calgary](Calgary "Calgary"){.wikilink} have a [Food Not Bombs](Food_Not_Bombs "Food Not Bombs"){.wikilink} serving at least once a week.

## Dumpster diving {#dumpster_diving}

In Canada, there is an increasing trend towards gigantic [supermarket](supermarket "supermarket"){.wikilink} chain stores with [compressor](compressor "compressor"){.wikilink} dumpsters, but that shouldn\'t deter you from heading out to find those places there are still classic [dumpsters](dumpsters "dumpsters"){.wikilink} for all your easy diving needs. The long [winters](winter "winter"){.wikilink} actually make dumpster diving easier because [food](food "food"){.wikilink} doesn\'t go bad easily.

### Legal status {#legal_status}

In several provinces of Canada, The Trespass to Property Act or the Trespass to Premises Act grant property owners and security guards the power to ban anyone from their premises, for any reason, permanently. This is done by either posting clear signs, enclosing the area with a fence or issuing a verbal or written notice to the intruder, who will only be breaking the law on return. A recent case involved a police officer who retrieved a discarded weapon from trash as evidence; the Judge ruled it as legal without a warrant, so some have speculated this is enough backing for anyone to raid garbage.
