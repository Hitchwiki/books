# Morocco

**Morocco** is a country in North [Africa](Africa "Africa"){.wikilink}. Morocco is not very rich country but even there you can find in big cities good food. People are used to place their old bread in bags on street. Sometimes you can find in plastic bags also couscous with vegetable or things like that. It is also possible to find some fruits and veggies at souks (markets). But still you will probably recycle just few of things and on countryside and smaller towns you will not find almost anything. Some Moroccans are very hospitable so they will probably give you some food or invite you for a dinner time to time.
