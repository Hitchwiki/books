## Dumpster diving {#dumpster_diving}

It is not easy to [dumpster dive](dumpster_dive "dumpster dive"){.wikilink} in Lithuania but it is possible to gather some amounts of [food](food "food"){.wikilink} for free. [Food markets](Food_markets "Food markets"){.wikilink} in major cities are the most common places for searching after food: after they are closed (but not yet cleaned of the [trash](trash "trash"){.wikilink}) one can find plenty of discarded [vegetables](vegetables "vegetables"){.wikilink} on the ground. Checking [supermarket](supermarket "supermarket"){.wikilink} [dumpsters](dumpsters "dumpsters"){.wikilink} is also worthwhile although one should not except too much: to the disadvantage of Lithuanian [freegans](freegans "freegans"){.wikilink}, many food stores do not dispose food right after its shelf life is over: many unpack these items (if possible) so that the expiration date is not visible anymore, and sell them at a discount. Such actions are not yet strictly controlled there, and it is generally known that food stores won\'t throw away even a black-dotted [banana](banana "banana"){.wikilink} so easily, although food stores like \"[Rimi](Rimi "Rimi"){.wikilink}\" and \"[IKI](IKI "IKI"){.wikilink}\" usually do not comply with this pattern.

The legal status of dumpster diving in Lithuania is most likely non-existant and/or not known.

## Wild Foraging {#wild_foraging}

[Wild foraging](Wild_foraging "Wild foraging"){.wikilink} is a popular activity in Lithuania. Some people manage to get up to 70-80% of all their food only by eating wild fruits, beries, herbs, mushrooms, etc.

It\'s good to know some basic information about Lithuanian flora before trying to find food in the nature. Luckily, just a few types of plants are poisonous and they aren\'t very common. It gets more complicated with mushrooms, because many of them are not edible and some are even deadly poisonous. The best way to learn these things is to go foraging with someone who already knows all the information. Special sites, like [Plants For A Future](http://www.pfaf.org), can also be very useful (see also [\"Maistas randamas gamtoje\"](http://gyvunuteises.lt/forum/viewtopic.php?t=658) in Lithuanian).

## Clothing/stuff swap {#clothingstuff_swap}

It is pretty easy to get new old [clothes](clothes "clothes"){.wikilink} and some other [stuff](stuff "stuff"){.wikilink} for free in Lithuania: one just has to know where to search. For that just use one of these websites:

- [Kaman.lt](http://www.kaman.lt) - share, give away or get anything for free
- [Mainyk.lt](http://www.mainyk.lt) - a social sharing service for exchanging any goods
- [Manodrabuziai.lt](http://www.manodrabuziai.lt) - clothing swap; there are also options like selling clothes or giving them away for free.

## Food Not Bombs {#food_not_bombs}

Every last Saturday of the month [Food not bombs](Food_not_bombs "Food not bombs"){.wikilink} event takes place at \"Reformatų\" square in [Vilnius](Vilnius "Vilnius"){.wikilink}. Part of the food distributed during the event comes from [dumpsters](dumpsters "dumpsters"){.wikilink}.

## Freecycle
