# Czech Republic

**Czech Republic** is a country in Central [Europe](Europe "Europe"){.wikilink}. It can offer some opportunities for [freegans](freegan "freegan"){.wikilink}, at least for those who seek. For sure you can find places for recycling in big cities but supermarkets have usually dumpsters locked so it\'s much harder to find an open ones than in western europe.
