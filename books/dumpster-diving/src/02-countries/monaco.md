# Monaco

**Monaco** is a very tiny and extremely wealthy country surrounded by [France](France "France"){.wikilink}. Because of its wealth it could be really good for [dumpster diving](dumpster_diving "dumpster diving"){.wikilink}, though it\'s hard to know for sure until someone can actually confirm this.

Some people say there it is forbidden by law to dumpster dive there (to be confirmed).

You can find plenty of good sandwiches, bread, pastries, deserts at the bakery \"La Boule de Neige\" at 20 Rue Princesse Caroline, Monaco. (last checked: March 2018)

There is a farmer\'s market at the \"place de la condamine\" every morning so there should be lots of waste every day after its closure.
