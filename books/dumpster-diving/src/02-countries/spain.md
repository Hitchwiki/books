# Spain

![Heading to the city garden for gathering food.](Runingarden.png "Heading to the city garden for gathering food."){width="250"} **Spain** has a general reputation of being a wonderful country for [dumpster diving](dumpster_diving "dumpster diving"){.wikilink}. This is somewhat surprising since, due to hot weather, messy containers and prevalence of small businesses it is not actually much better (likely worse) than many other places in [Europe](Europe "Europe"){.wikilink} and [North America](North_America "North America"){.wikilink}.

For a lot of people living in Spain dumpster diving is a usual part of their daily life, so don\'t be surprised to meet a lot of dumpster divers when the night falls down in a city before the trash picking service come to collect the [garbage](garbage "garbage"){.wikilink}.

There are many [food markets](food_market "food market"){.wikilink} which will provide you with great [food](food "food"){.wikilink} at closing hours. You can also try asking in smaller grocery shops - owners/employees often are happy to give you the remains of the day. This only works though if you have a basic command of Spanish, because the people working there tend to speak virtually no English.

It is difficult to find good food diving in containers of chain [supermarkets](supermarket "supermarket"){.wikilink} in Spain. You can try these for skipping your food: [Caprabo](Caprabo "Caprabo"){.wikilink}, [Carrefour](Carrefour "Carrefour"){.wikilink}, [Dia](Dia "Dia"){.wikilink}, [Lidl](Lidl "Lidl"){.wikilink}, [Mercadona](Mercadona "Mercadona"){.wikilink}, [Veritas](Veritas "Veritas"){.wikilink} (organic food). In Spain are some filials of the British [Iceland (store)](Iceland_(store) "Iceland (store)"){.wikilink} Supermarket, it is mostly easy and you find a lot in the bins of these stores.

Spanish people tend to just leave on the street the [stuff](stuff "stuff"){.wikilink} they don\'t want anymore. In the case of clothes, they will often leave them clean and neatly folded on top of a trash [container](container "container"){.wikilink}.
