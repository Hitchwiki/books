# Switzerland

**Switzerland** is a wealthy country which can offer some good possibilities for [freegan](freegan "freegan"){.wikilink} opportunities.

Most notably, the *Denner* [supermarkets](supermarkets "supermarkets"){.wikilink} tend to have accessible [dumpsters](dumpster "dumpster"){.wikilink}, whilst others like *Coop* or *Migros* sometimes but not always keep them [locked](locked "locked"){.wikilink} inside and send them away with the delivery trucks. Sometimes you can find the delivery truck or a cargo container unlocked and parked near the market. This happens in many cantons but not in all. Just check. The [Geneva](Geneva "Geneva"){.wikilink} area for example seems to have more to offer for successfull [dumpster diving](dumpster_diving "dumpster diving"){.wikilink} in a sense of wider range of supermarkets available for diving, than some other areas in Switzerland. The dumpsters of gas stations are accessible almost everywhere.

Dumpster diving is illegal in Switzerland but as long as you don\'t make a mess it\'s quite unlikely to get arrested for it.
