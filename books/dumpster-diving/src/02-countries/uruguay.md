# Uruguay

[Dumpster diving](Dumpster_diving "Dumpster diving"){.wikilink} exists only in dreams in [Uruguay](Uruguay "Uruguay"){.wikilink}, with the exception of bigger cities. Very few people are diving. On the southern coast it is even better, but watch out, sometimes people dump their dearly departed pets in the [dumpsters](dumpster "dumpster"){.wikilink}. In the big cities, you have the fast food places like *Burger King* and *McD*, but also more people diving. Good places to dive are gas stations: probably the best ones are *ANCAP* and *ESSO*. You can find all kinds of good [stuff](stuff "stuff"){.wikilink} there.
