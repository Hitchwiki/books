### Good places to do that {#good_places_to_do_that}

- *Tesco*. Address: 260 Wilmslow Rd, Manchester M14 6JR. Location of the bins: Round the back in the car park area (the gate is usually open). `<small>`{=html}*(Last checked: April 2012)*`</small>`{=html}
- *Tesco* Stretford (back of the mall).
- *Aldi* and *Iceland*. Hulme. Location of the bins: Round the back in the staff car park area (the gate is usually open). `<small>`{=html}*(Last checked: May 2011)*`</small>`{=html}
- *Co-op* (in most city areas): Majority leave gates unlocked, food is bagged in \"not fit for consumtion\". `<small>`{=html}*(Last checked: May 2011)*`</small>`{=html}
- *Morrisons*. On Grafton Street (opposite the Oxfam shop on Oxford Rd, near UoM and MRI). Occasional pickings. `<small>`{=html}*(Last checked: January 2012)*`</small>`{=html}
