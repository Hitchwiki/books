## Freeshops

Edinburgh has a fabulous permanent and high-turnover [freeshop](freeshop "freeshop"){.wikilink} in the *Forest Cafe*[1](http://www.theforest.org.uk/). *The Forest* as it\'s known is an all around good source of interesting people, information, and all things hippy, so if you\'re in Edinburgh start at the *Forest Cafe*. *The Forest* is also a popular last stop for [couchsurfers](http://www.couchsurfing.com) in the city who want to get rid of things before moving on.

There is another Freeshop at the [Shrub Co-op](http://www.shrubcoop.org/). The Shrub is a not-for-profit, volunteer-run co-operative, which aims to reduce waste and educate people about recycling, up-cycling, etc. It\'s a kind of shop where you can swap almost anything. It\'s run by really friendly people. They also have the [Wee Spoke Hub](http://www.shrubcoop.org/the-wee-spoke-hub/) bike workshop on Tuesday evenings where you can learn to repair your bike.

There is a swap shop located across town at St Margaret's House aka Arts Complex aka Edinburgh Palette. Buzz to get in, and through the doors on the left of the lobby and slightly along the corridor on the left.

## Climate Camp {#climate_camp}

[Freegans](Freegans "Freegans"){.wikilink} were be at the Climate Camp from 20th to 25th August 2010 in Edinburgh. A workshop was held and free [smoothies](smoothies "smoothies"){.wikilink} made from finds were distributed like last the last camp in [London](London "London"){.wikilink}.

See <http://climatecamp.org.uk> more info.
