# Germany

**Germany** is a wealthy country in Western [Europe](Europe "Europe"){.wikilink}.

\"[Containern](Containern "Containern"){.wikilink}\", as dumpster diving is called in Germany, is just easy - no chemicals and relatively little trouble with police/security (it might depend on a region - for example, Bavaria and its main city [Munich](Munich "Munich"){.wikilink} are more conservative than most of other German lands). Dumpster diving is somehow tolerated, and [some vendors](Relations_with_Vendors "some vendors"){.wikilink} from local [food markets](food_markets "food markets"){.wikilink} purposely leave a completely excellent produce so that [dumpster divers](dumpster_diver "dumpster diver"){.wikilink} would have a better and [safer](Health "safer"){.wikilink} meal.

## Dumpster diving {#dumpster_diving}

### Legal note {#legal_note}

First of all, dumpstering in Germany is [illegal](laws "illegal"){.wikilink}. You have to know this, and you mustn\'t be surprised if police come to check your identity. The best way to deal with these situations is to be conversative: talk to them, explain what you are doing, why you are doing it and ask what is bad about it.

There are known cases of store employees coming out and angrily asking the individual to stop dumpster diving, however, when talked very politely to, they leave dumpster divers in peace (see for more info [here](http://de.anarchopedia.org/Projekte:Lebensmittelvernichtung_stoppen#Wie_containere_ich_richtig.3F), in German).

If you get accused of dumpstering, it might be a good idea to answer *\"Oh good, I always wanted to tell people why I do that\"*, and you can talk to the [supermarket](supermarket "supermarket"){.wikilink} administration about that, do demonstrations while other people are shopping, etc. See more info on [workshops for creative anti-repression](http://wiki.tsolife.org/Events#Creative_Anti-Repression_Workshop).

Check as well private brown containers especially provided for organic garbage (Biomüll)they are usually full with bread and delicatessen.

- In December [2008](2008 "2008"){.wikilink} one dumpster diver [was arrested for dumpstering](http://de.indymedia.org/2008/12/235432.shtml).
- In December [2009](2009 "2009"){.wikilink} there was [a lawsuit against 2 dumpster divers](http://www.mopo.de/2009/20091217/hamburg/panorama/diese_beiden_hamburger_leben_aus_dem_muell.html) in [Hamburg](Hamburg "Hamburg"){.wikilink}/Bad Oldesloe.
- In February 2, 2014, a trial against three alleged dumpster divers began in Witzenhausen, Hessen. The prosecution was initially aiming for a penalty of 4500€ or 3 months of jail time each. In the end, all were acquitted february 20, 2914. Read more about the case \[here <http://containerprozesswiz.blogsport.de/>\] (in German).

So far, noone has been finally convicted for dumpster-diving in Germany.

## Table Diving {#table_diving}

In Germany and [Austria](Austria "Austria"){.wikilink} you can enter places like Burger King and go to the holder where people leave their trays. You can stuff your face with all the fries you\'ll ever want.

## Stuff

In living areas it is quite common to walk the street and find some [furniture](furniture "furniture"){.wikilink} and other [stuff](stuff "stuff"){.wikilink} in good shape right on a sidewalk. Some really cool people even include user\'s manuals and spare parts. A sign that reads \"zu verschenken\" (which basically means \"for taking away\") might be attached.

Good stuff doesn\'t stay very long, and it usually takes one or two days for the city-recyclers to come and clean it up, so be quick.
