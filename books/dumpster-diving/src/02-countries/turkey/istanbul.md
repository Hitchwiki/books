# Istanbul

> Named dumpsters and map pins go stale. For current spots use [dumpstermap.org](https://dumpstermap.org/) and the live Trashwiki page. This chapter is culture, legality, and general practice — not a dumpster list.

**Istanbul** is the biggest city in [Turkey](Turkey "Turkey"){.wikilink}.

If you are on *Istiklal*, the main street, you will find that it is very easy to [table dive](table_dive "table dive"){.wikilink} in the fast-foods if you are interested - you can really feed yourself in such a way.

There are street [food markets](food_market "food market"){.wikilink} in every neighborhood usually once a week. Find out when it is in your neighborhood and you can pick up whatever\'s left behind in the evening. There is always lots of [fruits](fruit "fruit"){.wikilink} and [vegetables](vegetables "vegetables"){.wikilink} and people will even hand you stuff they keep as trash.

There are a lot of Lokantas, bakeries and supermarkets where you can ask for old food. You can find a list of \[<http://trashwiki.org/en/Turkey#A_little_guide_for_asking_for_food>: useful phrases\] for asking.

## Markets

You can find a list of the street markets, sorted by weekday, [here](http://www.mymerhaba.com/Weekly-Markets-%28Pazar%29-in-Turkey-538.html).

### Beyoğlu

*Tarlabaşı Pazarı(Tarlabaşı Market)*: Every Sunday, open 10am - \~6pm. After 7pm would be easier to collect leftover: fresh, raw foods. **Location:** It starts from the begining of *Kurdela Sokak* to the end of *Serdar Ömer Paşa Caddesi*

*Kasımpaşa Pazarı (Kasımpaşa Market)*: Every Friday, open 10 - \~6pm. This market is a bit more expensive than Tarlabaşı but better in a way. This market has for example King Oyster Mushrooms which is really tasty. **Location:** *Hakim Sokak + Tahtakadı Sokak + Yeni Yol Ara Sokak* streets.
