# Latvia

**Latvia** is the country by the Baltic Sea, in East [Europe](Europe "Europe"){.wikilink}. Very similar to [Lithuania](Lithuania "Lithuania"){.wikilink}, Latvia has limited [dumpster diving](dumpster_diving "dumpster diving"){.wikilink} and [freegan](freegan "freegan"){.wikilink} opportunities in the country. Although useful trash is not in such abundance as it is in Scandinavia and Western Europe in general, it is possible to find some edible & useful things. For example, for [food](food "food"){.wikilink} it is worth checking the dumpsters of *[Maxima](Maxima "Maxima"){.wikilink}* and *[Rimi](Rimi "Rimi"){.wikilink}* [supermarkets](supermarket "supermarket"){.wikilink}, the containers of which are mostly [unlocked](unlocked "unlocked"){.wikilink} and easy accessable.
