# Singapore

A lot of great stuff can be found at residential areas in **Singapore**.

## Landed properties {#landed_properties}

Large pieces of [trash](trash "trash"){.wikilink} ([furniture](furniture "furniture"){.wikilink}, unwanted consumer electronics) are often left at the front of houses where it usually gets picked up by the professional *[karang guni](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karang_guni)* (trash recycler) people. They normally do their collections on the weekends, cruising around the neighbourhood with a pickup truck and sounding a horn.

## HDB flats (government housing) {#hdb_flats_government_housing}

The majority of Singaporeans live in blocks of flats. There are centralised trash collection centres for each group of flats. Good trash can sometimes be found there, but given that **everything** ends up in the collection centre, dumpster diving is often a stinky, unpleasant experience.

## Flea markets {#flea_markets}

*The Thieves Market* at *Jalan Besar* is one of the largest [flea markets](flea_markets "flea markets"){.wikilink} in the country.

## Freecycle

The Singapore [Freecycle](Freecycle "Freecycle"){.wikilink} mailing list can be found [here](http://groups.yahoo.com/group/SgFreecycle). Yahoo Groups membership is required to view and post messages.
