# Marseille

> Named dumpsters and map pins go stale. For current spots use [dumpstermap.org](https://dumpstermap.org/) and the live Trashwiki page. This chapter is culture, legality, and general practice — not a dumpster list.

**Marseille** is a [French](France "French"){.wikilink} Mediterranean city.

### Food markets {#food_markets}

There are several good [food markets](food_market "food market"){.wikilink} for [diving](diving "diving"){.wikilink} in Marseille.

- [Diver](Diver "Diver"){.wikilink} [guaka](User:Guaka "guaka"){.wikilink} randomly came across of some nice [fruit](fruit "fruit"){.wikilink} and [veggies](veggies "veggies"){.wikilink} after closure of a small street market.
- Diver [Cravos](User:Cravos "Cravos"){.wikilink} found a street market called *La Pradou*, close to the Castellane Metro, where at closure arond 2pm there were plenty of [bread](bread "bread"){.wikilink}, [vegetables](vegetables "vegetables"){.wikilink} and [fruit](fruit "fruit"){.wikilink} being left on the street.

## Food Not Bombs {#food_not_bombs}

- An anarchist social centre called *Le Seul Problème* on Rue Consolat runs an occasional [Food Not Bombs](Food_Not_Bombs "Food Not Bombs"){.wikilink} in the city.
