# Vancouver

> Named dumpsters and map pins go stale. For current spots use [dumpstermap.org](https://dumpstermap.org/) and the live Trashwiki page. This chapter is culture, legality, and general practice — not a dumpster list.

![Alphonse doing some garbage bin diving in Vancouver, searching out any items which may bring him some spare change.](Alphonse,_Doing_Some_Garbage_Bin_Recycling.jpg "Alphonse doing some garbage bin diving in Vancouver, searching out any items which may bring him some spare change."){width="300"} **Vancouver** in [British Columbia](British_Columbia "British Columbia"){.wikilink}, [Canada](Canada "Canada"){.wikilink}, has one of the mildest year round climates in Canada, and a ton of good places to dumpster dive. The downside is that there is a lot of competition in [dumpstering](dumpstering "dumpstering"){.wikilink} in this city.
