# Faroe Islands

The **Faroe Islands** are a paradise for [dumpster divers](dumpster_diver "dumpster diver"){.wikilink}. You\'ll have to look for [supermarkets](supermarket "supermarket"){.wikilink} since there are not many outside the capital region, but if you find a [dumpster](dumpster "dumpster"){.wikilink}, it is usually not locked. Since there are not many people living here, nearly nobody does dumpster diving. Diving is recommended during the night since people there are not used to it at all - if you don\'t make anybody feel uncomfortable, the dumpsters will probably remain unlocked.
