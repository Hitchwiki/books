# Armenia

**Armenia** like [Georgia](Georgia "Georgia"){.wikilink} is a link between Asia and Europe, however as it is the first Christian Country in world history and former member of ex USSR people of this Nation feel more like being part of Europe.

The Capital, [Ierevan](Ierevan "Ierevan"){.wikilink}, has a nice city center where you can find food easily especialy in parks, regarding the rest of the country look for \"bazaar\".

### Personal experience {#personal_experience}

*\"I spent there one week of hitchhiking around country and dumpster diving didn´t work very well for me. For sure it´s possible to find some food but not enough for living. In big cities you can find something but in the rest of country it´s bad. But Armenian people are very hospitable and they invited me often for a dinner or gave me a food and I spent in this country only about 2 or 3 euros.\"* - Král všech moří, September 2016
