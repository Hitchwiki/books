# Valencia

> Named dumpsters and map pins go stale. For current spots use [dumpstermap.org](https://dumpstermap.org/) and the live Trashwiki page. This chapter is culture, legality, and general practice — not a dumpster list.

**Valencia**, [Spain](Spain "Spain"){.wikilink}, is THE **citrus land** providing all Europe with oranges, clementines, mandarins and lemons ; plenty of tree-fields surrounding all the north side, at least until Sagunt. (last check nov 2012)

![frutas y verduras](Valencia.jpg "frutas y verduras"){width="230"} It is hard to find food behind bigger stores, but getting fruit and vegetables is rather easy if you ask the small \"frutas y verduras\" shops if they have anything to throw out, they are everywhere and mostly run by friendly immigrants. Same goes for the markets, there is a big one in the center and some smaller ones. Try: \"Hago reciclaje. ¿Tienes algo que vas a tirar?\"

One of the shops with fruit and vegetables has a lot of good food on Sundays. The address: Arquitecto Arnau, 8. Go there before the closing hours.

If you need clothes or shoes, just take a walk occasionally peeking into the bins. Surely you\'ll find a lot of stuff. There are also some squats with free shops, or \"tienda gratis\".
