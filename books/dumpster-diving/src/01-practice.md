# Part I — Practice

Dumpster diving is looking, with care, at what a shop or a house has already decided is finished. The pages that follow are how to do that anywhere: food, health, law, technique, what to bring, what not to wreck.

Place-specific notes come after this. Bins, locks, and store habits change by the month. The motion does not: show up, take what you will use, leave the place cleaner than you found it.
