# Design

Visual identity for this book is a generic Dutch handbook of living with less — pale garden paper, polder allotments, serif type. It is **not** geldloos.nl branding.

geldloos.nl is a **source** of chapters (see [README](README.md) and front matter), not the cover or chrome. The shared moneyless Drupal cyan stays unused here, as on the English [Moneyless](../moneyless/DESIGN.md) edition.

## Sites (content only)

| Site | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [geldloos.nl](https://geldloos.nl/) | Dutch chapters |

Sister editions: [Moneyless](../moneyless/) (English, moneyless.org) and [Sin Dinero](../sin-dinero/) (Spanish, sindinero.net).

## Logo

None on the cover or catalog card. No cyan wordmark, no dollar-sign O, no black field cloned from the Drupal header.

## Colors

Inspired by allotment soil and polder green — not sampled from the shared moneyless Drupal CSS (`#0ca6bb` cyan on `#181818`):

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `#eef4ea` | Page |
| Ink | `#1c2818` | Text |
| Muted | `#5a6b52` | Secondary |
| Leaf | `#3d6b3a` | Links, cover bar |
| Soil | `#8b5a2b` | Headings |
| Cover field | `#1e2e1c` | Photo scrim |
| Cover type | `#eef4ea` | Title on the photo |
| Rule | `#d5e0d0` | Lines |

## Type

**Source Serif 4** throughout so it reads as a book, not the site’s grotesque.

## Applied

Polder allotment photograph, dark green fade, cream title, leaf-green bar. Catalog kicker is “Leven met minder, of zonder.”
