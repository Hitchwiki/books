# Acerca de Sindinero.net

Moneyless.org is a socially critical project by Robino and Guaka about life without money. The purpose of Moneyless.org is to give ideas for reducing the influence of money on daily life in creative ways, in addition we're expressing our creativity.creativity. Moneyless.org is started as a translation of [Geldloos](http://geldloos.nl/), a project in Dutch, but will slowly take its own course and direction.

## Life beyond money

Naturally, Moneyless did not just fall from the sky. From his studies, political science and journalism Robino has learned a lot about topics such as the role of money and power in a society.
