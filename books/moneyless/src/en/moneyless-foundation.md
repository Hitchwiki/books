# Moneyless Foundation

We believe in a world free from money. Money has its time long overdue. We would like to see the monetary system to be replaced by a variation of bottom-up voluntary organisations that are [gift-economy](/gift-economy) based and rely on social mutualism.
> Our mission is to promote a moneyless society that aims to satisfy everyone's basic needs while (access to) power and resources are equally shared and distributed.
The Moneyless Foundation is here to pursue this goal. It is a virtual foundation, or rather a mutual fund of the founding members of Moneyless. We sponsor projects that we love or that we think are eligable. These are projects that are in our personal interest zone (such as [traveling](/travel-without-money)) or projects that we adhere to.
