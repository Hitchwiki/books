# Projects we love

Moneyless.org is a funny continuation of many projects we've worked on and built over the past 10 years. Back in 2006 I started [Hitchwiki](http://hitchwiki.org) together with MrTweek. Fortunately I didn't accept Casey Fenton's offer to host the project on CouchSurfing's servers. By now Hitchwiki has 3k+ articles in English and many more articles in other languages, by far the best resource for hitchhikers across the world. In 2008 [Robin](/robino), the other half behind Moneyless told me to register [trashwiki.org](http://trashwiki.org/). I did and another awesome wiki was born. This time for [dumpster diving](https://moneyless.org/tags/dumpster-diving).
Last year I started Nomadwiki.org as well, mostly thanks to [Mikael's energy](http://www.mikaelkorpela.fi/) and smiles.
Most people who are active in all these projects have been actively supporting BeWelcome, the best website for [hospitality exchange](/hospitality-exchange-networks).
I hope we'll be able to support all these projects with proceedings from Moneyless and spin off websites in the coming years.

## Nomad- and Hackbases

For about 4 years Robin's house was not like any other house. [Casa Robino](http://casarobino.org/) was a social experiment. One that is continuing in other places, other [nomad bases](http://nomadbase.org/). Yeah, nomadbase.org is one project that didn't really come off the ground. One day?
Meanwhile there's a great hackbase at Lanzarote: [CHT](http://hackerspaces.org/wiki/Cyberhippietotalism).

## Wikipedia

Wikipedia definitely deserves mention. Back in 2003 I was spending *a lot* of time editing Wikipedia. Early 2005 I spent 5 months in Mali with Geekcorps thanks to a post on Slashdot and I set up Wikipedia in Bambara.
