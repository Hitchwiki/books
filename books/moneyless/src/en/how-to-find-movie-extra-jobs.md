It easy, you have to signup, have good pictures of yourself, and standby!
\* Sign up online on some of the sites above
\* Have awesome pictures (more than one!)
\* Basic measurements
\* be ready for LONG days
Tips for movie extra jobs
-
\* Park where your car won't be towed!
\* Eat before you get there
\* Bring a folding chair
\* Bring a pillow
\* NO CAMERAS!
\* Bring food
\* Bring a book
\* Do not bring valuables
