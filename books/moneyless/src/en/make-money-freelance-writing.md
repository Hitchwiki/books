## How to start online writing career

Here are a few pointers to start your freelance writing career
\* Cold pitch: tell friends, family, contact bloggers, companies, small businesses
\* Have a business card
\* Create a free website using a site such as wix.com. have some examples and contact info!
\* Go to a jobboard such as Craigslist and have your pitch there. Post DAILY.
\* Pitch on these sites: www.problogger.net, www.bloggingpro.com, www.contena.co
\* Follow Twitter accounts for job boards: @Write\_Jobs, @WahJobAgency, @WhoPaysWriters, @JJobs\_tweets
