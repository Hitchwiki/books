# Moneyless

Tips and stories for living with less money, or none. Compiled from [moneyless.org](https://moneyless.org/). The project started as Geldloos.nl (Robino, later with Guaka). Dutch chapters are the book **Geldloos** (geldloos.nl); Spanish chapters are **Sin Dinero** (sindinero.net). French (`sansargent.fr`) and German (`ohnegeld.net`) editions are recovered if dumps exist.

License: **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0**.

Loan ads, remittance pitches, and content-mill “make money” pages are omitted. Dumpster diving and similar on-theme chapters stay. Reviews of other people's copyrighted books are omitted.
