# A brief history of hospitality exchange networks

**[Hospitality exchange](Hospitality_exchange "Hospitality exchange"){.wikilink} networks** have been around for a while.

### Non-profit networks {#non_profit_networks}

- [Servas](http://www.servas.org/) is the oldest hospitality exchange organization. Setup in 1948 by Bob Luitweiler, it was originally called \'PeaceBuilders\'. [read more](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Servas_Open_Doors) - [Servas](Servas "Servas"){.wikilink}
- [Hospex](https://www.hospex.org) was the first Internet based hospitality club, established back in 1992. Not active anymore.
- [BeWelcome](https://www.bewelcome.org) was started in 2007. [read more](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BeWelcome)
- [Global Free Loaders](http://www.globalfreeloaders.com)
- [Trustroots.org](https://www.trustroots.org), started in Dec 2014. Non-profit and open source.

### Commercial general networks {#commercial_general_networks}

- [CouchSurfing](http://www.couchsurfing.org) was started by Casey Fenton in 2003 and is currently the largest hospitality exchange network. Also the term \"*couch surf*\" has been around for decades, it used to mean staying on friends\' couches. [CouchSurfing](CouchSurfing "CouchSurfing"){.wikilink} is credited with changing the meaning to include international hospitality exchange. [read more](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Couchsurfing)
- [Hospitality Club](http://www.hospitalityclub.org) is the successor to Hospex and Travelhoo, founded in 2000 by Veit Kühne and grown after merging with the Hitch Hikers Database, a global site based in Lithuania which originally had a dispute with Veit who copied parts of their website. [read more](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospitality_club) - [Hospitality Club](Hospitality_Club "Hospitality Club"){.wikilink}
- BeLodged
- Stay 4 Free
- Tripping
- Horizon [1](http://www.horizonapp.co/) - Hospitality Exchange within your own networks (friends, friends of friends, and people in your communities)

### Specialized networks {#specialized_networks}

- [Dachgeber](https://dachgeber.de/) (a still paper based, mostly german network for cyclists)
- [Gig Swap](http://www.gigswap.org)
- [Warm Showers](https://www.warmshowers.org) (hospitality network for cyclists)
- [Organic Farming](http://www.wwoof.org)`<small>`{=html}^[wiki](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWOOF)^`</small>`{=html}
- [HelpX.net](https://www.helpx.net/) (help exchange network)
- [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/) (work in exchange for hospitality)
- [Volunteersbase](http://www.volunteersbase.com/) (non-profit work exchange for hospitality)
