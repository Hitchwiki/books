# Host

A host is a member of a [hospitality exchange](hospitality_exchange "hospitality exchange"){.wikilink} network who is willing to offer another member of the network a place to stay or sleep in their home. The sleeping arrangements and accommodation provided by a host is often refered to a [couch](couch "couch"){.wikilink}, even if it not in actuality. Hosts are typically willing to host other members under the pretext of [cultural exchange](cultural_exchange "cultural exchange"){.wikilink}, sharing, and mutual respect but [freeloading](freeloader "freeloading"){.wikilink} is frowned upon. Hosts should not expect money or payment for their services. However, they may be reimbursed by their guests for any expenses, such as food.
