# Part II — Networks

The practice is shared. The clubs are not. Each network has its own history, software, and habits — BeWelcome’s membership democracy, Trustroots’ circles and map, Couchers’ newer non-profit stack, Servas on paper, Hospitality Club as a founder’s site.

Chapters sit under the network they belong to. A list of projects and a short note on how the free networks are built come last. This book is not a scrape of CouchSurfing.com.
