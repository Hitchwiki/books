# Sofia

**Sofia** (София) is the capital of [Bulgaria](Bulgaria "Bulgaria"){.wikilink}.

For information about travelling to Sofia see also the [Lonely Planet Tips about Sofia](http://www.lonelyplanet.com/bulgaria/sofia).

## Couchsurfing community {#couchsurfing_community}

Sofia has the biggest Couchsurfing community in Bulgaria. As of August 2009 there are 1,753 members. The monthly meeting is held on the 13th of every month and is announced in the couchsurfing.org/group.html?gid=1325 Sofia Group.

Check the Sofia CS [Google Calendar](http://www.google.com/calendar/embed?src=sofiacsing%40gmail.com&ctz=Europe/Sofia) for more events.

## Getting there and away {#getting_there_and_away}

### By plane {#by_plane}

- Information about the *Sofia Airport* (SOF) on [Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Airport).

From the old airport (Terminal 1) take Bus 84 to the City Center. From the new airport (Terminal 2) Take Bus 284 to the City Center.

### By train {#by_train}

- Information about the *Central Train Station* on [Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Railway_Station,_Sofia).
- [Timetable information of Bulgarian Railways](http://bdz.creato.biz/en)

### By Bus {#by_bus}

- Information about the *Central Bus Station* on [Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bus_Station_Sofia)
- [Official english website of the the Central Bus Station](http://www.centralnaavtogara.bg/cbs/home.nsf/vPagesLookup/home~en?OpenDocument) with Timetable.
- Many international Buses leave from the Bus Station [Serdika](http://www.avtogara-serdika.com/), which is located between the train station and the central bus station.

### Popular Destinations {#popular_destinations}

#### [Istanbul](Istanbul "Istanbul"){.wikilink}

There is one train leaving Sofia at 19h, but it takes 13 hours. Buses are much faster and more flexible. Bus ticket is between 40 and 50lv for a oneway trip, 75-95lv for a round trip.

Bus Companies serving Istanbul:

- Metro Plus [1](https://metrotransport.bg/) Going at 09:00, 12:00,17.00, 20:30, 23:00 from Central Bus Station
- Has Turizm [2](http://www.hasturizm.com.tr/bulgaristan.asp) Going at 10:30, 22:30 from Bus Station Serdika (You can buy Tickets from Air Kona Office\]

<!-- -->

- Alpar Turizm [3](http://www.alparturizm.com/) Going at 22:30? from Serdika Bus Station (Buy Tickets from MATPU-96 Office, they also have the exact timetable)
- Materik Going at 9:00, 21:00 from Central Bus Station

More Companies see [Turkey Travel Planner](http://www.turkeytravelplanner.com/go/Istanbul/Transport/bus_ist_sofia.html)

#### [Wien](Wien "Wien"){.wikilink}

Possibilities i tried:

- By Bus with [Air Kona](http://www.airkona.com/). The Bus goes daily from Vienna-VIB/Erdberg to Sofia \"[Trafik Market](http://www.trafficmarket.bg/en/index.php)\" (The old bus stations between the rail station and the new bus station). The tickets can be bought via [Eurolines.at](http://www.eurolines.at/) or at the Air Konu office at Trafik Market (Attention Eurolines.bg is a different company, of which i have heard not so good reviews).

A two-way ticket is 98EUR/190lv, a one-way ticket is 61EUR/120lv.

- If you for some reasons can\'t take the Air Konu bus, there is the [Union Ivkoni](http://union-ivkoni.com/) Bus. It starts daily in the Netherlands and goes via various german cities to Vienna. It arrives in Vienna at around 4.00am at the touris bus stop \"Belvedere\" at Landstrasser Guertel on the opposite site of Südbahnhof. One way ticket is 56EUR and in my case included a free lunch sandvich at a serbian restaurant (huge white bread with lots of hot, fat, spicy meat).
  </li>
- Bulgaria Air. The flight goes to and from the new Airport in Sofia (Terminal 2). The included food is very basic, but you can get bulgarian wine if you like it. When i was there the bus stop at Terminal 2 was not where the signs say but in front of the entrance.

Possibilities i have not tried:

- Train. It is slower than the bus, 20hour versus approx 13hours.

## Getting around in Sofia {#getting_around_in_sofia}

- The [Sofia Public Transport Company](http://www.sumc.bg/en/common) operates one metro line, ca. 20 Tramway lines, ca. 10 Trolleybuslines and ca. 100 buslines. Tickets can be bought on the vehicle and at kiosks near the stops.

## Staying in Sofia {#staying_in_sofia}

- If you don\'t find a couch, [Hostelworld](http://www.hostelworld.com/) lists currently 17 Hostels in Sofia with [Hostel Mostel](http://www.hostelmostel.com/) being the most popular.
