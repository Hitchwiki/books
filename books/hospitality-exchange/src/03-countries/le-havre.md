# Le Havre

![Le Havre by night](Le_Havre_de_nuit.JPG "Le Havre by night"){width="500"}

**Le Havre** is a city in **[France](France "France"){.wikilink}**, at the mouth of the Seine River. It is a commune in the **Seine-Maritime** department and the **[Upper-Normandy](Upper-Normandy "Upper-Normandy"){.wikilink}** region.

There are 183,900 inhabitants in Le Havre, named the *Havrais* and *Havraise (s)* and 258,514 inhabitants in its agglomeration.

## Transport

### To get to le Havre {#to_get_to_le_havre}

**Train**

The train costs 28€ for one way from **[Paris](Paris "Paris"){.wikilink}** (Saint-Lazare Station) if you have no reductions. You can save money if you take a return and spend the night of Saturday before returning (around 36-42 € with the return).

The station is close to the town center.

**[SNCF website](http://www.voyages-sncf.com/)**

All trains go by **[Rouen](Rouen "Rouen"){.wikilink}**, so you can make a stop at that beautiful city.

If you are in **[Lower-Normandy](Lower-Normandy "Lower-Normandy"){.wikilink}**, it\'s very difficult to go by train because you must go to **Rouen** or **Paris** before arriving to **Le Havre**. Prefer the **coach** (see down)

**Boat**

Ferries from **[Portsmouth](Portsmouth "Portsmouth"){.wikilink}** (25€ - one way) and **[Newhaven](Newhaven "Newhaven"){.wikilink}** (25€ - one way)

Shuttle to the station

**[LD Lines website](http://www.ldlines.fr/)**

**Plane**

Le Havre-Octeveille Airport (**[Airport website](http://www.havre.aeroport.fr/)**)

Flights from **[Lyon](Lyon "Lyon"){.wikilink}**, **[Toulouse](Toulouse "Toulouse"){.wikilink}** and **[Brighton](Brighton "Brighton"){.wikilink}**

Bus to the town center

**Car**

\- From **Paris**: motorways A13 then A131 (via the Bridge of Tancarville - 2 hours)

\- From **Western France**: motorways A84 then A13 and A131 (via the Bridge of Normandy - 1 hour from [Caen](Caen "Caen"){.wikilink} / 2 hours from Mont Saint Michel)

\- From **Nothern** and **Eastern France**: motorway A29

**Hitchhiking**

Good spots to leave Le Havre:

\- traffic lights near the station (Quai Colbert / Cours de la République)

\- gas stations (Esso - BP) on Boulevard Winston Churchill

\- toll booths of Bridge of Normandy (to Western France, you must go by bus #20 and #50) or Bridge of Tancarville (to Rouen and Paris. But there are no bus!)

**Coach**

From **Caen**, **Lisieux** and the **Lower-Normandy**: **Bus Verts** (14€ - one way)

**[Bus Verts web site](http://www.busverts.fr/)**

The bus station is near the station.

### How to circulate in the City? {#how_to_circulate_in_the_city}

**Bus**

\- In Le Havre and its agglomeration (**[website of the agglomeration](http://www.agglo-lehavre.fr/)**), **Bus Océane** is in charge of the bus. But it\'s very difficult to have a bus after 10:00 PM!

Prefer the \"1-week ticket\" (a day to the Sunday of the same week - 11,50€) or the \"1-day ticket\" (3,30€): if you buy your ticket on the bus, it costs 1,50€ for 1 hour.

**[Bus Océane website](http://www.bus-oceane.com/)**

\- It\'s possible to go to cities like Fécamp or Étretat with **CNA** or **Autocars Gris** (ACG)

**[CNA Website](http://www.mobiregion.net/)**

No website for Autocars Gris (?)

**Train**

There is an urbain train called **LER**. It\'s between Le Havre Station and Harfleur, Montivilliers and Rolleville. **Bus Océane** is in charge of this train too. So it\'s the same prices.

**Bicycle**

You can rent a bicycle or a twin with **Vélocéane**

Bicycle: 2€ for 2 hours, 3€ for half a day, 5€ for a day

Twin: 3€ for 2 hours, 4€ for half a day, 7€ for a day

**[Vélocéane website](http://www.bus-oceane.com/presentation/index.asp?rub_code=35)**

\'\'\'[Informations about segregated cycle facilities in Le Havre](http://www.ville-lehavre.fr/delia-CMS/guichet_virtuel/rubrique/article_id-/topic_id-544/velo.html)

**Foot**

The best for seeing the city! Only one car-free zone (arround rue Victor Hugo) but large sidewalks and walk on the seefront.

## What to see / to do? {#what_to_see_to_do}

![St Joseph Church](Eglise_St_Joseph.JPG "St Joseph Church"){width="250"}

### In Le Havre {#in_le_havre}

\- The **city center** that was rebuilt between 1945 and 1964 by Auguste Perret after being destroyed at the end of World War II. Since 2005, it is registered on the list of world inheritance of humanity by UNESCO.

\- **Town Hall**, designed by Auguste Perret - 20th century

\- **Cultural Centre** (Le Volcan), designed by Oscar Niemeyer - 20th century

\- **[St Joseph\'s Church](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_St._Joseph)**, designed by Auguste Perret - 20th century

\- **Notre Dame Cathedral**, 15th century

\- **Priory of Graville**, 13th century

\- **Pasino** - Casino, old \"Palais de la Bourse\", designed by Otello Zavaroni - 20th century

\- **Villa Maritime**, old house of the French dramaturge Armand Salacrou - 19th century

\- **Château des Gadelles**, also named \"Porte de Burgos\", désigned by Pierre-Édouard Dumont, Henri Toutain and Gustave Eiffel - 19th century

\- **University Library**, designed by René and Phine Weeke Dottelonde - 21th century

\- **Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Le Havre**, designed by René and Phine Weeke Dottelonde - 21th century![Le Havre beach](La_plage_du_Havre.JPG "Le Havre beach"){width="350"} - **Novotel**, designed by Jean-Paul Viguier - 21th century

\- **Japanese Garden**, created thanks to the link between the harbours of Le Havre and Osaka

\- **Les Jardins Suspendus**, gardens in an old fort, from where you have a nice point of view on the city

\- Go to the **beach**: between April and October, bars and restaurants are open on the sea front, very pleasant to see the sunset!

\- Walk to the **\"End of the World\"** from the beach to the cliffs

### Museums

\- **Musée Malraux**: large Impressionist painting collection (5€ - free the first Saturday of the month - **[website](http://www.ville-lehavre.fr/delia-CMS/guichet_virtuel/rubrique/article_id-/topic_id-246/musee-malraux.html)**)

\- **Museum d\'Histoire Naturelle**: natural history museum and its large collection of French naturalist Charles Alexandre Lesueur\'s pictures (free - **[website](http://museum.ville-lehavre.fr/)**)

\- **Appartement témoin** (showflat) shows how was an apartment in the 50\'s (reservation at the Tourism Office)

\- **Maison de l\'Armateur** 18th century house shows haw was life in Le Havre at this century (3€ - free the first Saturday of the month - **[website](http://www.ville-lehavre.fr/delia-CMS/guichet_virtuel/rubrique/article_id-/topic_id-250/maison-de-l-armateur.html)**)

\- **Prieuré de Graville**: Christian art collection (2€ - free the first Saturday of the month - **[website](http://www.ville-lehavre.fr/delia-CMS/guichet_virtuel/rubrique/topic_id-249/prieure-de-graville.html)**)

### Near Le Havre {#near_le_havre}

\- **[Sainte-Adresse](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sainte-Adresse)** Belgian capital during WWI (Nice Havrais - 20th century): close to Le Havre by car - you can go by bus #1 and #9

\- **[Montivilliers](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montivilliers)** and its abbey (13th century): close to Le Havre by car - you can go by bus #1 and #9 and the LER

\- **[Harfleur](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harfleur)** and its medieval streets: close to Le Havre by car - you can go by bus #9 and the LER

\- **[Étretat](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etretat)** and its famous cliffs: 20 min from Le Havre by car - you can go by bus #24

\- **[Pont de Normandie - Bridge of Normandy](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pont_de_Normandie)**, famous cable-stayed road bridge: 20 min by car or by bus #20 and #50

\- **[Honfleur](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honfleur)** and its medieval village and old harbour: 30 min by car or by bus #20 and #50 ([Wikivoyage](http://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Honfleur))

\- **[Marais Vernier](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marais-Vernier)**, this old meander of the Seine River is a very beautiful place to walk and see animals (birds\...): 30 min by car or by bus #20

\- **[Deauville](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deauville)** and its famous casino and American Film Festival: 40 min by car or by bus #20
