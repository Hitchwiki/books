# Helsinki

## Introduction

Helsinki is the capital and biggest city of [Finland](Finland "Finland"){.wikilink}, situated on the coast of the Baltic Sea in the southern part of the country. It is the northernmost capital city in the world. Finns often advise that the best time to visit Helsinki is in summer: when the weather is warmest and the sun shines at night, when outdoor bars, cafes, and terraces spring up, and when Helsinki\'s many public parks are filled with picnics, festivals, sunbathers, and revellers. (*See [Summer in Helsinki](Summer_in_Helsinki "Summer in Helsinki"){.wikilink})*. However, an adventurous traveller is encourage to visit in Winter to get a taste of the real \"Finnish experience\".

## CouchSurfing in Helsinki {#couchsurfing_in_helsinki}

The [CouchSurfing](CouchSurfing "CouchSurfing"){.wikilink} situation in Helsinki is generally pretty good. There are a decent ratio of hosts to surfers, especially in the winter months; which means it is relatively easier to find a couch in Helsinki when compared to larger capital cities such as [Paris](Paris "Paris"){.wikilink} or [Berlin](Berlin "Berlin"){.wikilink}. Helsinki has a large and active community for it\'s size; with regular weekly events such as the [Weekly Meeting](Weekly_Meeting "Weekly Meeting"){.wikilink}, in addition various weekday and weekend meetups, multicultural dinners, large multi-day events, parties and excursions.

## Other Hospitality Exchange Networks {#other_hospitality_exchange_networks}

Memberships of alternative [hospitality networks](hospitality_exchange "hospitality networks"){.wikilink} such as [BeWelcome](BeWelcome "BeWelcome"){.wikilink} are increasing, however there are still very few [BeWelcome](BeWelcome "BeWelcome"){.wikilink} hosts in the Helsinki region.

## Tips for Finding a Host in Helsinki {#tips_for_finding_a_host_in_helsinki}

- You don\'t neccesarily need to find your [host](host "host"){.wikilink} in the \'center\' of the city. Many people, including hosts, live **outside the city center.** The \'city center\' itself is rather small and the bulk of the residential buildings lie to the North, East, and West. Public transport in Helsinki is excellent and will likely be the main way your host travels around the city.
- Consider searching for hosts in the two other cities in the [Helsinki](Helsinki "Helsinki"){.wikilink} metropolitan region: [Espoo](Espoo "Espoo"){.wikilink} and [Vantaa](Vantaa "Vantaa"){.wikilink}. In fact, many districts of [Espoo](Espoo "Espoo"){.wikilink} (Otaniemi, Tapiola, Leppävaara) are actually closer to Helsinki city center that the furthermost districts in Helsinki itself.
