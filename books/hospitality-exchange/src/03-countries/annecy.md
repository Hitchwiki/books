# Annecy

![500 px\|Annecy\'s old town from the Thiou](annecypano1.jpg "500 px|Annecy's old town from the Thiou")

**Annecy** is a city in [France](France "France"){.wikilink}- région [Rhônes-Alpes](Rhônes-Alpes "Rhônes-Alpes"){.wikilink}- département [Haute-Savoie](Haute-Savoie "Haute-Savoie"){.wikilink}.

Close to [Geneva](Geneva "Geneva"){.wikilink}, [Lyon](Lyon "Lyon"){.wikilink} and [Grenoble](Grenoble "Grenoble"){.wikilink}, the town is located between its lake and mountains.

Annecy has a preserved medieval old town often known as *the Venice of Alps*. It\'s a quiet and peacefull city with 52000 inhabitants during most of the year (140 000 inhabitants in the metropolitan area). However, in summer and winter, population is growing a lot because of the tourism.

## How to come {#how_to_come}

- **Hitch Hiking**

Hitch Hiking is pretty easy on every main ways. Just don\'t forget that the weather could be bad during the autumn and winter.

- **By car**

Road Network is quite important in the region. You can use the Motorway A41 but it\'s quite ewpensive. Check it on [Viamichelin.com](http://www.viamichelin.com)

- **By plane**

There is a little [airport in Annecy](http://www.annecy.aeroport.fr) (Meythet). The only available route is Annecy/Paris-Orly with Air France.

[Geneva\'s airoport](http://www.gva.ch/fr) is the closest international airport (40 km)

- **By bus**

[Sibra](http://www.sibra.fr) is the city bus company

[Voyages Frossard](http://www.frossard.eu/plans_lignes_fr.phtml?hashID=f1f433f5bc3d801f2c7d52c37bf8714c) and [Voyages Crolard](http://www.voyages-crolard.com/fr/lignes_regulieres/telecharger.aspx) have routes from/to Geneva and main cities around Annecy

- **By train**

Annecy\'s station could be the easiest way to come (especially from Lyon, Grenoble and Geneva). You can book on [www.voyages-sncf.com](http://www.voyages-sncf.com/leisure/fr/launch/home/)

## Best places to have a drink or a meal {#best_places_to_have_a_drink_or_a_meal}

Of course it is very subjective. But here are places we like\... and why

- *Pubs*
  - **Le Comptoir de la Folie Ordinaire** is a small pub with casual atmosphere, art exhibitions and sometimes free concerts. It opens everyday from 6pm to 2:30am
  - **Le Munich** is one of the biggest pub in Annecy.
- *Restaurants*
- *Fast foods*
  - **Quick** is the only burger restaurant in the city center
  - There are a lot of **kebabs** restaurants in town. Best are **Ali Baba** and **Istambul Doner Kebab**
  - You can buy **sandwiches** in most of bakeries and many sandwich shops are open during the summer in the old town
