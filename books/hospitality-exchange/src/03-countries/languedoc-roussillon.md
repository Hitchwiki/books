#### Rural Couchsurfing {#rural_couchsurfing}

![](RCSIcon2.gif "RCSIcon2.gif") In Languedoc-Roussillon you have the chance to have the possibility to couchsurf a bit everywhere, even in the countryside! [Check the map of the Rural CSers in Languedoc-Roussillon](http://maps.google.com/maps/ms?hl=en&ie=UTF8&t=p&msa=0&msid=113144055528929546446.0004605cd5254b0b0620c&ll=43.620171,2.636719&spn=3.642565,9.887695&z=7), you can find them easilier on this way! There is also a couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=15039 CS group called \"Rural CS France\" where you can talk with all Rural CSers in France.

But not all Rural CSers appears on the map or are members of the group \"Rural CS France\". To find the others, join couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=189 the Regional CS group of Languedoc-Roussillon!
