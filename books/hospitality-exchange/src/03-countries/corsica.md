# Corsica

![](Corsica.jpg){width="800"}

**Corsica** is an island in the Mediterranean Sea. It is located west of [Italy](Italy "Italy"){.wikilink}, southeast of [France](France "France"){.wikilink}, and north of the island of Sardinia. **Corsica** is one a the 26 regions of [France](France "France"){.wikilink} with the special status of \"territorial collectivity\". The island can be seen as a mountain in the sea, and it is well known for is diversity in terms of landscape, nature and ecology. It offers around 1,000km of coastline and more than 200 beaches, beside a very mountainous area (with an highest peak at 2706m) with various paths and trails. With the mountains providing plenty of fresh water, **Corsica** is a green and foresty island despite is long exposition to the sun all over the year.

**Cosica** was named « **Kallisté** » (the most beautiful) by Ancient Greece, and nowaday she is commonly named « **L´île de Beauté** » (the beauty island).

## Transport

### How to get to Corsica {#how_to_get_to_corsica}

#### By Boat {#by_boat}

Night boats are the most common. They are leaving from [France](France "France"){.wikilink} or [Italy](Italy "Italy"){.wikilink} at around 6 or 7 PM and are arriving in **Corsica** at 6 or 7 AM the next day. Sometimes, and especially during summer, you can find a day cruise. In all cases, It is better to reserve your tickets long before, in order to get the cheapest prices. The main advantage of the boat compared to the plane is that you can bring your own vehicle in **Corsica**.

There is two main Ferry companies to go in and out of Corsica:

**- [Corsica Ferries](http://www.corsica-ferries.fr/)**: departing from [Nice](Nice "Nice"){.wikilink} (FR), [Toulon](Toulon "Toulon"){.wikilink} (FR), [Livorno](Livorno "Livorno"){.wikilink}(IT) or [Savone](Savone "Savone"){.wikilink} (IT) and arriving in [Ajaccio](Ajaccio "Ajaccio"){.wikilink}, [Bastia](Bastia "Bastia"){.wikilink}, [Calvi](Calvi "Calvi"){.wikilink} or [Ile Rousse](Ile_Rousse "Ile Rousse"){.wikilink}.

**- [SNCM](http://www.sncm.fr/)**: departing from [Marseille](Marseille "Marseille"){.wikilink} (FR), [Toulon](Toulon "Toulon"){.wikilink} (FR) or [Nice](Nice "Nice"){.wikilink} (FR), and arriving in [Ajaccio](Ajaccio "Ajaccio"){.wikilink}, [Bastia](Bastia "Bastia"){.wikilink}, [Propriano](Propriano "Propriano"){.wikilink}, [Porto-Vecchio](Porto-Vecchio "Porto-Vecchio"){.wikilink} or [Ile Rousse](Ile_Rousse "Ile Rousse"){.wikilink}. **SNCM** also provide the NGV service (High speed boat), from April to October, departing from [Nice](Nice "Nice"){.wikilink} and reaching **Corsica** in less than 3 hours.

#### By Plane {#by_plane}

Obvioulsy the quickest way to go in Corsica. Air Corsica and Air France are the two main airlines, and you can take a plane from some major cities in France ([Clermont-Ferrand](Clermont-Ferrand "Clermont-Ferrand"){.wikilink}, [Lyon](Lyon "Lyon"){.wikilink}, [Marseille](Marseille "Marseille"){.wikilink}, [Montpellier](Montpellier "Montpellier"){.wikilink}, [Nice](Nice "Nice"){.wikilink}, and [Paris](Paris "Paris"){.wikilink}). Prices are more (to much more) expansive compared to the boat, especially during the high season (Mid-June to Mid-September).

You can book your tickets online and try to get discount prices here: [Air Corsica](http://www.aircorsica.com//).

**Important**: Starting the 28th of May 2008, for the first time, a low cost company will open a connection between [Paris](Paris "Paris"){.wikilink} and [Figari](Figari "Figari"){.wikilink} (3 flights per week: Monday, Wednesday and Friday). You can check on: [XL airways](http://www.xl.com/) or call them.

Some other low cost companies are now available on the island. Have a look at German Wings or Ryanair (from Belgium mainly)

### Moving in Corsica {#moving_in_corsica}

Public transport is very poor in Corsica. So, except if you\'re planning a lazy holiday on a beach or beat some Corsica\'s long-distance trail, it is essential to bring or to rent (at the airport or harbor) your own vehicle. If you can\'t afford it, hitchhiking is a good option. On the road, be very careful, except for few main routes, Corsica\'s roads are narrow and full of hairpin bends, watch out also for wild and domestic animals\... In addition be also ready for parking problems and traffic jams in majors towns, especially in high season. Travelling is a slow process and can be uncomfortable.

**About the Train**: The corsican train is named the ***U trinighellu*** (the trembler). It can be considered as a tourist attraction (because it offers stunning views hidden to road travellers) as well as a slow and unconvenient transport to link few cities: [Ajaccio](Ajaccio "Ajaccio"){.wikilink}, [Corte](Corte "Corte"){.wikilink}, [Bastia](Bastia "Bastia"){.wikilink}, [Ille rousse](Ille_rousse "Ille rousse"){.wikilink} and [Calvi](Calvi "Calvi"){.wikilink}. Here for the Timeables and prices: [U trinighellu](http://www.trainstouristiques-ter.com/corse_anglais.htm).

**About buses**: There are many bus companies that operate routes between the main towns, but the timeables and the directions are not always easy to find out\... Fortunately there is this unofficial website, which provide accurate information: [Corsican Buses](http://www.corsicabus.org/). Be aware, by using the bus, you loose a lot of flexibility and miss the wild part of **Corsica**.

**About boat renting**: If you want to avoid the mountains, another way to discover **Corsica**, is to rent a boat and go along the coastline. Note that this become an expansive trip and it\'s not given to everyone!

## What to visit in the Region {#what_to_visit_in_the_region}

**Corsica** offers a large diversity of landscape and generally you cannot be wrong wherever you go even if you move only with the wind. It\'s the island where you enjoy nature and small towns all around it.

**Outdoor activities include**: Beaches activities, coastal sailing, diving, kayaking, trekking, biking, horse riding, climbing, canyoning, skiing, hunting and fishing\...

The [GR20](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GR_20) (or ***fra li monte***) is the famous GR footpath of Corsica, reputed to be the most diffcult in Europe (but some parts are accesible to everyone).
