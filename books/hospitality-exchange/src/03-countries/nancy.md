# Nancy

![](Nancy.jpg){width="600"}

**[Nancy](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy)** is a city in [France](France "France"){.wikilink} about 410 000 inhabitants in metropolitan area (1999), Eastern couchsurfing.com/wiki/france France. **Nancy** is a famous place about [Art Nouveau (US)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_Nouveau) / [Art Nouveau (FR)](http://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_nouveau).
