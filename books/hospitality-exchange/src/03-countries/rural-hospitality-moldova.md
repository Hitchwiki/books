## Introduction

Moldova is a small country in the east of Europe between Ukraine and Romania. Despite large numbers of people leaving the coutryside for either cities or to emigrate abroad, there are still people leaving a traditional way of life, sometimes unchanged since before WW2.

In Moldova you can find a variety of landscapes from mountains to the north, rivers (Nistru) and different cultures.

## Definition

\"\"An establishment is considered rural if the majority of the work force is focused on agriculture, forestry and/or fishing, offers a specific and sustainable lifestyle for its inhabitants. The rural administrative environment is made up by communes. Communes are administrative-territorial units which includes the rural population united in common interests and traditions and are composed by more villages. A village is a small establishment not having more than 1000 inhabitants.\"
