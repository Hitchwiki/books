## **THE MAIN CS CITIES Of THE REGION** {#the_main_cs_cities_of_the_region}

### Nantes

- couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=2455 Nantes group
- [the wiki page Nantes](Nantes "the wiki page Nantes"){.wikilink}

the main city of the pays-de-la-loire is the most active CS community of the Region, constituated by more than 559 couchsurfers. In this city, you will enjoy the ambiance of the sixth biggest student city in France, Nantes, modern and contemporary. Industrial architecture as the factory of the famous or Batignolles plant Lefèvre-Utile (LU) today houses the national stage in Nantes, religious buildings like the Sainte-Thérèse, habitats or modern offices ( Delamarre building by Le Corbusier) for the reconstruction of post-war, Art Nouveau (the villa Jeannette), to Art Deco (building CGA), Nantes full of architectural, aesthetic and contemporary urban formed largely during the post-war.

### Angers

- couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=5382 angers group
- [the wiki page angers](Angers "the wiki page angers"){.wikilink}

Angers is the 17th city of France, on the Maine river, an affluent of the Loire. The city has 150 000 inhabitants and offers a very mild and quiet setting to its visitors. Things to see in Angers include the medieval castle, some museums (Musée des beaux-arts, Galérie David d\'Angers, Jean Lurçat,\...), the enjoyable pedestrian \"centre ville\", and the surroundings (beautiful landscapes, vineyards, etc.). The Angers CS group boasts 165 members of which the majority are very active. You will most likely have no trouble having your questions answered. For travelers who are tired of making the same old trip (Paris-Provence), Angers is a good place to stop on the way to the Atlantic coast.

### St nazaire and its area {#st_nazaire_and_its_area}

- couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=11433 saint-nazaire group
- [the wiki page st nazaire](Saint-Nazaire "the wiki page st nazaire"){.wikilink}

The town is situated near the swamp of Brière, an important natural reserve, the second biggest damp area in France after the Camargue (South of France). It is also located just a few minutes away from the salt pan of Guérande, ancestral salt production. Both of these areas include a great fauna and flora.

Saint-Nazaire has a few beaches, like, for example, Villès-Martin, or, a bit further, Porcé, from which you can reach creeks if you follow the pathway along the beach (« chemin des douaniers »).

The urban area of Saint-Nazaire stretches on the right bank of the river Loire\'s estuary to its end in Batz-sur-Mer, Le Croisic and Guérande.
