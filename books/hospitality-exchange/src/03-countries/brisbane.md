# Brisbane

Known as Meean-jin to the local [Turrbal people](http://www.dakibudtcha.com.au/turrbal.htm), Brisbane is about halfway along the Eastern Coast of [Australia](Australia "Australia"){.wikilink} and is the capital city of its state, Queensland. It is the third most populous city in Australia and one of the fastest growing areas.

There are roughly 2000 Couchsurfing members here and this is also growing quickly. We\'ve set this page up to provide a bit of general info about Brisbane things and to provide a \'local knowledge\' resource of things that Brissy CSers like to do, see and recommend.

## Location

![](Brisbanner.jpg "Brisbanner.jpg")

The city is built along the winding [Brisbane River](http://www.ourindooroopilly.com/brisbane-river-map.html) which empties into Moreton Bay. [The islands of the bay](http://www.moretonbayislands.com.au/default.aspx) (Bribie Island, Moreton Island and North and South Stradbroke Islands) shelter this bay and beyond these islands begin the surf beaches; the [Sunshine Coast](http://www.sunshinecoast.org/) (Caloundra to Noosa) to the North of Bribie Island and the [Gold Coast](http://www.verygc.com/) (The Spit to Coolangatta) to the South beyond Straddie.

Further North is [Fraser Island](http://www.fraserisland.net/) and the rest of Queensland including the towns of Gympie, Maryborough, Bundaberg, Rockhampton, [Townsville](Townsville_CS_Wiki "Townsville"){.wikilink}, Mackay, [Cairns](Cairns "Cairns"){.wikilink} and Mt. Isa as well as countless islands, [The Great Barrier Reef](http://www.cultureandrecreation.gov.au/articles/greatbarrierreef/), [The Gulf of Carpentaria](http://www.burkeshirecouncil.com/gulf_history.htm) and [Cape York Peninsula](http://www.capeyorkinfo.org/).

Further South is the border into New South Wales and the Tweed River and beyond that the Northern Rivers region including Byron Bay, Mt. Warning, Nimbin and Lismore.

There are lots of different areas to inner and outer city Brisbane, here are a few **Interesting suburbs** that we live in/like to hang out in, or just think are interesting.

## Climate

Brisbane's Spring begins in September but really takes off in late October when the Jacarandas, followed closely by the Frangipanis, flower. Summer is December/January/February which means Christmas and New Year\'s is usually hot, humid and sometimes stormy! Spring and Summer are also the wet season though Brisbane is currently in drought and heavy water restrictions should be respected by all. The months of March, April and May are Autumn and though most trees in Brisbane are evergreen many do lose their leaves. Brisbane\'s Winter is kind of hard to notice with clear skies and mild temperatures but we get dry, cold Westerly winds towards the end of the season.

## CS History so far {#cs_history_so_far}

*This rather selective history is just meant as a way to record our major events, big stories and organisation as a CS community in Brisbane and the greater South East Queensland area - it\'s not an elaborate diary of events. For that, check out the Brisbane group [calendar](http://www.google.com/calendar/embed?src=brisbanecouchsurfers%40gmail.com&ctz=Australia/Brisbane) or some of the archived issues of the monthly Aussie CS Newsletter (started by couchsurfing.com/people/leimondo Leimondo in Feb 2008) [here](Australian_CS_Newsletter#Other_Helpful_Links_at_a_Glance "here"){.wikilink}.*

### 2007

- Estonian traveller couchsurfing.com/people/lylian Lylian emails most Brisbane profiles to organise a BBQ at the South Bank Parklands on May 20th.
- First \'official\' meeting on the 17th November at couchsurfing.com/profile.html?id=EBOOIF Beni MC\'s. About 20 members attended.
- December - Beni MC becomes Brisbane\'s first City Ambassador for CS.
- Brisbane - Toowoomba bilateral gathering on the 12 December at couchsurfing.com/people/adriannqld Adrian\'s flat in the Valley

### 2008 {#section_1}

- Meeting hosted by couchsurfing.com/people/kourdizo Gayle on January 20th. About 10 members.
- A camping trip to couchsurfing.com/profile.html?id=2HBLTGF Dan\'s property just West of Brisbane in [Marburg](http://maps.google.com.au/maps?hl=en&q=marburg&ie=UTF8&ll=-27.562765,152.596321&spn=0.14944,0.299377&z=12&iwloc=addr) on the 23/24th of February. HUGE, brilliant.
- March - couchsurfing.com/people/boymeetsworld Darren becomes a City Ambassador for Surfer\'s Paradise on the Gold Coast and couchsurfing.com/people/ratandgeoff Geoff becomes a City Ambassador for Toowoomba.
- May - couchsurfing.com/people/dajinjaninja Ninja and couchsurfing.com/profile.html?id=3D13NH0 Skye become Nomadic Ambassadors.
- Meeting at couchsurfing.com/profile.html?id=EBOOIF Beni MC\'s on 17th May. About 15 of us.
- Meeting at [Kangaroo Point cliffs](http://www.ourbrisbane.com/lifestyle/527779.abseiling-kangaroo-point-cliffs) on June 21st before [Fete de la Musique](http://www.ourbrisbane.com/whats-on/gig-guide/953577.fete-de-la-musique). About 10 of us.
- June - couchsurfing.com/people/mustang79 Sally becomes a City Ambassador for Maleny on the Sunshine Coast.
- Meeting at couchsurfing.com/people/nicole604 Nicole\'s on July 27th. About 15 members and guests in attendance.
- On September 21st Brissy CS had its first attempt at representing CS within the local community by entering a team in the [Brisbane City Romp](http://www.brisbanecityromp.com/).
- In December, our Aussie CS mascot, Macalla Koala, came to town for a visit. Check out her pics [here](http://picasaweb.google.com.au/brisbanecouchsurfers/MacallaSFirstVisit#).

### 2009 {#section_2}

- January - couchsurfing.com/meetings.html?mid=25743 Brissy CS Zone established at the Plough Inn at South Bank every 2nd Thurs from January 22nd.
- On March 1st members of the Brissy CS community get together with the [4ZzZfm mob](http://www.4zzzfm.org.au/) to help clean the creek around the [Northey Street community farm](http://www.northeystreetcityfarm.org.au/) for [Clean Up Australia Day](http://www.cleanup.com.au/au/).
- April - Beni MC steps down as City Ambassador.
