# Rural hospitality Serbia

Welcome to Rural Hospitality Serbia!

## Introduction

[Serbia](Serbia "Serbia"){.wikilink} is a great land, with an old history mixed with epic battles, orthdox churches and Turkish invasions.

The colourful history has combined to make lovely villages situated on the flat plains in the north of the country, on the banks of the Danube and tucked away in little mountain villages.

## Rural In Serbia {#rural_in_serbia}

What is considered Rural in Serbia? Obviously there is more to Serbia than just Novi Sad, Nis and Belgrade. And smaller towns like Vrsac, Subotica or Pirot are still simply that - smaller towns.
