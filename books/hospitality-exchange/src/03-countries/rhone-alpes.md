## The Main CS Cities of the Region {#the_main_cs_cities_of_the_region}

### Lyon

[thumbs\|250px\|right\|Pentes de la Croix Rousse](File:Pente_Croix_Rousse.JPG "thumbs|250px|right|Pentes de la Croix Rousse"){.wikilink} [Lyon](Lyon "Lyon"){.wikilink} is the capital of the region Rhône-Alpes, the préfecture of the Rhône département, with a total population of about 1,8 million (city + suburbs). It\'s the second biggest city in France (when including the suburbs) after Paris. Lyon is a major centre of business and has a big student population. Situated between Paris and Marseille, the city is perfectly located 2 hours from the French Alps ski resorts and 3 hours from the Mediterranean sea. Lyon is also well known as the French capital of gastronomy and has a significant role in the history of cinema. For more general information about Lyon, you can check out the Lyon Wikipedia page [1](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyon).

### Grenoble

[thumbs\|250px\|left\|Bulles de la Bastille](File:Grenoble2008.jpg "thumbs|250px|left|Bulles de la Bastille"){.wikilink}

[Grenoble](Grenoble "Grenoble"){.wikilink} is surrounded by mountains. This is the the préfecture of the Isère département, witch 560,222 inhabitants at the 2007 estimate. This is a big student\'s city.

### Chambéry

[thumbs\|250px\|right\|Fontaine des éléphants](File:ChamberyElephant.JPG "thumbs|250px|right|Fontaine des éléphants"){.wikilink} [Chambéryis](Chambery "Chambéry"){.wikilink} a middle town, the préfecture of the département of Savoie. The metropolitan area has more than 125,000 inhabitants.

### Saint-Etienne {#saint_etienne}

[thumbs\|250px\|left\|Cathédrale Saint Charles](File:Cathédrale_Saint-Charles.jpg "thumbs|250px|left|Cathédrale Saint Charles"){.wikilink}

[Saint-Etienne](Saint-Etienne "Saint-Etienne"){.wikilink} is a city of about 180 000 inhabitants 45 minutes from Lyon by train and we love to pretend there is a war-like atmosphere between the two cities. This is the préfecture of the département Loire.

### Annecy

[thumbs\|250px\|right\|Annecy](File:Annecy1.jpg "thumbs|250px|right|Annecy"){.wikilink}

[Annecyis](Annecy "Annecy"){.wikilink} the préfecture of the département Haute-Savoie. Close to Geneva, Lyon and Grenoble, the town is located between its lake and mountains. Annecy has a preserved medieval old town often known as the Venice of Alps. It\'s a quiet and peacefull city with 52000 inhabitants during most of the year (140 000 inhabitants in the metropolitan area). However, in summer and winter, population is growing a lot because of the tourism.

### Valence

[thumbs\|250px\|left\|Valence](File:kiosquevalence.jpg "thumbs|250px|left|Valence"){.wikilink}

[Valence](Valence "Valence"){.wikilink} is the préfecture of the département Drôme, situated on the left bank of the Rhône, 65 miles south of Lyon on the railway to Marseille.

### Getting to The Rhône-Alps - By air {#getting_to_the_rhône_alps___by_air}

\- Lyon-Saint-Exupéry international airport (25 km from Lyon ): [2](http://www.lyon.aeroport.fr/)

\- Geneva Cointrin international airport (in Switzerland): [3](http://www.gva.ch)

\- Grenoble airport (35 km from Grenoble and 80 km from Lyon). The closest option to LES 2 ALPES and ALPES D\'HUEZ ski resorts [4](http://www.grenoble-airport.com)

\- Chambéry airport (10 km from Chambéry) The closest option to Savoy world\'s top ski resorts (3 VALLEYS, ESPACE KILLY, etc.) [5](http://www.chambery-airport.com)

### Getting to The Rhône-Alps - By bus {#getting_to_the_rhône_alps___by_bus}
