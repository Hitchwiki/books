# Franche-Comté

![](Franchecomte.jpg){width="800"}

Franche-Comté is a French region including 4 départements: Doubs (25), Jura (39), Haute-Saône (70), Territoire de Belfort (90).

## What to visit in the Region {#what_to_visit_in_the_region}

Besançon: Citadelle, old city center\... check out the [town\'s website](http://www.besancon.fr)

Belfort: Citadelle, the Lion, old city, music festivals: \"Eurockéennes\" and \"FIMU\"

Montbéliard: castle

Franche-Comté remains a green countryside with amazing landscapes, especially in the eastern (and higher) part: Haut-Doubs and Haut-Jura.

Lot of stuff for those who like hikking, fishing, cycling\...
