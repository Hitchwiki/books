## Things to do in Stockholm: {#things_to_do_in_stockholm}

- **Have a picnic in Djurgården.** Grab smth to eat and go to the green oasis of Djurgården - this island has many picnic spots. You can enjoy a tranquil walk, or take bicycle tour around it, enjoying some of the best views in the city.
- **[Rent a bicycle](http://www.citybikes.se/home) and see Stockholm by bike!** they are situated in many places and all you need is to get a special card which unlocks bikes. Cards can be valid for a few days or a few months, if needed. You can buy a season (April-October) Bike Card for 250 SEK.
- **Catch a ferry to the Stockholm Archipelago**. It is a popular destination in the summer months. Archipelago begins just a few miles east of the capital and covers about 140 kilometres from north to south. Only very small part of the islands are inhabited, although many Stockholmers have summerhouses on islands. Some islands are pieces of forest in the sea, some are simply bare rocks. Ferries depart from the Blasieholmen peninsula.
- **Catch a ferry from Nybroviken or Slussen to Djurgården and Hammarby Sjöstad** - you can use your SL ticket
- **Make a grill and feed touristic swans and ducks at Sätrastrandsbadet.** It\'s the local beach with outdoor gym and beautiful lake. The swans are friendly and can get very close to you (especially if you have food to share) but don\'t try to catch them or make movements that could make them feel unsafe when you\'re right next to them.
- **Take free guided tours** and see [the main City area, Södermalm, Old Town](http://freetourstockholm.com/free-tours/) and [Subway art](http://www.stockholmfreetour.com/the-stockholm-subway-an-underground-gallery/) with a free guide but don\'t forget to give them a tip.
- **Explore the art of Stockholm\'s Subway** - Stockholm is known of its Underground Gallery and some of the stations are really impressive. So buy SL ticket and enjoy it by yourself or join in the free Subway Art guided tour!
- **Check out CouchSurfing Events in Stockholm**. There are a lot of events organized by local people in Stockholm.
- **See beautiful cherry blossoms**. The cherry blossoms are expected to peak between mid to late April. You can find them at Kungsträdgården (you will know why do they call this place The King\'s Garden) and Sickla Kaj.
- **Join Hangouts on CouchSurfing** and explore the area with other travellers or local people.
- **Try different kind of flavours of meads and beers at [Aifur Krog&Bar](http://aifur.se/)**. Feel like a true Viking.
- **Visit the [Tiki Room\'s bar](https://www.tikiroomstockholm.se/).** The drinks are expensive but at the same time very exotic and delicious.
- **Take [Ocean Bus](https://oceanbus.se/)**
- **Have a drink at [Ice Bar](https://www.icebarstockholm.se/)** - feel the swedish winter even in the summer!
- **Check out flea market at Karlaplan** - outdoor market with free admission (Saturdays, 11-3pm).
- **Take [the antique tram no. 7N](https://www.djurgardslinjen.se/) to Djurgården.**

## What to see in Stockholm? {#what_to_see_in_stockholm}

- **Gamla Stan** - old town located on Stadsholmen island is the most touristic place in Stockholm, full of souvenir shops and beautiful alleys. During the rush hours, especially in the summer time there are a lot of people but if you come in the early morning you\'ll find it empty.
- **Stortorget** is the main square at Gamla Stan known from The Stockholm\'s Bloodbath. There you can find those famous, old, colorful buildings so often presented on Stockholm\'s postcards. While wandering around Gamla Stan it's good to know about secret places and hidden things which the most of tourists will miss:

<!-- -->

 

<li>

**Järnpojke** -- in the small courtyard at Slottsbacken behind the Finnish Church (Finska Kyrkan) lives 'Iron Boy' called by some 'Little Boy who looks at the Moon'. During the winter time local people bring him scarfs and hats to not let him get sick. If you leave him a coin he'll bring you luck. Järnpojke is the smallest monument in Sweden.

</li>
<li>

**Upplands runinskrifter 53** -- this runestone is built into a wall in the intersection between the thoroughfares Prästgatan and Kåkbrinken.

</li>
<li>

**Cannonball in the corner of Skomakargatan** -- according to the legend the cannon ball was fired at the Danish kind Christian Tyrant, back to the Stockholm Bloodbath in 1520.

</li>
<li>

**Mårten Trotzigs Gränd** -- the narrowest alley in Stockholm leading from Västerlånggatan and Järntorget up to Tyska Stallplan and Prästgatan. In the narrowest place it has only 90cm.

</li>
<li>

**Aifur Krog&Bar** is a restaurant/bar named after the legendary Viking ship Aifur. Feel the viking atmosphere by listening to hurdy-gurdy, eating viking food, drinking mead/beer. One of the most authentic places in Stockholm. Try different flavors of mead and beer, eat that what Vikings used to eat -- Reindeer sausage, elk sausage, flat-smoked pork, reindeer heart, lamb steak, cheddar cheese. Does it sound yummy already? It is indeed.

</li>
<li>

**Science-Fiction Bokhandeln** -- Great inside decoration, big collection of books (also in English), movies, board-games and gadgets.

</li>
</ul>
<li>

**Stockholms Stadsbibliotek** - the largest public library in Sweden with very interesting interior design and the collection of more than half a million books in over 100 languages.

</li>
<li>

**Djurgården** -- is an island located in central Stockholm known of its parks, monuments, historical buildings, galleries and museums. You\'ll find there places such as:

- **Gröna Lund** -- is an amusement park with many attractions. It's a cool place to hang out with friends and have some fun but also quite expensive. A lot of concerts and events are taking place there.

You can discover Sweden's history, see how Swedes lived according to the changing seasons, through the customs and traditions, work, celebrations and everyday life. During the summer the program is fulfilled with activities such as singing, dancing, concerts. During the winter you can enjoy Christmas markets.

## Public Transportation (SL Ticket) {#public_transportation_sl_ticket}

Arriving to Stockholm to move around need to buy SL Ticket. It works for bus, metro, light rail, rail, tram and ferry. Use [sl.se](https://sl.se/en/) to find your way or SL app (app is more useful and you can buy tickets through it but it costs more). SL-Access Card costs 20 kronor. You can load it with tickets over and over again and the card should last for 6 years. With the card the single tickets will cost 32 kronor instead of 45 kronor. A single ticket loaded on an SL Access card is valid for 75 minutes from the time you start using it.

**Normal prices:** (last update: April 2019)**:**

1. Single ticket (75min) - 45 SEK; 32 SEK with SL Card (you have to put at least 100kr on your card)
2. 24 hours: 130 SEK
3. 72 hours: 260 SEK
4. 7 days: 335 SEK

**Where can you buy the tickets?** You can buy the tickets at each Tunnelbana station (metro station) and Pendeltågstation (train station). There are ticket machines or you can buy it at the gates. You can also buy tickets at SL Center (at the central station) and Pressbyrån. Another option is to buy a ticket through SL app.

**With a single ticket you have 75 minutes to get to your destination.** So, if you pass the gates at the last minute of ticket\'s validity, you can still get a fine. The ticket inspectors check randomly the subways and sometimes stand by the exit from the station.

**To get a student discount, you need to study in Sweden.** Otherwise you don\'t get the discount. Student identity cards from other countries do not work. Neither ISIC does.

SL (Stockholm Lokaltrafik) is **not** UL (Upplands Lokaltrafik). For example for the train to Uppsala you need to pay extra. During the night metro doesn\'t work. You need to take a bus then what is not the easiest, fastest and most comfortable way.

## How to get to Stockholm from Arlanda Airport? {#how_to_get_to_stockholm_from_arlanda_airport}

(Last update: April 2019)

There are three ways to get to Stockholm from Arlanda airport:

1. **Take the bus no. 583** (Buss) from one of the terminals to Märsta station. From Märsta station take Pendeltåg (the commuter train) no. 41 or 42X (all of them go to Stockholm City). This way takes 1h and SL ticket is valid for this transportation.
2. **Take [Flygbuss](https://www.flygbussarna.se/)** (possible destinations: Stockholm City, Liljeholmen, Kista/Sundbyberg/Bromma). Buy your tickets through their page (https://www.flygbussarna.se/) as it\'s much cheaper! Tickets are valid for 3 months from the time of purchase. The ticket is not personal and can be used in any direction on a selected route.

Prices for Flygbuss: Arlanda - Stockholm City (99 kronor, 45 min) Arlanda - Liljeholmen (119 kronor, 46 min) Arlanda - Kista/Sundbyberg/Bromma (119 kronor).

<li>

**Take [Arlanda Express](https://www.arlandaexpress.se/)** it\'s the fastest and most expensive way. It takes about 20 minutes to Stockholm City.

</li>

Prices: Adult (\>26 years): 295 kronor Youth (8-25 years): 165 kronor

</ol>

## How to get to Stockholm from Skavsta Airport? {#how_to_get_to_stockholm_from_skavsta_airport}

(Last update: April 2019)

There are three ways to gets to Stockholm from Skavsta Airport:

1. **Take [Flygbuss](https://www.flygbussarna.se/)** Tickets are valid for 3 months from the time of purchase. The ticket is not personal and can be used in any direction on a selected route. It takes 80 minutes to get to Stockholm City. Always buy the tickets through their page, as tickets bought at the airport are much more expensive. The ticket costs 139 kronor.
2. **Take [SJ train](https://www.sj.se/#/sok/Skavsta%2520flygplats/Stockholm%2520Central/enkel/avgang/20190217-1403/avgang/20190217-1500/VU--///0///)** It is usually the cheapest way to get to the center, but the price varies depending on the time and type of ticket. Youths (\<25 years) have discounts. It takes 2 hours to get to Stockholm City.
3. **Hitchhiking** - it works very well, if you try to get to Stockholm, but not the other way. A lot of people going from the airport pass through the city, so you might have the opportunity to get a ride.
