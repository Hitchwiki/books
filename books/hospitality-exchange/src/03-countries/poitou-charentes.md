![](Children_Welcome.jpg "Children_Welcome.jpg"){width="70"} In the region, there is a very nice, active and developped CS Families network. If you travel with your children, if you would like to be hosted by a family, if you are searching for a host who accept a family, meeting other families making to your children more different cultural friendship, you are welcome to join the couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=16134 French Family\'s group!

A tool exists to localise easily where you can find the CS families\' in the region, please check the [Family Welcome Map of the area](http://maps.google.com/maps/ms?ie=UTF8&msa=0&msid=111108041083507430561.000455631b07e074b6178&ll=76.016094,3.515625&spn=147.966727,360&z=1).

#### Rural Couchsurfing {#rural_couchsurfing}

![](RCSIcon2.gif "RCSIcon2.gif") In Poitou-Charentes you have the chance to have the possibility to couchsurf a bit everywhere, even in the countryside! [Check the map of the Rural CSers in Poitou-Charentes](http://maps.google.com/maps/ms?hl=en&ie=UTF8&t=p&msa=0&msid=113144055528929546446.0004605cd5254b0b0620c&ll=46.13417,-0.186768&spn=3.486898,9.887695&z=7), you can find them easilier on this way! There is also a couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=15039 CS group called \"Rural CS France\" where you can talk with all Rural CSers in France.

But not all Rural CSers appears on the map or are members of the group \"Rural CS France\". To find the others, join couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=196 the Regional CS group of Poitou-Charentes!
