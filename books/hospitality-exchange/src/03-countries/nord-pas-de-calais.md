# Nord-Pas-de-Calais

![](Nord_Andy.jpg){width="800"}

The **Nord-Pas-de-Calais** is the nordest region of **[France](France "France"){.wikilink}**, constitued by the French Flanders, the Artois, the French Hainaut and the Opale Coast. Nowadays, the **Nord-Pas-de-Calais** is an attractive area in [Europe](Europe "Europe"){.wikilink}, making a crossroad between **[Paris](Paris "Paris"){.wikilink}**, **[London](London "London"){.wikilink}**, **[Brussels](Brussels "Brussels"){.wikilink}** and even **[Amsterdam](Amsterdam "Amsterdam"){.wikilink}** and **[Cologne](Cologne "Cologne"){.wikilink}**. If you come firstly in the region, you will mainly see that:

**\"The People of the North of France have in the Heart the Sun they don\'t have in the Sky\...\"**

## The Main CS Cities of the Region {#the_main_cs_cities_of_the_region}

![Lille](PlacedutheatreLille.jpg "Lille"){width="250"}

### Lille

The Main City of the **Nord-Pas-de-Calais** is the most active CS community of the Region, constituated by more than **650 couch surfers**, always ready to host someone. In this city, you will enjoy the ambiance of the second biggest student city in France after Paris, with his street full of people, full of bars, pubs and dancing clubs. You will enjoy the nice classical and modern architecture of the different parts of the city.

To have more information about **[Lille](Lille "Lille"){.wikilink}**, you can check **[the CS wiki page of Lille](Lille "the CS wiki page of Lille"){.wikilink}** and the **couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=3421 CS group of Lille**.

![Dunkerque](Dunkerque2.jpg "Dunkerque"){width="250"}

### Dunkerque

**[Dunkerque](Dunkerque "Dunkerque"){.wikilink}** is one of the nordest french city, situated in the corner between the Belgian Border and the North Sea. Dunkerque is an active CS city especially its annual events like **the Carnaval** which happen during 3 months from January to March. You will enjoy this harbour-city, doing harmonious the mix between its classical city-center and its front of the sea.

To have more information about **[Dunkerque](Dunkerque "Dunkerque"){.wikilink}**, you can check the **[CS wiki page of Dunkerque](Dunkerque "CS wiki page of Dunkerque"){.wikilink}** and the **couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=8487 CS group of Dunkerque**.

![Arras](Arras.JPG "Arras"){width="250"}

### Arras

**[Arras](Arras "Arras"){.wikilink}** is not a big city (about 50000 people live there), but it seems to be the most beautiful city of the **Nord-Pas-de-Calais**. It city-center is a mix of the flamish and the classical style. You will enjoy the beauty of its **two big squares and its City Hall**. There are some CS meetings in Arras, especially during its annual events like the **Main Square Festival** on beginning of July, with the concerts of some of the most famous bands in the World.

To have more information about **[Arras](Arras "Arras"){.wikilink}**, you can check the **[CS wiki page of Arras](Arras "CS wiki page of Arras"){.wikilink}** and the **couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=8490 CS group of Arras**.

![Valenciennes](Valenciennes.JPG "Valenciennes"){width="250"}

### Valenciennes

**[Valenciennes](Valenciennes "Valenciennes"){.wikilink}** is a nice city, with its **new tramway**, it makes the city much better with less cars in the city-center. You will enjoy the modern and the classical city-center. There are some CS meetings in this city.

To have more information about **[Valenciennes](Valenciennes "Valenciennes"){.wikilink}**, you can check the **[CS wiki page of Valenciennes](Valenciennes "CS wiki page of Valenciennes"){.wikilink}** and the **couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=8489 CS group of Valenciennes**.

![Boulogne](Boulogne.jpg "Boulogne"){width="250"}

### Boulogne

**[Boulogne](Boulogne "Boulogne"){.wikilink}** is one of the most biggest habour in France, a former industrial area, changing to a very nice touristical area. You will enjoy the old city, in the top of a hill, the beach, the nautical center called \"Nausicaa\" and its nice CS community which organise sometime some CS meetings.

To have more information about **[Boulogne](Boulogne "Boulogne"){.wikilink}**, you can check the **[CS wiki page of Boulogne](Boulogne "CS wiki page of Boulogne"){.wikilink}** and the **couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=8528 CS group of Boulogne**.

![Calais](Calais.jpg "Calais"){width="250"}

### Calais

**[Calais](Calais "Calais"){.wikilink}** is nowadays known as the **city of the Eurotunnel** which can reach you England by car or by train from France. The city is situated almost on the corner, making the border between **the North Sea and the Channel**. The CS community is increasing in this city and some CS events has to be come in the near future.

To have more information about **[Calais](Calais "Calais"){.wikilink}**, you can check the **[CS wiki page of Calais](Calais "CS wiki page of Calais"){.wikilink}** and the **couchsurfing.com/group.html?gid=8488 CS group of Calais**.

### The CS Activity in the Region {#the_cs_activity_in_the_region}

The CS activity is nowadays concentrated mostly in **[Lille](Lille "Lille"){.wikilink}** where there are often some CS events like the **CS Monthly meetings** happening the first Tuesday of each months, and other kind of meetings like some Quizz, concerts\... There were already some CS meetings in **[Valenciennes](Valenciennes "Valenciennes"){.wikilink}**, **[Dunkerque](Dunkerque "Dunkerque"){.wikilink}** (for the Carnaval), **[Arras](Arras "Arras"){.wikilink}** and **[Boulogne](Boulogne "Boulogne"){.wikilink}**.

If you don\'t want to miss a CS meetings in the **Nord-Pas-de-Calais**, check at the **[CS Calendar](http://www.google.com/calendar/embed?src=cslille%40hotmail.com)**!
