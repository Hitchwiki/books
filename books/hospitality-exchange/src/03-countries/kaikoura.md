# Kaikoura

**Kaikoura** is a town in [New Zealand](New_Zealand "New Zealand"){.wikilink}.

The Maori name is **Te Ahi-kai-koura-a-Tama-ki-te-rangi**

A stunningly beautiful seaside town on the east coast of the south island approx half-way between [Blenheim](Blenheim "Blenheim"){.wikilink} and [Christchurch](Christchurch "Christchurch"){.wikilink}. Beautiful crescent-shaped bay right in town, great for swimming. Fantastic place for beach combing. Just north of town the mountains literally meet the sea.

## Things to to see and do {#things_to_to_see_and_do}

- Beachcombe for interesting pebbles on the beach behind the shops.
- Walk out to the seal colony.
- Walk around the peninsula from the seal colony to South Bay. Huge variety of birdlife.
- Hike up Mount Fyfe - strenuous full day hike.
- There are many commercial tours operating eg 'Swim with Seals', 'Swim with Dolphins', 'Whale Watch Tours', \"Kayak with Seals\'.
- Highly recommended is a trip with [Kaikoura Kayaks](http://www.kaikourakayaks.co.nz) - 0800 452 456 for bookings and info.
