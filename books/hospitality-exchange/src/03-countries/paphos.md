### Transport

#### To/from Paphos Airport {#tofrom_paphos_airport}

Paphos Airport Transport the bus connecting the airport to the city is the 612 and it terminates at the Old Harbour, in the summer period, May to October, it departs at 30 minutes past the hour every hour from 7:30 am until 12:30 am except at 11:30 am and 8:30 pm. In the winter period, December to March, the service is much reduced and runs every hour from 11:30 am to 8:30 pm but there is no bus at 3:30 and 4:30 pm.

Note that in Paphos all intercity buses leave from the Karavella bus station about 3 Km north of the Harbour bus station but the two locations are connected by the 618 bus.

The 613 bus also departs daily from the airport, it terminates at the Karavella bus station but is very infrequent and makes only two departures per day at 8 am and 7 pm.

From April 2012 after Ryan started 15 connections from the Paphos Airport, EAL inaugurated a shuttle bus service to Limassol for 9 Euro which terminates at Agios Georgios Havouzas near the Troodos Junction (Roundabout) of the A1 motorway.
