# Austin

## **Welcome to AWESOME AUSTIN!** {#welcome_to_awesome_austin}

This is our local community message board. It\'s a place for travelers to meet Austinites, and for local members to meet each other. Post about your cool events, favorite local spots, questions for the experts, or just introduce yourself and [KEEP AUSTIN WEIRD!](http://www.austintexas.org/)

**[Austin Couchsurfing Calendar of Events!](http://www.google.com/calendar/embed?src=atxcouchsurfing%40gmail.com&ctz=America/Chicago)**

**Guidelines:** Couch Requests: Please ask members individually after reading their profile information. For urgent or unique requests, you should post your request in the main Austin group.

**Emergencies:** In a **real emergency** contact couchsurfing.com/people/sassyaustinredhead Marcy, CS Country Ambassador or couchsurfing.com/people/omjoshuaom Joshua, the Austin City Ambassador.
