# Part IV — Future

Hospitality exchange is unfinished. Its future will not be decided only by the largest platform or the newest software. It will also be shaped by people who host, visit, organize locally, document what they learn, and try small ideas of their own.

Start with an experiment small enough to do. Host once. Visit someone nearby. Organize a meal or a rural weekend. Try two networks and write down what works differently. Improve a wiki page. Help a free network with moderation, translation, design, governance, or code. Share what you learn so the next person does not have to begin from zero.

The chapters that follow offer starting points: practical experiments, academic questions, related projects, the software behind free networks, and proposals for more decentralized hospitality exchange.
