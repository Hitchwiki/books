# How to search for a couch on CouchSurfing

This is a step-by-step guide aimed at beginners and first-time users of [CouchSurfing](CouchSurfing "CouchSurfing"){.wikilink}. In this guide we will show you how to search for a [couch](couch "couch"){.wikilink}, or a place to stay with a [host](host "host"){.wikilink} on the [CouchSurfing](CouchSurfing "CouchSurfing"){.wikilink} [hospitality exchange](hospitality_exchange "hospitality exchange"){.wikilink} network.

## Step 1: Fill out your Profile. {#step_1_fill_out_your_profile.}

Fill out your [Couchsurfing profile](Couchsurfing_profile "Couchsurfing profile"){.wikilink}. Tell your potential host who you are, and what you are about! Write about your interests, your experiences, and your motivations. It doesn\'t have to be an essay - the important thing is to give it a try. Upload a profile picture, preferably one which shows your face. **You are unlikely to find a place to stay without a fully filled in profile page.** If you need more information, see [How to fill out your profile](How_to_fill_out_your_profile "How to fill out your profile"){.wikilink}.

## Step 2: Where would you like to go? {#step_2_where_would_you_like_to_go}

- At the top of every page you will should find a **search bar** with the words **\"Where would you like to go?\"**.
- Type your destination into the search bar, then click **Surf** or hit **Enter**.
- In this example we have used [Paris](Paris "Paris"){.wikilink}, France as an example.

![Where would you like to go?](Where_would_you_like_to_go.PNG "Where would you like to go?")![Where would you like to go?](Searching_for_paris.PNG "Where would you like to go?")

## Step 3: Find a place to stay {#step_3_find_a_place_to_stay}

### `<span style='color:green'>`{=html}The CORRECT way ✔ {#the_correct_way}

- Searching for your destination takes you to the [place page](place_page "place page"){.wikilink} associated with your destination. In this example, we are taken to the [place page](place_page "place page"){.wikilink} for the [Paris](Paris "Paris"){.wikilink} area.
- At the top of the [place page](place_page "place page"){.wikilink} for your destination you will find a box with the text **Find a place to stay for \[ \] traveler(s)**

![Find a place to stay](Find_a_place_to_stay.PNG "Find a place to stay")

- Select the number of people that require a place to stay and click **Go!** or hit **Enter**.
- You will be taken to the [Couch search results page](Couch_search_results_page "Couch search results page"){.wikilink}.

### `<span style='color:red'>`{=html}The WRONG way ✘ {#the_wrong_way}

- The [place page](place_page "place page"){.wikilink} also acts as a hub for the local community. To the bottom of the **Find a place to stay** section is the [conversations](conversations "conversations"){.wikilink} section.
- The conversations section is a **public forum** where locals and travellers can discuss the local area, for example, to arrange meetups or discuss events that may be happening.
- **Do not post a [request](request "request"){.wikilink} for a [couch](couch "couch"){.wikilink} in this section.**

![Don\'t do this](Conversations_no.png "Don't do this")

- For more details see [community guidelines](community_guidelines "community guidelines"){.wikilink}.
- *Why?* **Safety**: by posting in a public forum, you are inviting strangers to contact you.
- *Why?* **Simple courtesy**: it is more polite to ask people directly for their hospitality. You will be more successful finding a place to stay through personalized messages.
- *Why?* **Spam**: Thousands of people are looking for hosts. Filling up the [conversations](conversations "conversations"){.wikilink} forum prevents the local community from using the forum for legitimate uses.
- *If you still decide to go ahead*: your post will most likely be [flagged](flagging "flagged"){.wikilink} and then **deleted**.

## Step 4: {#step_4}

- You now arrive at the [Couch search page](Couch_search_page "Couch search page"){.wikilink}. Here you can browse the available [hosts](host "host"){.wikilink}, and click through to visit their [profiles](CouchSurfing_profile "profiles"){.wikilink}.
- This is the most important part of your search. Look through the list of available hosts and decide who you would like to stay with.

<!-- -->

- A brown couch (right of the username) symbolises that the member has a [couch](couch "couch"){.wikilink} available.
- You can use the **filters** (bottom left corner) and the **sort these people by:** menu (top) to further refine your search.
- Visit the profile of a potential [host](host "host"){.wikilink} and **read their profile thoroughly**.
