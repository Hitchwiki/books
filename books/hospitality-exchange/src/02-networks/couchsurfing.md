# CouchSurfing

CouchSurfing.com is the large commercial hospitality site most people have heard of. This book does **not** reprint that site, member profiles, or its branding.

What belongs here is history and a how-to that still applies on other networks: searching for a place, writing to a host. [OpenCouchSurfing](https://opencouchsurfing.org/) (2007) argued for a more open organisation; the campaign’s full essays are not copied until authors grant a free license. BeWelcome, Trustroots, and Couchers.org sit downstream of that fight.
