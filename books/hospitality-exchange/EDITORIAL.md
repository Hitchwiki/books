# Hospitality Exchange editorial guidelines

The repository-level `EDITORIAL.md` applies to this book.

Put practice first (`01-practice`), then networks (`02-networks`, each network's history and how-tos nested under that network), then geography (`03-countries`, including rural hospitality), and finally the future (`04-future`). Network-specific history and software sit with that network. Cross-network research, experiments, related projects, software comparisons, and proposals belong in the future section.

Hospitality Exchange is an **explicit title list**, not the whole Trustroots dump. Change `titles.py` for every future fetch; use `omit.txt` for this edition only.

## Placeholders for articles the wikis still need

A missing article may be represented by a placeholder in this editorial file. A placeholder is a commissioning note, not finished book copy. Give it a proposed title, a target source wiki, and a brief explaining what a contributor should investigate. Write the article on the wiki first; add it to `scripts/titles.py` and the book only after it has useful sourced content and a compatible license.

Do not fill placeholders with invented facts, stale platform instructions, private member information, or text copied from a non-free site. First-hand experiments are welcome when clearly labelled and when everyone involved has consented to publication.

### Current article placeholders

- **Small hospex experiments anyone can try** — Target: Trustroots Wiki. Collect low-risk experiments in hosting, visiting locally, organizing a meal or camp, testing more than one network, and recording what was learned.
- **How to start a local hospitality-exchange group** — Target: Trustroots Wiki. Cover the first gathering, moderation, safety, accessibility, continuity, and avoiding dependence on one organizer or commercial platform.
- **How to contribute without writing code** — Target: Trustroots Wiki. Explain moderation, support, translation, documentation, governance, outreach, event organizing, and user research across free networks.
- **Rural hospitality today** — Target: Trustroots Wiki. Refresh the old rural guides through current first-hand reports, including transport, communication, accessibility, seasonal work, and expectations between hosts and guests.
- **Trying several hospitality networks** — Target: Trustroots Wiki. A transparent comparison method based on actual use, without scraping member data or turning the article into brand promotion.
- **Decentralized hospitality exchange now** — Target: Trustroots Wiki. Revisit the older P2P proposals, document present experiments, and separate deployed systems from sketches.
