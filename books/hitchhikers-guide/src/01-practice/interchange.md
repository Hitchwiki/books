# Interchange

![A typical cloverleaf interchange with collector/distributor roads.](Interchange.jpg "A typical cloverleaf interchange with collector/distributor roads."){width="350"}

An on a highway is where multiple highways cross. They are useful to change directions but might be difficult to manage as a hitchhiker.

The so-called highway interchanges are usually poorly designed, and in most cases (e.g., in Germany), pedestrians are generally prohibited from lingering there. It sometimes requires a bit of attention and situational awareness---or even adequate communication (sometimes there are language barriers, for example) with the driver---to ensure you aren't dropped off there by mistake because other options were overlooked.

When are dropped of at a highway rest area before an interchange it might be difficult to find rides that change the direction of the highway you are currently on. Switching rides on a rest area that is not the last before an interchange or choosing a route that avoids interchanges are possible strategies to avoid long waiting times. All which directions of the interchange are favorable can change though depending on how close important cities are places around the interchange.
