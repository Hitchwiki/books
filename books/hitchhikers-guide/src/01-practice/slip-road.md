# Slip road

![](Slip_lane_diagram.svg "Slip_lane_diagram.svg"){width="350"}

![A typical cloverleaf [interchange](interchange "interchange"){.wikilink} with collector/distributor roads.](Interchange.jpg "A typical cloverleaf interchange with collector/distributor roads."){width="350"}

**Slip road** is a segment of roadway that joins a [motorway](highway "motorway"){.wikilink} to ordinary roads (in either direction) on an [interchange](interchange "interchange"){.wikilink}.
