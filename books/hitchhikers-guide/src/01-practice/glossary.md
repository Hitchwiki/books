# Glossary

- [off-ramp](off-ramp "off-ramp"){.wikilink} - highway exit
- [shoulder](shoulder "shoulder"){.wikilink} - emergency land on highways
- [interchange](interchange "interchange"){.wikilink} - highway junction
- [slip road](slip_road "slip road"){.wikilink} - connector road on a highway junction
