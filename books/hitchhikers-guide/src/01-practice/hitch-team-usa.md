# Hitch Team USA

**Hitch Team USA** is the first [competitive hitchhiking](competitive_hitchhiking "competitive hitchhiking"){.wikilink} organization in the [United States](United_States "United States"){.wikilink}. It was founded by [Keith](User:Dr.Keith "Keith"){.wikilink} in the summer of 2014, in hopes of one day competing in the [world championships](world_championships "world championships"){.wikilink}. As of October, 2015, it has three members, spread out over the country.

Are you an American hitchhiker interested in sport hitchhiking? Feel free to join the Facebook group below, or ask [Keith](User:Dr.Keith "Keith"){.wikilink} for more info.
