# Toll road

![A toll road in [Venezuela](Venezuela "Venezuela"){.wikilink}](Toll_road_in_Venezuela.JPG "A toll road in Venezuela"){width="250"}

A **toll road** ( **tollway**, **turnpike**, **pike**, or **toll highway**) is a road for which a driver pays a fee for use.

The building or facility in which a fee is collected is called a [toll station](toll_station "toll station"){.wikilink} (toll plaza, toll booth, or toll gate). Toll stations can be a good option for hitchhikers to get a ride.

In [France](France "France"){.wikilink} and French-speaking countries toll roads are called *[péage](péage "péage"){.wikilink}*.
