# Good times to hitchhike

There are **good times** and less good times to hitchhike. Naturally [hitchhiking at night](hitchhiking_at_night "hitchhiking at night"){.wikilink} should be discouraged to anyone [new to hitchhiking](first_time_hitchhiking "new to hitchhiking"){.wikilink}. But there is more to say.

## Time of day {#time_of_day}

Data from over 43,000 logged rides on [maps.hitchwiki.org](https://maps.hitchwiki.org/statistics) shows that **early morning (5--8 AM)** has the shortest median wait times --- about 15 minutes, versus 20 minutes during the middle of the day. The difference is modest (5 minutes), but starting early also means more daylight hours ahead and less competition from other hitchhikers at popular spots.

Late afternoon (around 3 PM / 15:00) shows a similar dip to the early morning, likely because commuters are heading home.

In short: **start early if you can**, but don\'t let the time stop you --- the median wait is 15--20 minutes regardless of when you go.

## Days of the week {#days_of_the_week}

**Friday** afternoon is definitely the best time for hitchhiking in most western countries. So many people are rushing to visit friends or family for the weekend. Also, everyone is in a great mood, leaving their work behind and looking forward for the weekend, which makes them more likely to pick you up.

**Sunday** morning until noon seems the worst time to hitchhike in [Europe](Europe "Europe"){.wikilink}, and many other parts of the \"developed\" world influenced by Christianity (the [US](United_States_of_America "US"){.wikilink}, [Oz](Australia "Oz"){.wikilink}, [Kiwiland](New_Zealand "Kiwiland"){.wikilink} and [Canadialand](Canada "Canadialand"){.wikilink} come to mind). Cars are full of families, with possibly one grumpy person on the side and not too many working people on the road. It usually gets better in the afternoon, when people are driving back from their weekend trip alone. Sundays during extended weekends due to religious holidays (e.g. Easter) are even worse; make sure to start early enough and reach your destination way before the evening hours, or you\'ll be stuck by extremely low traffic, esp. along German highways.

On the other hand, on Sunday afternoon and early evening it is possible to catch fast & long lifts since many people visit their family, wife, husband boy/girlfriend who live far away during the weekend and have to return for work on Monday morning.

In [Israel](Israel "Israel"){.wikilink} people work on Sundays so you\'ll be fine, but Shabbath is a horrible day for hitchhiking there since religious people are not driving at all (and they\'re usually quite skilled at [picking up hitchhikers](picking_up_hitchhikers "picking up hitchhikers"){.wikilink}).

In many Muslim countries people also work on Sundays, and Friday is the day of the mosque, with considerably less traffic in countries like [Jordan](Jordan "Jordan"){.wikilink}.

In a lot of [European](Europe "Europe"){.wikilink} countries, most of the [trucks](hitchhiking_a_truck "trucks"){.wikilink} are not allowed on the highways on Sundays until 22:00. They too are a hitchhiker-fond crowd. Sometimes. However, they all leaving their places at exactly 22:00. So, if you stuck late on a big service area, you can see them as a *get-home-insurance*. It\'s surely not a problem to find a truck to any destination if you start asking around at around 21:30.

## Time of the year {#time_of_the_year}

In general, because of the length of the days and the cold [Winter](Winter "Winter"){.wikilink} is bad, Autumn is also not that great, especially late Autumn. Spring and Summer are best, especially around the longest day, 21st of June (in the Northern hemisphere of course).
