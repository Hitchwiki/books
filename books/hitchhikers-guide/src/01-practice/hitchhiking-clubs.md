# Hitchhiking clubs

*For organizations who are dedicated to races see [Races](Races "Races"){.wikilink}. For lobbying organizations who promote hitchhiking see [Lobby](Lobby "Lobby"){.wikilink}*

**Hitch-hiking clubs** bring hitchhikers together, organize various competitions, meetings, etc. They are especially popular in countries of former Soviet Union.
