# Taxi

It\'s definitely possible to hitch a taxi. In most of [Europe](Europe "Europe"){.wikilink} taxi drivers recognize hitchhikers and in rare cases also pick them up. Just make sure they are taking you for free before you get in. [Albania](Albania "Albania"){.wikilink} is an exception, almost every driver tries to convince you he\'s taxi driver and that you should pay.

In some [African](Africa "Africa"){.wikilink} or [Asian](Asia "Asia"){.wikilink} countries taxi drivers are often annoying and it\'s hard to get rid of them. But even there you can sometimes get a free lift with a taxi.

## Shared taxi / bush taxi / marshrutka {#shared_taxi_bush_taxi_marshrutka}

Common mode of public transportation in many countries both within and between the cities. It can be the cheapest alternative to hitchhiking. [More information at wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_taxi)
