# The Hitchhiker's Guide to Hitchhiking

Compiled from [Hitchwiki](https://hitchwiki.org/), a collaborative guide for hitchhikers. Hitchwiki was originally named *Hitchhiker's Guide to Hitchhiking*. This book is **not** related to Douglas Adams or *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*.

License: **CC-BY-SA-4.0**. Credit the Hitchwiki community and link to https://hitchwiki.org/.

This edition is how-to practice plus country overviews. It does not include city or roadside-spot gazetteers. Those stay on the live wiki and on Hitchwiki Maps.

Hitchhiking is legally grey in some places. Trust your judgement; this book republishes community guides, it is not legal advice.
