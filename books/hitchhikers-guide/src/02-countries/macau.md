# Macau

The **Macau Special Administrative Region**, commonly known as *Macau* or *Macao* (Chinese: 澳門), is one of the two special administrative regions of the [People\'s Republic of China](China "People's Republic of China"){.wikilink}.

## Hitchhiking out to Hong Kong {#hitchhiking_out_to_hong_kong}

You can hitch at the entrance of the bridge going to the Immigration building on Artificial Island (Avenida Doutor Ma Man Kei). If you are lucky enough, you\'ll stop a car going to Hong Kong through the Bridge. But many cars will just stop to the Arrivals Hall to pick-up relatives. In all cases, you need to pass through immigration on the first floor of the building: ignore the bus ticket machines, and go directly to the immigration offices. On your way out, meet your driver on the drop-off area or find another car by hitchhiking on the road (left side when you exit the building) - experience by SerialHikers, September 2019.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in Macau against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on. If you can read the official text, please replace this paragraph with the provision it turns on and a link to it.
