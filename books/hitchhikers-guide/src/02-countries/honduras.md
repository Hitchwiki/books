# Honduras

**Honduras** is a country in [Central America](Central_America "Central America"){.wikilink}.

It borders [Nicaragua](Nicaragua "Nicaragua"){.wikilink} to the south, [Guatemala](Guatemala "Guatemala"){.wikilink} to the north and [El Salvador](El_Salvador "El Salvador"){.wikilink} to the north-west and west.

## Hitchhiking

Hitchhiking is generally easy in Honduras. It is a quite poor country and a lot of people don\'t have cars. Rides come readily from people with pick-up trucks and it is not rare to see locals hitch-hiking.

Like in most of Central-America, there are lots of minibusses and not so easy to spot taxis on the road, be sure you know if you\'re paying or hitching free when you climb in any type of vehicle. Here people refer to hitch-hiking as *jalón* or *ride*, *pedir jalón* and *buscar un ride* are common expressions.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in Honduras against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on.
