# Northern Ireland

**Northern Ireland** is a part of the island of [Ireland](Ireland "Ireland"){.wikilink}, but is politically part of the [United Kingdom](United_Kingdom "United Kingdom"){.wikilink}. Unlike the Irish Republic which uses the Euro the currency in Northern Ireland is the British Pound.

## Hitchhiking

Hitchhiking is usually quite good, comparable to Ireland. Northern Ireland is connected to Scotland and to England via ferry (not hitchable) to [Larne](Larne "Larne"){.wikilink} and [Belfast](Belfast "Belfast"){.wikilink}.

Hitching along the coast from a little ways north of Belfast up to and around Giant\'s Causeway is lovely. Be sure to stand where there is room to see you and pull over. People were very wonderful in picking me up, but I\'m a woman. Many said they wouldn\'t stop for men alone, though they very rarely see hitch hikers anymore. Be sure to look nice, dress well, for easier rides.

For more detailed hitchhiking information, you should check the article [Ireland](Ireland "Ireland"){.wikilink}.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

Hitchhiking is legal in Northern Ireland and no law forbids asking for a lift; only the motorways are closed to you, under regulations of their own rather than the ones covering Great Britain. [The Motorways Traffic Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/135/made) treat pedestrians as excluded traffic and regulation 13(1)(b) allows them on a motorway only where an accident, an emergency or a vehicle at rest makes it necessary --- so the carriageway, the hard shoulder and the slip roads of the M1, M2 and the rest are out. Drivers cannot legally stop for you there either, the same regulations restricting stopping to emergencies and the places provided. Northern Ireland has a short motorway network and a long A-road one, and on the A-roads hitchhiking is unregulated. Stand off the carriageway, on the verge, where a driver can see you and pull in safely.
