# Côte d'Ivoire

**Cote d\'Ivoire** is a country in south [West Africa](Western_Africa "West Africa"){.wikilink} and borders the countries of [Liberia](Liberia "Liberia"){.wikilink}, [Guinea](Guinea "Guinea"){.wikilink}, [Mali](Mali "Mali"){.wikilink}, [Burkina Faso](Burkina_Faso "Burkina Faso"){.wikilink} and [Ghana](Ghana "Ghana"){.wikilink}. The official language is French but other languages are commonly used as well, e.g. [Dioula](Africa_phrasebook#Bambara "Dioula"){.wikilink}.

**Warning:** as of 2011 a severe crisis is going on in the country, with civil unrest and widespread violence.

## Hitchhiking

Possibly not a good idea to hitch around until this is settled.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in Côte d\'Ivoire against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on. If you can read the official text, please replace this paragraph with the provision it turns on and a link to it.
