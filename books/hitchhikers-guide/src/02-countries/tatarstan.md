# Tatarstan

The **Republic of Tatarstan** (Russian: Респу́блика Татарста́н; Tatar Cyrillic: Татарстан Республикасы, Latin: Tatarstan Respublikası) is a federal subject of the Russian Federation (a republic). Its capital is Kazan.

## Hitchhiking

Hitchhiking within Tatarstan can be considered as pretty easy.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

Tatarstan is a federal subject of [Russia](Russia "Russia"){.wikilink} and road traffic is federal competence, so the law here is exactly the law described under Russia, and hitchhiking (*автостоп*) is not an offence. [Clause 16.1 of the *Правила дорожного движения*](https://base.garant.ru/1305770/), made by Government Decree No. 1090 of 23 October 1993, bans pedestrians from an *автомагистраль* and forbids a driver to stop outside the parking areas marked by sign 6.4 or 7.11; clause 16.3 extends the section to roads signed 5.3. This matters more in Tatarstan than in most of Russia, because the M12 toll road to [Kazan](Kazan "Kazan"){.wikilink} is built and signed to motorway standard --- on that one, both the walking and the stopping are prohibited, and the signed service areas are the only lawful place to ask. The M7 and the older federal routes are not uniformly signed that way, so read the sign at the point you are standing rather than assuming from the road number. Off a signed motorway, waiting on the verge and thumbing breaks no rule.
