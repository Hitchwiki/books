# Saint Barthélemy

\'\'\'\'\'\' is an island in the eastern Caribbean Sea and an overseas department of [France](France "France"){.wikilink}. To the north-west lies [Saint Martin](Saint_Martin "Saint Martin"){.wikilink}, the south [Saint Kitts and Nevis](Saint_Kitts_and_Nevis "Saint Kitts and Nevis"){.wikilink} as well as [Saba](Saba "Saba"){.wikilink} and [St. Eustatius](St._Eustatius "St. Eustatius"){.wikilink}.

The majority of the population living there are from Europe and North America. Saint Barthélemy is unique in the Caribbean as no slave population lived here more than a few years as the island has no natural water source, is quite rugged and very dry.

## Hitchhiking

Like France, Saint Barthélemy is an easy island to get a lift although hitching is not common.

There is a ferry is Saint Martin from [Gustavia](Gustavia "Gustavia"){.wikilink}.

Gustavia is the best place to hitch onto boats.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in Saint Barthélemy against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on.
