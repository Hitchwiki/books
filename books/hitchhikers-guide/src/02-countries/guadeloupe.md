## Hitchhiking

### On the island {#on_the_island}

Quite easy, and unlike [Martinique](Martinique "Martinique"){.wikilink} some locals will also pick you up but lifts mainly come from the French/Europeans.

### Boats

Basse-Terre and Deshaies on the west coast are the best places. Ferries to [Dominica](Dominica "Dominica"){.wikilink} and [Martinique](Martinique "Martinique"){.wikilink}.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

Guadeloupe is an overseas department, so the French *code de la route* applies in full and, as in metropolitan [France](France "France"){.wikilink}, hitchhiking is not an offence. No road in Guadeloupe is classified as an *autoroute*, so [article R421-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006842313), which bars pedestrians from motorways, and article R421-1, which extends the ban to the slip roads, have nothing here to apply to --- unlike neighbouring [Martinique](Martinique "Martinique"){.wikilink}, which has the A1. The rule that governs you instead is [article R412-36](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074228/LEGISCTA000006177125/): outside built-up areas a pedestrian keeps to the left-hand edge of the road, facing the oncoming traffic. The catch is the *voie rapide* --- the dual-carriageway sections of the N1 and N11 around [Pointe-à-Pitre](Pointe-à-Pitre "Pointe-à-Pitre"){.wikilink} and [Baie-Mahault](Baie-Mahault "Baie-Mahault"){.wikilink} are signposted as reserved for motor vehicles, and that sign excludes pedestrians in its own right even though the road is not an autoroute. Thumb from the roundabouts and the ordinary N-road sections either side of those stretches.
