# North Korea

Travels to North Korea will only be approved if somebody from the government shows you around and guides you.

## Hitchhiking

Until a revolution or some form of *perestrojka*, **North Korea** is probably the last country on the face of the planet Earth where you cannot go put up your thumb on the side of the road. You cannot travel freely in North Korea and the roads are empty. You\'ll probably have more luck in [South Korea](South_Korea "South Korea"){.wikilink}.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in North Korea against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on. If you can read the official text, please replace this paragraph with the provision it turns on and a link to it.

Local people will be punished if you (as a foreigner) talk to them.
