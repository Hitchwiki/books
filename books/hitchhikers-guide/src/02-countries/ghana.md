# Ghana

**Ghana** is a country on the coast of [Western Africa](Western_Africa "Western Africa"){.wikilink}. A former British colony, the official language is English. It\'s considered to be one of the safer countries of Western Africa.

## Cities

- [Accra](Accra "Accra"){.wikilink}

## Additional information {#additional_information}

Like many countries in Africa, Ghana has a lot of police barriers and at times (like when you are 3 people in a 2 seater truck) your being in the vehicle will mean the driver will have pay a fine. It\'s good to be aware of this, and offer some money if you think you are part of the alleged problem.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in Ghana against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on. If you can read the official text, please replace this paragraph with the provision it turns on and a link to it.
