# Togo

**Togo** is a country in [Western Africa](Western_Africa "Western Africa"){.wikilink}, with borders to [Ghana](Ghana "Ghana"){.wikilink}, [Benin](Benin "Benin"){.wikilink} and [Burkina Faso](Burkina_Faso "Burkina Faso"){.wikilink}. The official language is [French](Africa_phrasebook "French"){.wikilink}.

## Hitchhiking

Hitching Togo is very easy if you are on a road that has any traffic. By at least one statistic Togo has the least vehicles per capita of any country on earth so be prepared to walk when not on main highways. It is quite often that people will offer you free motorcycle rides in the backwoods. Many vehicles are taxis so make sure the ride is free before you get in, speaking basic French is important. White plates are private cars and yellow plates are all other non-governmental vehicles. Police are rare and have little power. The Togolese are generally friendly and may invite you to stay at their house.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in Togo against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on. If you can read the official text, please replace this paragraph with the provision it turns on and a link to it.
