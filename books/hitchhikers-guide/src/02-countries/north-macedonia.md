# North Macedonia

**North Macedonia** is a landlocked country in [Eastern Europe](Eastern_Europe "Eastern Europe"){.wikilink}, surrounded, clock-wise from the north, by [Kosovo](Kosovo "Kosovo"){.wikilink}, [Serbia](Serbia "Serbia"){.wikilink}, [Bulgaria](Bulgaria "Bulgaria"){.wikilink}, [Greece](Greece "Greece"){.wikilink} and [Albania](Albania "Albania"){.wikilink}. People are extremely friendly here and particularly fascinated by travelers due to the difficulty in obtaining visas into the EU.

## Hitchhiking

Just thumbing a direction will produce quick results with people eager to help. There are many truck drivers headed to the border of [Greece](Greece "Greece"){.wikilink} but may be reluctant to take you through. This appears to be the same for most borders within the Balkan area. Most of the time they will drive with you to the border, ask you to get out and walk through the border and enter the truck again once both of you are through.

People are usually friendly and ready to help, although most car drivers will not understand the concept of hitch-hiking and private taxi is a major form of public transport due to high level of unemployment. Therefore, make sure before you board a vehicle that the driver is not an informal taxi. Also make sure to keep an eye on the road you are going, if the driver makes a sudden turn around, changes his natural path or takes another direction, it is likely that he is either a taxi driver or that he thinks he is doing good by bringing you to the local bus station. You therefore will have to say, if necessary again and again, that you do \'autostop\' all the way.

- *According to [Rozwal](User:Rozwal "Rozwal"){.wikilink}\'s experience (November-December 2011) after hitchhiking in North Macedonia in all directions, the above problems seemed redundant. He has never come across any hidden taxi nor have had any issues with people not understanding the concept of hitch-hiking. No-one asked for money too. In general, North Macedonia was hitch-friendly and the use of signs with number plates\' symbols of cities worked just fine (instead of writing Skopje you can write SK, Prilep: PP, Veles: VE, Bitola: BT, Ohrid: OH, etc.).*

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

Nothing in Macedonian law makes hitchhiking (*автостоп*) an offence, and on ordinary roads it is legal. [Article 129(1) of the *Закон за безбедност на сообраќајот на патиштата*](https://rsbsp.org.mk/sites/default/files/zakon-za-bezbednost-na-soobrakajot-na-patistata-precisten.pdf) keeps pedestrians off an *автопат*, an *експресен пат* and any road reserved for motor vehicles, together with bicycles, tractors, horse-drawn vehicles and livestock. Article 130(1) lets a driver stop or park on those roads only on the areas off the carriageway that are specially arranged and marked, and 130(3) forbids U-turns and reversing, so a driver who has passed you cannot come back. The marked rest areas and their petrol stations are therefore the one place on the motorway network where a lift can lawfully begin. On the *магистрални* and *регионални патишта* that carry most of the country\'s traffic, thumbing from the verge breaks no rule.
