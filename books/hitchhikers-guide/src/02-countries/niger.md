# Niger

![[Niamey](Niamey "Niamey"){.wikilink} city at night](Niamey_night.jpg "Niamey city at night")

**Niger** is a landlocked country in [Western Africa](Western_Africa "Western Africa"){.wikilink}, most of it is covered by the Sahara desert. It is bordered by [Algeria](Algeria "Algeria"){.wikilink}, [Libya](Libya "Libya"){.wikilink}, [Chad](Chad "Chad"){.wikilink}, [Nigeria](Nigeria "Nigeria"){.wikilink}, [Benin](Benin "Benin"){.wikilink}, [Burkina Faso](Burkina_Faso "Burkina Faso"){.wikilink} and [Mali](Mali "Mali"){.wikilink}.

## Safety

The region close to Mali is not very safe. Read up on [safety](hitchhiker's_safety "safety"){.wikilink} before you go. Crime rate is high, landmines in some regions, check out carefully before you go.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in Niger against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on. If you can read the official text, please replace this paragraph with the provision it turns on and a link to it.
