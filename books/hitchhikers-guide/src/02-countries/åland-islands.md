# Åland Islands

The **Åland Islands** (Finnish:**Ahvenanmaa**) are a huge bunch of sometimes really small islands between [Finland](Finland "Finland"){.wikilink} and [Sweden](Sweden "Sweden"){.wikilink}.

## Hitchhiking

As all roads are really small, a good spot for hitchhiking is never difficult to find. The city buses in Mariehamn are for free. The short range ferries (on which you easily can find a lift to the next one) are for free as well for foot passengers.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

Åland is autonomous in road traffic and legislates for itself rather than applying [Finland](Finland "Finland"){.wikilink}\'s law, and hitchhiking appears nowhere in what it has enacted. The [Vägtrafiklag (2023:108) för Åland](https://www.regeringen.ax/alandsk-lagstiftning/alex/2023108) does not use the concept of a motorway at all --- there are none in the islands --- so unlike almost everywhere else in Europe there is no class of road you are excluded from. What governs you is § 15: a pedestrian uses the footway or the *vägren*, the strip outside the carriageway\'s edge line, and only where neither is passable without difficulty may you use the edge of the cycleway or the carriageway. Anyone on the vägren or the carriageway shall, \"if possible\", keep furthest to the left in the direction of travel, meaning facing the oncoming traffic --- the one point of friction for a hitchhiker, who wants the right-hand verge where the cars going their way can pull in, though the rule is qualified and is addressed to walking rather than standing still. The same section makes a reflector compulsory after dark, which on Åland\'s unlit country roads in winter matters more than the law behind it.

## How to get there {#how_to_get_there}

The common way to get to Åland is by ferry. There are big ferries from [Sweden](Sweden "Sweden"){.wikilink} ([Stockholm](Stockholm "Stockholm"){.wikilink}, [Kapellskär](Kapellskär "Kapellskär"){.wikilink}, [Grisslehamn](Grisslehamn "Grisslehamn"){.wikilink}) and from [Finland](Finland "Finland"){.wikilink} ([Helsinki](Helsinki "Helsinki"){.wikilink}, [Turku](Turku "Turku"){.wikilink}). They usually don\'t cost more than EUR 20.

### Archipelago ferries {#archipelago_ferries}

From Turku, you can also try to hitch-hike via the archipelago. You can get a map of the archipelago and the ferries at the tourist info in Turku.

1. North: Hummelvik − Kumlinge, Brändö − Osnäs
2. South: Långnäs − Sottunga − Galtby
