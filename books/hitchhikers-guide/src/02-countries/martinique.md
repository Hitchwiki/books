# Martinique

**Martinique** is an island in the eastern Caribbean Sea and an overseas department of [France](France "France"){.wikilink}. To the northwest lies [Dominica](Dominica "Dominica"){.wikilink}, to the south [Saint Lucia](Saint_Lucia "Saint Lucia"){.wikilink}.

The island is bigger than it\'s neighbours and it can take some time going from the north coast to the south.

Ferries to Dominica, Guadeloupe and Saint Lucia are available.

## Hitchhiking

Like France, Martinique is an easy island to get a lift and is quite common. You\'ll probably only be picked up by the French/Europeans that live there, as the other locals expect you to use the buses (but still hitch hike themselves).

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

Martinique is an overseas department, so the French *code de la route* applies in full and there is no offence of hitchhiking. Martinique is also the only French territory outside Europe with a classified motorway: the A1 between the airport at [Le Lamentin](Le_Lamentin "Le Lamentin"){.wikilink} and the Fort-de-France ring road, some seven kilometres and toll-free. On it [article R421-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006842313) bars pedestrians outright and article R421-1 extends that ban to the access ramps, while [article R421-7](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006842319) forbids a driver to stop on the carriageway or the hard shoulder except in absolute necessity --- so neither of you may lawfully be there. Everywhere else on the island the roads are ordinary *routes nationales* and *départementales* where hitchhiking is unregulated, and [article R412-36](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074228/LEGISCTA000006177125/) simply puts pedestrians outside built-up areas on the left-hand edge, facing the traffic. Given how short the A1 is, this costs you nothing in practice: start from the roundabouts either side of it rather than the motorway itself.

## Hitchhiking a boat {#hitchhiking_a_boat}

Le Marin is the main marina to look for boats to hitch onwards. It is one of the biggest in the Caribbean and you will find a lot of boat and a lot of boat hitchhikers. There is basically two Marina in Le Marin. The new one, close to the big parking and the he old one called Mango Bay. Both are good to find a boat.

To get a ride, use your imagination. You can put signs, ask directly at the dinghy dock, hang out in the bars.

The two bars called Kokoarum and l\'annexe are the two sailors bar. You will find many there to speak to to get a ride.

If you are planning on island hopping North, you can also try your luck at the dinghy dock in Saint Pierre. It is usually the last anchorage boats stay at before heading to Dominica or Guadalupe. Ask around and if you manage to find a ride, the Customs Office is located in the Information Center building right next door.

## Accomodation and sleep {#accomodation_and_sleep}

Since there is a lot of travelers and hitchhikers, some camps were created near Le Marin and built with what we found from the trash and includes tarps, fireplaces and everything you need to cook, sofa, mattress\... You can find them on maps.me. one is up the hill behind the simply market and the other one is in the mangrove in the direction of Saint-Anne. Please respect these places and do not make many noises for the neighborhood.

## Dumpster Diving {#dumpster_diving}

Martinique is a very nice place to dumpster dive. In Le Marin, go to Simply Market (dumpster diving friendly staff) or Leader Price (less friendly, be stealthy)
