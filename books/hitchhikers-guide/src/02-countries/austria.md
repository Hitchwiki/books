# Austria

**Austria** is a member state of the [European Union](European_Union "European Union"){.wikilink} as well as of the [Schengen Agreement](Schengen_Agreement "Schengen Agreement"){.wikilink}.

People in Austria speak German (in some parts locally also Slovene, Croatian and Hungarian) but if you are not a German speaker you won\'t be in big trouble since almost every Austrian speaks at least basic English.

## Hitchhiking

It is a good country for hitchhiking, with fabulous views of the surrounding Alps and small lakes. While hitchhiking by motorway you can easily "hop" from city to city - ramps are usually easy to get to and easy to hitch from. Good places are service stations, too. In Austria you can find [official hitchhiking spots](Official_Hitchhiking "official hitchhiking spots"){.wikilink} mostly in the form of [benches (\"Mitfahrbankerl\")](Hitchhiking_Bench "benches ("Mitfahrbankerl")"){.wikilink} in rural areas.

Austria is surrounded by [Germany](Germany "Germany"){.wikilink}, [Hungary](Hungary "Hungary"){.wikilink}, [Czech Republic](Czech_Republic "Czech Republic"){.wikilink}, [Slovakia](Slovakia "Slovakia"){.wikilink}, [Switzerland](Switzerland "Switzerland"){.wikilink}, [Slovenia](Slovenia "Slovenia"){.wikilink}, [Liechtenstein](Liechtenstein "Liechtenstein"){.wikilink} and [Italy](Italy "Italy"){.wikilink} - to any of these countries one can get easily by hitchhiking.

In Austria most motorway service stations serve both directions of the motorway and there is one situated in the junction of the A9 and A1, serving all four directions! ## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

Hitchhiking is not an offence in Austrian law; where you stand is. [§ 46(1) of the *Straßenverkehrsordnung 1960*](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10011336&Paragraf=46) reserves the *Autobahn* for motor vehicles built for more than 60 km/h and states that all other traffic, \"in particular pedestrian traffic\", is forbidden on it --- carriageway, *Pannenstreifen* and on-ramp alike. The same paragraph makes one narrow exception for going in on foot: at a border crossing or a toll station you may enter the Autobahn for business connected with the crossing. § 46(4)(e) allows vehicles to stop or park only at the places marked by signs, meaning the *Rastplätze* and *Raststationen* --- which is exactly where you ask drivers for the next ride. On *Bundesstraßen* and everything smaller hitchhiking is unregulated and legal, so thumb before the motorway sign.

## Border crossings {#border_crossings}

### [Germany](Germany "Germany"){.wikilink}

If you\'re coming from Germany and plan to come to / pass by Austria, There are two main points to cross the border: [Passau](Passau "Passau"){.wikilink} and [Salzburg](Salzburg "Salzburg"){.wikilink}

- Most of the traffic going through **Passau** is heading **East** to [Linz](Linz "Linz"){.wikilink}, [Vienna](Vienna "Vienna"){.wikilink}, [Budapest](Budapest "Budapest"){.wikilink}
- Most of the traffic going through **Salzburg** is heading **South** to [Trieste](Trieste "Trieste"){.wikilink}, [Italy](Italy "Italy"){.wikilink}, [Slovenia](Slovenia "Slovenia"){.wikilink}, [Balkans](Balkans "Balkans"){.wikilink}

### [Hungary](Hungary "Hungary"){.wikilink}

- [Hegyeshalom-Nickelsdorf border crossing](Hegyeshalom-Nickelsdorf_border_crossing "Hegyeshalom-Nickelsdorf border crossing"){.wikilink}

## Regions and Cities {#regions_and_cities}

Vienna is by far the country\'s largest city. Graz is second in size, with 291,007 inhabitants, followed by Linz (206,604), Salzburg (155,031), Innsbruck (131,989), and Klagenfurt (101,303). All other cities have fewer than 100,000 inhabitants.
