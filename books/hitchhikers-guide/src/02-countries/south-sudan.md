# South Sudan

**South Sudan** is a very young country in [Northern Africa](Northern_Africa "Northern Africa"){.wikilink} that split off [Sudan](Sudan "Sudan"){.wikilink} on July 8th, 2011. It is bordered by [Sudan](Sudan "Sudan"){.wikilink} to the north, the [Central African Republic](Central_African_Republic "Central African Republic"){.wikilink} to the west, the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo "Democratic Republic of the Congo"){.wikilink} to the south west, [Uganda](Uganda "Uganda"){.wikilink} to the south, [Kenya](Kenya "Kenya"){.wikilink} to the south east and [Ethiopia](Ethiopia "Ethiopia"){.wikilink} to the east.

## Danger

The independence was unbloody, but it\'s very possible that the old conflict with the North might flare up again.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in South Sudan against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on. If you can read the official text, please replace this paragraph with the provision it turns on and a link to it.
