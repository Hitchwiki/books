# Marshall Islands

The **Marshall Islands** are an archipelago nation in [Micronesia](Micronesia "Micronesia"){.wikilink}, [Oceania](Oceania "Oceania"){.wikilink}.

## Hitchhiking

Hitchhikernick - Visited Majuro in October 2023 for a week. Average waiting time was 10-15 minutes. Is possible to walk between islands of Majuro Atoll when the tide goes out. There is some designated picnic spots (one at far tip of Laura for 1\$ entry fee, don\'t think your supposed to sleep there for the night, but i did with no issues and the charge improved security). I camped out on several islands on their rugged perimeter (not many beaches and tide comes in) and in fields. Dogs seemed real chilled here compared to Pohnpei.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in the Marshall Islands against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on.
