# Greenland

**Greenland** is a huge, cold, and sparsely inhabited country.

There are a lot of ties with [Denmark](Denmark "Denmark"){.wikilink}, and many people speak Danish.

## Hitchhiking

There is no road or rail system on **Greenland**; so best is to get around by plane. Maybe you can hitch a plane there. There is also a ferry company that goes between the major towns in Greenland.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

Greenland has its own traffic act rather than [Denmark](Denmark "Denmark"){.wikilink}\'s --- the [Bekendtgørelse af færdselslov for Grønland](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2009/995), LBK no. 995 of 26 October 2009 --- and it says nothing whatever about hitchhiking. It says nothing about motorways either: the word *motorvej* does not occur in it once, because no two Greenlandic towns are joined by road and the network is a few dozen kilometres inside each settlement plus the run to its airport. The provision that applies to you is § 42, which requires a pedestrian on a road to keep as far to the side as possible and by preference to the left-hand side of the road. So a lift is legally unproblematic anywhere you are in a position to ask for one, and there is no road class, verge rule or stopping rule standing in the way. What limits hitchhiking in Greenland is not the traffic act but the geography: the long-distance connections are boats, planes and helicopters, and for those the law that matters is maritime rather than this one --- see [Hitchhiking a boat](Hitchhiking_a_boat "Hitchhiking a boat"){.wikilink}.
