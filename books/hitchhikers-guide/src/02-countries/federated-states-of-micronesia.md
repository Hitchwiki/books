# Federated States of Micronesia

The **Federated States of Micronesia** are an archipelago nation in [Micronesia](Micronesia "Micronesia"){.wikilink}, [Oceania](Oceania "Oceania"){.wikilink}.

## Hitchhiking

Hitchhikernick - I hitched around Pohnpei for 10 days October 2023. Waiting times were around 10-15 mins on average, went all over island on ring road and some back roads.

Be very careful around Kolonia at night as there are a lot of aggressive dogs and i had to fend off packs in over 5 occasions in the space of 500meters, quite a few all over island so be prepared with stick/rock whilst walking. Got bitten on first night when walking from airport, went to hospital and informed that most A&E cases were dog bite related.

Camping not the easiest as familys own most of the land and people everywhere, though is possible if you ask drivers for good quiet spots, be well prepared for rain as it rains so much that abandoned cars are covered in moss.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in Federated States of Micronesia against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on.
