## Hitchhiking

Hitchhiking in Puerto Rico itself from any place to any other place even inside of the towns is quite easy and people are very friendly. Mostly they are worried about your safety. I am hitchhiking here over 2 weeks every day and till now I haven\'t had one bad experience, so don\'t let the natives put you down. Its possible and it\'s easy.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

Puerto Rico\'s [*Ley de Vehículos y Tránsito* (Ley 22-2000)](https://bvirtualogp.pr.gov/ogp/Bvirtual/leyesreferencia/PDF/22-2000.pdf) does not make hitchhiking an offence, and its two relevant articles are unusually favourable to it. Article 9.02(f) (9 LPRA § 5252) requires you to use the pavement where there is one and, where there is not, to walk *por el borde o paseo izquierdo de la vía pública, de frente al tránsito* --- the left-hand edge or shoulder, facing the traffic --- and not to step off it suddenly in front of a car. Article 22.05(a) (9 LPRA § 5655) bars pedestrians from the toll expressways, the *autopistas de peaje*, except in the service, parking and recreation areas designated by the Autoridad, so the *área de servicio* is not merely tolerated there but written into the exception itself. Nothing in the Act forbids asking a driver for a lift or giving one, so on the ordinary carreteras the left shoulder facing traffic is both the lawful and the sensible place to stand.

## Hitchhiking out of [San Juan](San_Juan "San Juan"){.wikilink} {#hitchhiking_out_of_san_juan}

### West towards [Arecibo](Arecibo "Arecibo"){.wikilink}, [Aguadilla](Aguadilla "Aguadilla"){.wikilink}, [Isabela](Isabela "Isabela"){.wikilink}, [Mayaguez](Mayaguez "Mayaguez"){.wikilink} Route 2 {#west_towards_arecibo_aguadilla_isabela_mayaguez_route_2}

From the end station of the San Juan\'s Metro \"Bayamon\". You just go out of the metro and turn to your right. Go down the stairs leading to the junction. You cross the street and then you will directly see the sign of [Arecibo](Arecibo "Arecibo"){.wikilink}, at that moment you actually hit the older route 2, which takes almost all the people and there is wide lane for stopping and the cars are going really slow.

I was waiting at this point around 5 minutes. Here you can see the videos: [1](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nPkAPCjo2-c) [2](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ztAI_SD9rI8)

## Hitchhiking in Vieques {#hitchhiking_in_vieques}

Very easy to hitchhike around this small island off the East Coast, especially if you are going between Isabel and Esperanza. I waited around 5 minutes for each ride. I felt safe as a solo female traveler even with male drivers. Several police officers passed my outstretched thumb and did not stop.
