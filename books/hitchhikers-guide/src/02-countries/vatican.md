# Vatican

The **State of the Vatican City** (official sovereign territory of the **Holy See**) is the smallest independent state in the world, located within the city of [Rome](Rome "Rome"){.wikilink} in [Italy](Italy "Italy"){.wikilink}.

### Number plate {#number_plate}

There are less than 500 vehicles registered in Vatican. The number plate is white with black letters beginning with *SCV* or *CV*. It might be a hitchhikers challenge to hitchhike with a car registered in Vatican.

### Sleeping

(Wild)camping is not possible in Vatican City - every place where you can go as a tourist is full of people, the other places are watched by the swiss guard. There are no hostels or hotels on the Vatican territory.

## Hitchhiking

It is very hard to hitch a ride on the vatican territory - the parkings and the streets leading to it are blocked by the swiss guard. It is so small that there is no need to hitchhike.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in the Vatican against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on.
