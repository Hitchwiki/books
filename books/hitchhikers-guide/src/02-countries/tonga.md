# Tonga

**Tonga** is a small archipelago state in [Polynesia](Polynesia "Polynesia"){.wikilink}, [Oceania](Oceania "Oceania"){.wikilink}.

## Hitchhiking

Hitchhikernick - Visited in June 2023 for 3 weeks. Average waiting time around 20 mins. Camped out in remote areas, left no trace and all worked out. The trash on the outskirts of main city and is ridiculous and stinks so you don\'t want to camp/saty there. Vavau is much nicer and wilder with the free roaming pigs.

Supermarkets are available and the Chinese ones (quite a few) have all you need at affordable prices.

Catching the 24hr ferry from Tongatapo to Vavau was a real pleasant experience as there was literally no other tourists. Was much cheaper than flying and may take time to hitch a boat, so didn\'t bother as ferry price was reasonable.

Be prepared for some rain showers.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in Tonga against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on.
