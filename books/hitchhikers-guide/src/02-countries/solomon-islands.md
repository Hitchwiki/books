[Hitchhikernick](User:Hitchhikernick "Hitchhikernick"){.wikilink} visited Guadalcanal in September 2023 for a couple of days. Hitched to Honiara from airport and waited 10 mins max, took 2 lifts and received money off both drivers. Then hitched to shipwreck 20km to the west and got a few rides with ease and last lift offered me to stay at theirs for the night in Honiara. After that walked around town with no issues and hitched to Tenaru waterfalls and camped in parangiju lodge with beautiful views.

Overall people seemed really friendly and kind in Honiara/Guadalcanal. Average waiting time 10 minutes.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in the Solomon Islands against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on. If you can read the official text, please replace this paragraph with the provision it turns on and a link to it.
