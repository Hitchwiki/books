# Liechtenstein

\

**Liechtenstein** is a tiny country in [Western Europe](Western_Europe "Western Europe"){.wikilink}, surrounded by [Austria](Austria "Austria"){.wikilink} and [Switzerland](Switzerland "Switzerland"){.wikilink}. It is a member state of the [Schengen Agreement](Schengen_Agreement "Schengen Agreement"){.wikilink}. People speak [German](Germany "German"){.wikilink}.

## Hitchhiking

There is no motorway in Liechtenstein, but the [A13](A13_(Switzerland) "A13"){.wikilink} in Switzerland is within walking distance from any point in the country. It goes down to [Bellinzona](Bellinzona "Bellinzona"){.wikilink} and merges into the [A2](A2_(Switzerland) "A2"){.wikilink} towards [Lugano](Lugano "Lugano"){.wikilink} and [Italy](Italy "Italy"){.wikilink}.

Liechtenstein was the destination of the [Abgefahren Hitchhiking Race 2009](Abgefahren_Hitchhiking_Race_2009 "Abgefahren Hitchhiking Race 2009"){.wikilink}.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

Liechtenstein has its own *Strassenverkehrsgesetz*, closely modelled on [Switzerland](Switzerland "Switzerland"){.wikilink}\'s but not identical to it, and hitchhiking is not an offence under it. The difference that matters to you is that there is no motorway in the country and no motorway chapter in the law --- the word *Autobahn* does not occur anywhere in the [SVG of 30 June 1978](https://www.gesetze.li/konso/1978.018), so unlike in either neighbour there is no class of road you are shut out of. What governs you is article 45: pedestrians must use the pavements, and where there are none they keep to the edge of the road, walking in single file where particular dangers require it. The same article tells you to keep to the **left** edge unless special circumstances prevent it, *namentlich ausserorts in der Nacht* --- especially outside built-up areas at night. The real constraint here is geography rather than law: the country is some 25 km end to end, and the motorway you actually want is the Swiss A13 or the Austrian A14, where those countries\' rules take over.

## Hitchhiking out {#hitchhiking_out}

### South towards [Bellinzona](Bellinzona "Bellinzona"){.wikilink}, [Lugano](Lugano "Lugano"){.wikilink}, [Italy](Italy "Italy"){.wikilink} {#south_towards_bellinzona_lugano_italy}

#### Option 1 {#option_1}

If you want to hitch out of **Liechtenstein**, try to go to to the service station *Rheintal* between the ramps *Buchs* and *Sevelen*. It doesn\'t matter at which ramp you are, it\'s possible to walk along the bicycle path along the river to get directly to the service station. The on-ramp Sevelen is also pretty good to hitchhike a short ride to the petrol station.

### Eastbound/Northeast [Bregenz](Bregenz "Bregenz"){.wikilink}, [Ulm](Ulm "Ulm"){.wikilink}, [München](München "München"){.wikilink} {#eastboundnortheast_bregenz_ulm_münchen}

#### Option 1 {#option_1_1}

Take bus number 11 from Vaduz to Feldkirch (rarely controlled, leaves twice an hour). In Feldkirch get off at Rappenweg (Tisis) and walk back to the Agip service area (everyone who goes to Austria will take petrol here and not on the Liechtenstein side - it\'s cheaper here). Alternatively get off at the border (custom control) - cars have to get slow there, so perfect to catch a ride to Bregenz and Germany.
