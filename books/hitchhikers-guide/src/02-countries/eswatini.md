# Eswatini

The **Kingdom of eSwatini** (formerly known as the **Kingdom of Swaziland**) is a small, landlocked monarchy in [Southern Africa](Southern_Africa "Southern Africa"){.wikilink}, which borders both [South Africa](South_Africa "South Africa"){.wikilink} and [Mozambique](Mozambique "Mozambique"){.wikilink}. The two official and most widely spoken languages are siSwati and English.

## Hitchhiking

Overall, the country is very safe, people are friendly, and the hitching is great.

## Legality of Hitchhiking {#legality_of_hitchhiking}

*The law here has not been checked.* Nobody has yet read the road traffic rules in force in Eswatini against the official text, so this section cannot tell you what they say, and nothing elsewhere on this page should be read as a statement of the law. What holds almost everywhere is that hitchhiking is not an offence in itself and that what the traffic code regulates is where a pedestrian may stand --- off the carriageway, and off motorway-class roads and their slip roads altogether --- but whether that is true here is simply unknown. Ask locally before standing on a major road, and treat the rest of this article as what hitchhikers report rather than as a rule you can rely on. If you can read the official text, please replace this paragraph with the provision it turns on and a link to it.

## siSwati

siSwati is a Bantu language that is very similar to Zulu, and is the most widely spoken language in eSwatini. In general, everyone speaks siSwati and maybe a bit of English; in general, younger people speak more English than the old.
