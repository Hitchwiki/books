# Taste Before Waste Mini-market

> On Wednesdays from 18.30 till 20.30 we create a free mini-market on the street, for people to come and pick up food in return for the promise of trying to cut back on their food waste at home. We are very happy that the local grocery-shops are willing to participate in "Taste Before You Waste" because with their help and that of the volunteers less food ends up in the trash and more food is tasted before it is wasted!
