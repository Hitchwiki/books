# Publish / print notes

Local notes for putting these books in stores, archives, and paper. Not legal advice. License facts live in each `books/<slug>/LICENSE` and in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

ShareAlike allows commercial use. NonCommercial does not. Do not sell NC-SA books. Free digital (catalog, Internet Archive, itch.io at $0) is fine for both licenses. Print copies you pay for and give away are fine for NC-SA; a store listing that takes money is not.

Canonical free EPUB/PDF stays on the catalog site. Paid channels are convenience (paper, Kindle, Apple Books), not a replacement for that download.

## What CC-BY-SA requires on every edition

1. **Attribution** — community authors (Hitchwiki / Trustroots Wiki / Nomadwiki contributors), link to the source wiki, link to [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Compiler/editor named separately.
2. **ShareAlike** — the edition stays CC-BY-SA-4.0. No “all rights reserved” copyright page.
3. **No extra restrictions** — DRM off. No exclusivity programs (KDP Select / Kindle Unlimited, or any store’s “digital exclusive”). Anyone else may also publish this material, and the EPUB is already free.

Keep the free EPUB/PDF on the catalog. Charging for a print copy or a store convenience file is allowed; locking redistribution is not.

Author field = the community (`Hitchwiki contributors`, etc.). Role for a named person = compiler/editor, not sole author. Description says it is a compiled wiki edition under CC-BY-SA, with a free EPUB/PDF at the catalog. Front matter: title, compiler, community authors, license notice with URL, “adaptation of the wiki as of [build date]”. Hitchhikers Guide keeps the Douglas Adams disclaimer.

Imprint: `books.hitchwiki.org` (or Hitchwiki / Trustroots entity), not a personal “I wrote this” brand. Royalties to the wikis/foundations, not private profit — CC does not require revenue sharing; this catalog should.

Buy ISBNs so the publisher is books.hitchwiki.org, or accept a platform’s free ISBN (imprint will show as Independently Published / Lulu / D2D).

## Print (paperback people can order)

Usual retail combo: **KDP Amazon-only** (no Expanded Distribution) **+ IngramSpark** (or D2D Print) for shops and libraries. Do not send the same ISBN to Amazon through both KDP Expanded Distribution and Ingram.

| Channel | Ease | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Lulu](https://www.lulu.com/) | easiest POD | Own storefront, author copies, optional Ingram-backed retail. Free to start. Good first non-Amazon print. |
| [Amazon KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/) Print | easy | Amazon paperback. Highest Amazon royalties. See KDP notes below. |
| [IngramSpark](https://www.ingramspark.com/) | a bit more setup | Bookstores, libraries, Bookshop.org, many countries. Needs ISBN, proof, wholesale discount. The “real shops” path. |
| [Draft2Digital Print](https://www.draft2digital.com/) | easiest wide | One upload, they feed Ingram. Less control than Spark; fine if you do not want Spark’s dashboard. |
| [Barnes & Noble Press](https://press.barnesandnoble.com/) | easy, US | Nook + B&N print. Skip if D2D already covers B&N. |
| Mixam / local printer | box of copies | Hitch / Trustroots gatherings. No store listing. Pay, collect, give or sell BY-SA copies. |

Lulu Direct can print from a Hitchwiki webshop later. Blurb is for photo-heavy books; skip for these titles.

Shared print spec (KDP, Lulu, Ingram, D2D):

- Trim **6 × 9 in** (152 × 229 mm)
- Black ink, cream paper, matte cover
- Print-ready interior PDF + wrap cover (front + spine + back). Spine width follows page count.
- Order a proof before publishing

## Amazon KDP

Paperback is the better Amazon product. People pay for paper. Kindle may be price-matched to $0 once they find the free download.

KDP may refuse **copyrighted text that is already free on the web** unless the submitter is the copyright owner. Wiki contributors still own the text; this repo is a licensee. That is the main rejection risk.

Mitigations: honest community author field and description; ship the **book edition**, not a dump (structure, locked front matter, no pin lists, original notes, covers, typesetting). If they ask for rights proof: each book’s `LICENSE`, the wiki license page, the CC deed, and a short statement that this is a CC-BY-SA compilation.

If they still bounce the Kindle file, the paperback often still works. If they bounce both, use Lulu / Ingram / D2D instead.

Kindle: upload EPUB, **DRM off**, **KDP Select off**. Price as convenience; $0 is fine if they match the free file.

If cover or interior images were produced by an AI tool, tick KDP’s **AI-generated** box. AI-assisted editing does not need that box.

## Build gap

`make all` produces a reading PDF/EPUB (screen margins), not a print interior. Print still needs:

- Fixed 6 × 9 trim, larger inner/gutter margin
- Wrap cover sized to trim + bleed + spine
- Page numbers / TOC that work on paper

No print-POD make target yet.

## Do not

- Sell NC-SA books
- Mix BY-SA and NC-SA
- Claim exclusive copyright, turn DRM on, or enroll in KDP Select (or any store exclusive)
- Hide that the same text is free on the wiki and the catalog
- Use CouchSurfing / Couchers branding, or any chapter that is still “ask first”

## Not worth it for v1

BookBaby (fees), StreetLib (EU extra), MagCloud, Feedbooks, Project Gutenberg / Standard Ebooks (public domain only), KDP Select, ACX/audiobook until there is a human narration plan.
